Introduction & Overview

Where Science Comes Alive

The Mind Museum is the Philippines' first world-class science museum, opened in 2012 in Bonifacio Global City, Taguig. It houses over 250 interactive exhibits across five thematic galleries — the Atom Gallery, Life Gallery, Earth Gallery, Universe Gallery, and Technology Gallery.

The museum is designed to make science accessible and exciting for all ages — from young children experiencing their first science experiments to adults exploring the frontiers of physics, biology, and astronomy. The exhibits are hands-on, beautifully designed, and genuinely educational.

The Mind Museum also features a 3D Dome Theater, a Science-in-the-Park outdoor area, and regular special exhibitions and science events. It is consistently rated as one of the best museums in Southeast Asia and the top family attraction in Metro Manila.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

Getting to The Mind Museum

The Mind Museum is in Bonifacio Global City, Taguig — accessible by BGC Bus, taxi, or Grab from most parts of Metro Manila.

01

BGC Bus from Ayala

Take the BGC Bus from Ayala Avenue in Makati to BGC. The bus stops near Bonifacio High Street — The Mind Museum is a short walk from the High Street area.

BGC Bus: ~₱12–₱15  |  ~5 min walk from High Street
02

Grab or Taxi

Book a Grab directly to The Mind Museum at J.Y. Campos Park, BGC. The museum has a dedicated drop-off area.

Grab/Taxi: ~₱150–₱300 from Makati  |  J.Y. Campos Park, BGC
03

Book Tickets Online

Book tickets online in advance — The Mind Museum sells out on weekends and school holidays. Online booking also allows you to choose your entry time slot.

Tickets: ~₱775 adults, ₱625 children  |  Book online
04

Plan Your Visit

Allow at least 3₱4 hours for a thorough visit. Start with the gallery that interests you most — the Universe Gallery and Life Gallery are the most popular. The 3D Dome Theater requires a separate ticket.

🚌 Recommended: 3₱4 hrs  |  3D Dome: separate ticket

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

📌

The Five Galleries

  • Atom Gallery: The building blocks of matter — from subatomic particles to the periodic table. Interactive exhibits on chemistry and physics.
  • Life Gallery: The science of living things — DNA, evolution, ecosystems, and the human body. The most popular gallery for families.
  • Earth Gallery: Philippine geology, volcanoes, earthquakes, and the natural history of the archipelago.
  • Universe Gallery: Astronomy, cosmology, and the scale of the universe — the most visually spectacular gallery.
📌

3D Dome Theater

  • Book in advance: The 3D Dome Theater shows are popular and sell out quickly. Book your show time when purchasing museum tickets.
  • Show schedule: Multiple shows run throughout the day — astronomy, nature, and science documentaries projected on a full-dome screen.
  • Best for: The Universe and Earth shows are the most impressive — the full-dome format creates an immersive experience unlike any conventional cinema.
  • Separate ticket: The Dome Theater requires a separate ticket in addition to the museum admission.
📌

Practical Tips

  • Book online in advance — especially on weekends and school holidays. The museum has limited capacity and sells out regularly.
  • Arrive early: The museum opens at 9 AM on weekends. Arriving early means smaller crowds and more time with the interactive exhibits.
  • Combine with BGC: After the museum, explore Bonifacio High Street, the street art, and the Mercato Centrale (FridaySunday evenings).
  • Science-in-the-Park: The outdoor science park adjacent to the museum is free to explore — a good option for children who need a break from the indoor galleries.
Mini Itinerary

A BGC Science & Culture Day

Combine The Mind Museum with Bonifacio High Street and the Mercato Centrale for the perfect BGC day.

Morning
9:00 AM

The Mind Museum

Arrive at The Mind Museum at opening time. Start with the Universe Gallery — the most visually spectacular — then work through the Life, Earth, Atom, and Technology galleries.

11:30 AM

3D Dome Theater

Catch a 3D Dome Theater show — book your time slot in advance. The astronomy shows are the most impressive in the full-dome format.

Afternoon & Evening
1:00 PM

Lunch on Bonifacio High Street

Walk to Bonifacio High Street for lunch — dozens of restaurants ranging from casual to fine dining in the open-air lifestyle strip.

3:00 PM

BGC Street Art Walk

Explore the BGC street art — murals by local and international artists cover building facades throughout the district. Pick up a street art map from the BGC Arts Center.

7:00 PM

Mercato Centrale

End the day at Mercato Centrale (FridaySunday) — the best night market in Metro Manila, with dozens of food stalls and a vibrant evening atmosphere.
