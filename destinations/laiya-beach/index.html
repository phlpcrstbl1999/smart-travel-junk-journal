Introduction

Manila's Nearest White Sand Paradise

Laiya Beach in San Juan, Batangas is the most accessible white sand beach from Metro Manila — just 3 hours by car via SLEX and the Batangas highway. Unlike the grey volcanic sand of Zambales or the black sand of some Batangas beaches, Laiya offers the fine white sand and clear turquoise water that most Manila residents associate with a proper beach holiday.

The Laiya coastline stretches for several kilometers along the Tayabas Bay side of the Batangas peninsula, facing the Sibuyan Sea. The water is calm, clear, and warm year-round — ideal for swimming, snorkeling, and kayaking. The offshore coral reefs are among the most accessible in Batangas, with vibrant marine life visible even from the surface. Several dive shops along the beach offer equipment rental and guided dives for more serious underwater exploration.

The beach is lined with a range of beach resorts — from budget-friendly day-use facilities to mid-range overnight resorts with cottages, pools, and restaurants. The area is particularly popular for company outings, family reunions, and barkada (friend group) trips — the combination of easy access from Manila, white sand, clear water, and affordable resorts makes it the default beach destination for millions of Metro Manila residents.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Laiya Beach

Laiya Beach is in San Juan, Batangas — about 160 km southeast of Manila. The drive takes about 3 hours via SLEX and the Batangas highway.

01

Drive from Manila via SLEX

Drive via SLEX — Sto. Tomas — Batangas City — San Juan — Laiya. The drive takes about 3 hours from Manila depending on traffic. Having your own vehicle is the most convenient option — it gives you flexibility to choose your resort and explore the coastline.

Manila to Laiya: ~3 hours  |  Parking at all resorts
02

Bus from Manila to San Juan

Take a DLTB or Jam Liner bus from Buendia (Pasay) to San Juan, Batangas. From the San Juan terminal, take a tricycle to your resort in Laiya (about 20₱30 minutes). Some resorts offer shuttle services from the terminal.

Manila to San Juan: ~3₱3.5 hours  |  ~₱200–₱250
03

Choose Your Resort

Laiya has dozens of resorts ranging from budget day-use facilities to mid-range overnight resorts. Book in advance for weekends and holidays — popular resorts fill up quickly. Most resorts charge an entrance fee for day use and offer cottage rentals for overnight stays.

Day use: ~₱200–₱500  |  Overnight cottage: ~₱1,500₱5,000
04

Snorkeling & Water Activities

Rent snorkeling gear from your resort or from the beach vendors to explore the offshore coral reefs. Kayaks, paddleboards, and banana boats are available at most resorts. For scuba diving, several dive shops along the beach offer equipment rental and guided dives.

Snorkel gear: ~₱100–₱200  |  Kayak: ~₱200–₱300/hour

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

📌

Best Time to Visit

  • November — May (Dry Season): The best time for Laiya — calm seas, clear water, and sunny skies. The beach is at its most beautiful from December to April.
  • Avoid typhoon season: JuneOctober can bring rough seas and heavy rain. Some resorts close or limit activities during typhoon season. Check weather forecasts before going.
  • Weekdays: Laiya is extremely popular on weekends — weekday visits are far less crowded, the water is cleaner, and resorts are more relaxed. If you can go on a weekday, do it.
  • Book in advance: Popular resorts fill up weeks in advance for peak weekends (Christmas, New Year, Holy Week, summer). Book early to secure your preferred resort and cottage.
📌

Water Activities

  • Snorkeling: The coral reefs off Laiya are accessible from the beach — wade out 50₱100 meters and the reef begins. Best snorkeling is in the morning before the afternoon boat traffic stirs up the sand.
  • Scuba diving: Several dive sites around San Juan offer wall dives, coral gardens, and occasional pelagic sightings. Dive shops along the beach offer PADI courses and guided dives.
  • Island hopping: Boat tours to nearby islands and snorkeling spots are available from the beach. Ask your resort to arrange a boat for a half-day island hopping trip.
  • Kayaking: Kayaks are available at most resorts — paddle along the coastline for a different perspective of the beach and the surrounding hills.
📌

Food & Practical Tips

  • Fresh seafood: The restaurants at Laiya resorts serve excellent fresh seafood — grilled fish, shrimp, squid, and crab caught locally. The seafood is fresher and cheaper than in Manila.
  • Bring cash: Most resorts and beach vendors are cash-only. There are ATMs in San Juan town but not on the beach itself. Bring enough cash for your entire stay.
  • Sunscreen: The Batangas sun is intense — apply waterproof sunscreen generously and reapply after swimming. A rash guard is recommended for extended time in the water.
  • Respect the reef: Do not stand on or touch the coral. Do not take shells, coral, or marine life from the water. The reefs are fragile and take decades to recover from damage.
Mini Itinerary

How to Spend a Weekend at Laiya Beach

Two days at Laiya — snorkeling, island hopping, fresh seafood, and sunsets over Tayabas Bay — is the perfect Manila weekend escape.

Day 1
5:00 AM

Early Departure from Manila

Leave Manila before 6 AM to beat the SLEX traffic. The 3-hour drive is much more pleasant in the early morning. Arrive at your resort by 8₱9 AM — early enough to catch the best morning snorkeling conditions.

9:00 AM

Morning Snorkeling

The water is clearest in the morning before the afternoon boat traffic. Rent snorkel gear and explore the offshore coral reefs — the marine life is most active in the morning hours.

12:00 PM

Fresh Seafood Lunch

Have lunch at your resort restaurant — grilled fish, shrimp, and squid fresh from the Tayabas Bay. Order the catch of the day for the freshest option.

3:00 PM

Kayaking & Beach Time

Rent a kayak for an afternoon paddle along the coastline, then relax on the beach as the afternoon light turns golden. The late afternoon is the best time for beach photography.

Day 2
7:00 AM

Sunrise & Early Swim

Wake up for the sunrise over Tayabas Bay — the morning light on the water is spectacular. Take an early swim before the beach gets busy.

9:00 AM

Island Hopping Boat Tour

Arrange a half-day island hopping boat tour through your resort — visit nearby snorkeling spots, small islands, and hidden coves along the San Juan coastline.

1:00 PM

Check Out & Drive Home

Check out after lunch and begin the drive back to Manila. Stop at Taal town on the way back to visit the Taal Basilica — a perfect heritage complement to the beach weekend.
