Introduction

Tarlac's Countryside Escape

Located in Tarlac province, Sumino Farm is a popular agri-tourism destination that offers visitors a refreshing escape from the city into the peaceful rhythms of farm life. The farm is set against the backdrop of the Tarlac countryside — rolling fields, fresh air, and the simple beauty of agricultural life in Central Luzon.

The farm offers a range of activities for visitors of all ages — vegetable picking, farm animal feeding, nature walks, and farm-to-table dining featuring fresh produce grown on the property. The farm is particularly popular with families and school groups who want to give children a hands-on experience of where food comes from and how it is grown.

Sumino Farm is also a popular destination for photography — the scenic countryside setting, the colorful vegetable gardens, and the farm animals create a visually rich environment that is a welcome contrast to the urban landscapes of Metro Manila. The farm is best visited as the relaxing afternoon finale to a Tarlac day tour that begins with the more solemn Capas National Shrine and the spiritual Monasterio de Tarlac.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Sumino Farm

Sumino Farm is located in Tarlac province — about 100₱120 km north of Manila. It's accessible via the NLEX and SCTEX expressways.

01

Bus from Manila to Tarlac City

Victory Liner, Genesis Transport, and other bus lines operate services from Cubao or Pasay to Tarlac City. Travel time is about 2₱2.5 hours via NLEX and SCTEX.

Travel time: ~2₱2.5 hours  |  ~₱150–₱220 one-way
02

Tarlac City to Sumino Farm

From Tarlac City, hire a tricycle or van to Sumino Farm. The farm is accessible by road and the journey takes about 20₱40 minutes depending on the exact location. Ask locals or your accommodation host for the most current directions.

Tricycle/van: ~20₱40 min  |  ~₱80–₱150
03

Drive from Manila

Driving from Manila via NLEXSCTEX takes about 1.5₱2 hours. Having your own vehicle is the most convenient way to visit Sumino Farm and combine it with Capas National Shrine and Monasterio de Tarlac in one day.

Drive: ~1.5₱2 hours  |  Parking available at the farm
04

Book in Advance

It is recommended to book your visit in advance — especially for groups and school trips. Contact the farm directly to confirm availability, entrance fees, and any special activities or packages available during your visit.

Book in advance for groups  |  Entrance fee: ~₱100–₱200

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Visit

📌

Best Time to Visit

  • November — May (Dry Season): Best time — dry trails, comfortable temperatures, and the most pleasant farm conditions for outdoor activities.
  • Morning visits (8:00₱11:00 AM): The farm is coolest and most active in the morning. Animals are fed and vegetables are freshest early in the day.
  • Weekdays: Fewer visitors and a more relaxed, personal atmosphere. School groups typically visit on weekdays — check the schedule if you prefer a quieter visit.
  • Harvest season: Check with the farm for the best times to visit for specific vegetable harvests — the experience of picking fresh produce is one of the highlights.
📌

Activities

  • Vegetable picking: Pick fresh vegetables directly from the garden — a hands-on experience that is especially meaningful for children who have never seen where food comes from.
  • Farm animal feeding: Feed and interact with farm animals — chickens, goats, pigs, and other animals depending on the season.
  • Nature walks: Walk the farm trails and enjoy the countryside scenery — rolling fields, fresh air, and the peaceful sounds of rural Tarlac.
  • Farm-to-table dining: Enjoy meals prepared with fresh produce grown on the farm — a genuine farm-to-table experience.
🎒

What to Pack

  • Comfortable walking shoes — the farm involves walking on uneven terrain and grass.
  • Sun protection — hat, sunscreen, and sunglasses for the outdoor farm activities.
  • Light, casual clothing — farm-appropriate attire. Leave formal wear at home.
  • Camera — the farm setting, the animals, and the countryside views are very photogenic.
  • Cash — for entrance fees, food, and any produce you want to take home.
Visitor Guide

How to Experience Sumino Farm

A complete guide to the farm and the perfect Tarlac day tour using Sumino as your relaxing finale.

At the Farm
Step 1

Arrival & Farm Tour

Arrive at the farm and take an orientation tour of the property. Learn about the crops being grown, the farm animals, and the sustainable farming practices used. The farm staff are knowledgeable and enthusiastic about sharing their work.

Step 2

Vegetable Picking

Head to the vegetable gardens for the picking experience. Choose from whatever is in season — tomatoes, eggplant, kangkong, pechay, and other fresh vegetables. The experience of picking your own food is surprisingly satisfying and educational.

Step 3

Farm Animal Feeding

Visit the farm animals and participate in feeding time. Children especially love this activity — the interaction with goats, chickens, and other animals creates memorable moments and a genuine connection with farm life.

Step 4

Farm-to-Table Lunch or Merienda

Enjoy a meal or snack prepared with fresh farm produce. The food is simple, fresh, and delicious — a genuine farm-to-table experience that tastes all the better for knowing exactly where the ingredients came from.

Full Tarlac Day
Morning

Capas National Shrine

Start with the Death March memorial — museum, memorial wall, and obelisk. A solemn and historically significant start to the day.

Midday

Monasterio de Tarlac

Chapel visit, Stations of the Cross, Risen Christ statue, and panoramic views. A peaceful and spiritually uplifting midday experience.

Afternoon

Sumino Farm

End your Tarlac day at the farm — vegetable picking, animal feeding, nature walk, and farm-to-table dining. The perfect relaxing and joyful close to a day of history and spirituality.
