Introduction & History

A Church Swallowed by the Earth

The Cagsawa Ruins in Daraga, Albay, are the remains of the Cagsawa Church — a Franciscan church originally built in 1724. On February 1, 1814, Mayon Volcano erupted with devastating force, burying the town of Cagsawa under lava flows and volcanic debris. Hundreds of townspeople who had taken refuge inside the church perished. Only the bell tower survived above the surface.

Today, the partially buried ruins — with the iconic bell tower rising from the earth against the backdrop of Mayon's perfect cone — are one of the most photographed landmarks in the Philippines. The juxtaposition of colonial stonework, volcanic destruction, and the ever-present volcano creates an image that is both beautiful and deeply sobering.

The site is now a National Cultural Treasure and a popular park where visitors can walk among the ruins, explore the small museum, and enjoy what is arguably the best ground-level view of Mayon Volcano in Albay.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Cagsawa Ruins

The Cagsawa Ruins are located in Daraga, Albay, about 10 km from Legazpi City — the main gateway to the Mayon Volcano area.

01

Fly to Legazpi City

Fly from Manila to Legazpi Airport in Albay. Philippine Airlines and Cebu Pacific operate this route. The flight takes about 1 hour. From the airport, take a tricycle or taxi to Legazpi City proper (~15 minutes).

Flight: ~1 hr  |  ~₱1,500₱3,500 one-way
02

Alternatively: Bus from Manila

Several bus lines operate overnight trips from Manila to Legazpi City. The journey takes about 10₱12 hours. Philtranco and DLTB Co. are the main operators. An affordable option if you prefer not to fly.

Bus: ~10₱12 hrs  |  ~₱700–₱1,100 one-way
03

Jeepney or Tricycle to Cagsawa

From Legazpi City, take a jeepney bound for Daraga and ask to be dropped at the Cagsawa Ruins (~20 minutes). Alternatively, hire a tricycle for a direct trip. The ruins are well-known — any driver will know the way.

Jeepney: ~₱15–₱20  |  Tricycle: ~₱80–₱120
04

Entrance & Park

The Cagsawa Ruins Park has a small entrance fee. Inside, you can walk freely among the ruins, visit the small on-site museum, and enjoy the open grounds with Mayon Volcano as a backdrop. Souvenir stalls and food vendors are available outside the park.

Entrance: ~₱30–₱50  |  — Allow 1₱2 hours

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

🌞

Best Time to Visit

  • Early morning (6:00₱8:00 AM): The best light for photography — golden hour illuminates the ruins and Mayon's cone is often clear before clouds build up.
  • November — April (Dry Season): Best chance of a clear Mayon view. The volcano is frequently cloud-covered during rainy season.
  • Check Mayon's alert level before visiting. PHIVOLCS issues alert levels 1₱5 — at level 3 or above, access to the area may be restricted.
  • Avoid midday: The open grounds get very hot. Visit early morning or late afternoon for comfort and better light.
📷

Photography Tips

  • The classic shot: Position yourself so the bell tower frames Mayon's cone in the background. Early morning light from the east is ideal.
  • Wide-angle lens captures both the ruins and the full volcano in a single frame.
  • Sunrise visits offer dramatic light and mist around the volcano's base — arrive before 6 AM for the best conditions.
  • Drone flying requires a permit — check with the park administration before flying.
🎒

What to Pack & Know

  • Sunscreen and hat — the park grounds are open and exposed. The Albay sun is intense.
  • Cash — for entrance fees, food vendors, and souvenir stalls outside the park.
  • Combine with Daraga Church — just 2 km away, it's another stunning Mayon viewpoint and a beautiful Baroque church worth visiting on the same trip.
  • Try Bicol Express — the spicy pork and coconut milk dish is Albay's most famous food. Eat it at a local restaurant in Legazpi after your visit.
Mini Itinerary

How to Spend a Day in Albay

Combine the Cagsawa Ruins with Daraga Church and the Mayon Skyline View Deck for a full day of Albay's most iconic Mayon Volcano experiences.

Morning
5:45 AM

Cagsawa Ruins at Sunrise

Arrive at the Cagsawa Ruins before sunrise. As the sky lightens, Mayon's cone emerges above the ruins — first as a dark silhouette, then bathed in golden light. This is the most photographed moment at Cagsawa and worth the early wake-up.

7:30 AM

Explore the Ruins & Museum

Walk among the partially buried stonework of the old church, read the historical markers, and visit the small on-site museum which displays artifacts recovered from the ruins and documents the 1814 eruption and its impact on the community.

9:00 AM

Daraga Church

Take a tricycle to Daraga Church (Our Lady of the Gate Parish) — a stunning Baroque church on a hill with one of the best elevated views of Mayon Volcano. The church facade and the volcano behind it make for extraordinary photographs.

Afternoon
11:00 AM

Mayon Skyline View Deck

Head to the Mayon Skyline View Deck in Legazpi City for the closest accessible view of Mayon Volcano. On a clear day, the cone fills the entire sky above you — an overwhelming and humbling sight.

1:00 PM

Lunch: Bicol Express & Laing

Eat lunch at a local restaurant in Legazpi City. Order Bicol Express (spicy pork in coconut milk), laing (taro leaves in coconut cream), and pinangat — the definitive Bicolano dishes that you can only truly appreciate in Albay.

3:00 PM

Legazpi Boulevard Sunset

End the day at Legazpi Boulevard along the waterfront. Watch the sunset with Mayon Volcano glowing in the background — the volcano turns shades of orange and pink as the light fades. A perfect close to a full day in Albay.
