Introduction & History

Faith at the Cattle Capital

The Masbate Cathedral (Saint Anthony of Padua Parish) is the Diocese of Masbate's mother church and the most prominent religious landmark in Masbate City. Dedicated to Saint Anthony of Padua, the cathedral serves as the spiritual center of the province and the seat of the Bishop of Masbate.

The cathedral's architecture reflects the Spanish colonial heritage of the Philippines, with a distinctive facade, bell tower, and interior that have been carefully maintained and restored over the centuries. The church plaza is a central gathering point for the community, especially during religious festivals and the feast of Saint Anthony of Padua on June 13.

Masbate City itself is known as the "Cattle Capital of the Philippines" — the province is the country's largest cattle-producing region, and the annual Rodeo Masbateo festival celebrates this heritage with Philippine-style rodeo competitions, cultural shows, and festivities centered around the cathedral plaza.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Masbate Cathedral

Masbate Cathedral is located in the heart of Masbate City — the provincial capital of Masbate, accessible by air from Manila or by ferry from various Visayas and Bicol ports.

01

Fly to Masbate City

Fly from Manila to Masbate Airport (~1 hour). Cebu Pacific and Philippine Airlines operate this route. The airport is about 3 km from the city center. Take a tricycle to the cathedral (~15 minutes).

Flight: ~1 hr  |  ~₱1,500₱3,500 one-way
02

Alternatively: Ferry from Cebu or Iloilo

Masbate City is connected by ferry to Cebu City (~8₱10 hours) and Iloilo City (~6₱8 hours). 2GO Travel and other operators serve these routes. A good option if you're island-hopping through the Visayas.

Ferry from Cebu: ~8₱10 hrs  |  ~₱500–₱1,200
03

Tricycle to the Cathedral

From anywhere in Masbate City, take a tricycle to the cathedral. It is located in the city center near the plaza — any driver will know it. The cathedral is within walking distance of most city hotels.

Tricycle: ~₱15–₱30  |  Walkable from city center
04

Combine with Buntod Reef & Ticao

Masbate Cathedral is the perfect starting or ending point for a Masbate marine adventure. Combine it with a morning trip to Buntod Reef and a multi-day visit to Ticao Island for whale shark encounters.

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

📌

Best Time to Visit

  • Rodeo Masbateo (April): The annual festival celebrating Masbate's cattle heritage — Philippine-style rodeo competitions, cultural shows, street parties, and the city at its most festive.
  • Feast of Saint Anthony of Padua (June 13): The cathedral's patron feast day — a major religious celebration with processions, masses, and community festivities.
  • March — June (Dry Season): Best weather for exploring Masbate City and combining with Buntod Reef and Ticao Island visits.
  • November — February: Cooler and drier — pleasant for city exploration and heritage visits.
—

Visiting the Cathedral

  • Dress modestly — the cathedral is an active place of worship. Cover shoulders and knees when entering.
  • Daily masses are held in the morning and evening. The cathedral is open to visitors throughout the day outside of mass times.
  • The plaza surrounding the cathedral is a pleasant spot to sit and observe local life — vendors, students, and families gather here throughout the day.
  • Photography of the exterior is freely permitted. Ask permission before photographing inside during services.
📌

Masbate Food & Culture

  • Masbate beef: The province is the cattle capital of the Philippines · the beef here is exceptional. Try inihaw na baka (grilled beef), beef caldereta, and beef kare-kare at local restaurants.
  • Rodeo Masbateo: If visiting in April, the rodeo festival is a unique cultural experience — Philippine cowboys (vaqueros) compete in lassoing, bull riding, and other cattle-herding events.
  • Fresh seafood: Despite the beef fame, Masbate's coastal location means excellent fresh fish, squid, and shellfish are also widely available.
  • Local markets: The Masbate City public market is a lively spot to experience local life and buy fresh produce, dried fish, and local crafts.
Mini Itinerary

A Day in Masbate City

Combine Masbate Cathedral with Buntod Reef and the city's local food scene for a perfect day in the Cattle Capital of the Philippines.

Morning
7:00 AM

Buntod Reef Snorkeling

Start the day with an early morning trip to Buntod Reef Marine Sanctuary. The morning light and calm conditions make for the best snorkeling. Spend 2₱3 hours exploring the coral gardens and the white sandbar.

10:30 AM

Return to Masbate City

Take the boat back to Masbate City. Freshen up at your hotel and head to the city center for the rest of the day's activities.

11:30 AM

Masbate Cathedral

Visit the Masbate Cathedral in the late morning. Walk around the exterior, admire the colonial architecture, and step inside the cool, serene interior. The plaza is a pleasant spot to rest and people-watch.

Afternoon
1:00 PM

Lunch: Masbate Beef

Have lunch at a local restaurant near the cathedral plaza. Order inihaw na baka or beef caldereta — Masbate's cattle heritage produces some of the best beef in the Philippines, and you should not leave without trying it.

3:00 PM

Masbate City Market & Waterfront

Explore the Masbate City public market for local produce, dried fish, and crafts. Then stroll along the city waterfront as the afternoon light softens over the harbor.

5:00 PM

Sunset at the Port

Watch the sunset from the Masbate City port as fishing boats return to harbor. The golden light over the water and the silhouettes of the boats make for a beautiful end to the day in this underrated provincial city.
