Introduction & About

A World Wonder Beneath the Earth

The Puerto Princesa Subterranean River National Park protects one of the world's most remarkable cave systems — an 8.2-kilometer navigable underground river that flows through a mountain and empties directly into the South China Sea. The cave contains some of the most dramatic stalactite and stalagmite formations in Southeast Asia, including chambers large enough to fit a cathedral.

The park is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and was voted one of the New Seven Wonders of Nature in 2012. It sits within a protected limestone karst landscape covered in old-growth tropical forest, home to eight of the Philippines' 13 forest types and over 800 plant species.

Visitors explore the river by paddle boat, guided by a boatman who navigates the dark passages while a recorded audio guide describes the formations overhead. The experience — gliding silently through a vast underground world lit only by your headlamp — is genuinely unlike anything else on earth.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to the Underground River

The Underground River is located in Sabang, about 80 km north of Puerto Princesa City. A permit is required and must be arranged in advance.

01

Fly to Puerto Princesa

Fly from Manila to Puerto Princesa International Airport. Multiple airlines operate this route daily. The flight takes about 1 hour 20 minutes. Book early — seats fill up fast, especially during peak season.

Flight: ~1 hr 20 min  |  ~₱1,500₱4,000 one-way
02

Get Your Underground River Permit

A permit is mandatory and must be secured before your visit. Get it at the City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO) in Puerto Princesa, or book through a licensed tour operator who will arrange it for you. Daily visitor numbers are strictly limited.

Permit fee: ~₱150  |  Book at least 1₱2 days ahead
03

Van to Sabang

From Puerto Princesa, take a van or join a tour to Sabang — the jump-off point for the Underground River. The drive takes about 2 hours along a scenic road through the Palawan jungle. Most tours include van transfer.

Van: ~2 hrs  |  ~₱200–₱300 per person
04

Boat to the Cave Entrance

From Sabang Beach, take a short bangka boat ride to the cave entrance — about 15 minutes across a calm bay. The approach by sea, with the limestone cliffs rising from the jungle ahead, is one of the most dramatic arrivals in Philippine travel.

Boat: ~15 min  |  Included in most tour packages

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

📌

Booking & Permits

  • Book your permit early. Daily visitor numbers are capped — permits sell out weeks in advance during peak season (NovemberMay).
  • Use a licensed tour operator for the easiest experience — they handle permits, transport, and boat transfers in one package.
  • Arrive at Sabang early. Boat departures to the cave are scheduled — missing your slot means waiting or losing your permit.
  • Check weather conditions. The cave tour may be cancelled during rough seas or heavy rain. Have a backup day planned.
🎒

What to Pack

  • Life jacket — provided at the cave, but wearing it is mandatory inside the river.
  • Headlamp or flashlight — the cave is dark. Your boatman has a light but bring your own for a better view of the formations.
  • Waterproof bag — protect your phone and camera. The cave is humid and the boat can get splashed.
  • Insect repellent — the jungle trail to the cave entrance has mosquitoes.
  • Cash — for permits, tips, and food at Sabang Beach.
📌

Inside the Cave

  • Watch for falling bat droppings. Millions of bats live in the cave — look up carefully and keep your mouth closed.
  • Listen to your guide. The boatman knows the cave intimately and will point out the most impressive formations.
  • Photography is allowed but flash can disturb the bats. Use a high-ISO setting for better results in low light.
  • The standard tour covers 4.3 km of the 8.2 km river. A special permit is required for the full length — arrange this in advance.
Mini Itinerary

How to Spend a Day at the Underground River

Most visitors do the Underground River as a day trip from Puerto Princesa. Combine it with Sabang Beach and the jungle trail for a full day in the national park.

Morning
6:00 AM

Depart Puerto Princesa

Leave Puerto Princesa early by van for the 2-hour drive to Sabang. The road passes through lush Palawan jungle and small farming communities. Arrive at Sabang Beach by 8 AM to check in for your boat slot.

8:30 AM

Boat to the Cave Entrance

Board a bangka at Sabang Beach for the 15-minute boat ride to the cave entrance. The approach by sea — limestone cliffs rising from the jungle, monkeys visible on the shore — is spectacular. Collect your life jacket and headlamp at the entrance.

9:00 AM

Underground River Boat Tour

Enter the cave by paddle boat. Your boatman navigates the dark passages while the audio guide describes the formations — the Cathedral Chamber, the Italian's Chamber, and the massive stalactites and stalagmites that have been forming for millions of years. The tour takes about 45 minutes.

Afternoon
10:30 AM

Sabang Beach & Jungle Trail

After the cave tour, relax on Sabang Beach — a beautiful stretch of sand backed by jungle. Walk the Monkey Trail through the national park forest, where long-tailed macaques, monitor lizards, and hornbills are commonly spotted.

12:30 PM

Lunch at Sabang

Eat at one of the small restaurants along Sabang Beach. Fresh grilled fish, kinilaw, and cold coconut water are the highlights. The setting — eating on the beach with the jungle behind you — is hard to beat.

2:00 PM

Return to Puerto Princesa

Board your van back to Puerto Princesa. In the evening, explore the city's famous Palaweno BBQ restaurants along Rizal Avenue — an essential Puerto Princesa experience after a full day in the national park.
