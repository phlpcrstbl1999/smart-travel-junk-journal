Introduction

Asia's Grandest Colonial Church

The Basilica of Saint Martin de Tours in Taal, Batangas is the largest Catholic church in Asia — a breathtaking monument to 18th-century Spanish colonial architecture, faith, and craftsmanship. Built between 1755 and 1865, the basilica stands on a commanding hilltop above the heritage town of Taal, its massive coral-stone facade visible from miles away across the Batangas countryside.

The church measures an extraordinary 96 meters long and 48 meters wide — dimensions that dwarf most European cathedrals. Its twin bell towers rise 42 meters above the plaza, and the interior is a soaring space of coral-stone columns, gilded altars, and centuries of accumulated religious art. The main retablo (altar screen) is one of the finest examples of colonial religious art in the Philippines, and the church's collection of antique religious artifacts is among the most significant in the country.

The basilica is the centerpiece of the heritage town of Taal — one of the best-preserved Spanish colonial towns in the Philippines, with ancestral houses, cobblestone streets, and a rich tradition of balisong (butterfly knife) craftsmanship and barong Tagalog weaving. The town itself is a National Cultural Treasure and a destination in its own right, best explored on foot after visiting the basilica.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Taal Basilica

The Taal Basilica is in Taal town, Batangas — about 110 km south of Manila. It's easily combined with Taal Volcano and Tagaytay Ridge in a single day trip.

01

Bus from Manila to Lemery or Taal

Take a DLTB or Jam Liner bus from Buendia (Pasay) to Lemery, Batangas. From Lemery, take a jeepney or tricycle to Taal town (about 15 minutes). Alternatively, buses to Batangas City pass through the area.

Manila to Lemery: ~2₱2.5 hours  |  ~₱150–₱200
02

Drive from Manila via SLEX

Drive via SLEX — Sto. Tomas — Tanauan — Taal. Having your own vehicle is ideal for combining Taal Basilica with Taal Volcano (Talisay) and Tagaytay Ridge in a single day trip — all within 30₱45 minutes of each other.

Manila to Taal: ~2₱2.5 hours  |  Parking near the basilica plaza
03

Climb the Basilica Steps

The basilica sits on a hill above the town — a broad staircase of 144 steps leads up from the town plaza to the church entrance. The climb is worth it: the view from the top of the steps across the Batangas countryside is magnificent.

Entrance: free (donations welcome)  |  Allow 1₱1.5 hours
04

Explore Taal Heritage Town

After the basilica, walk through the heritage streets of Taal town — ancestral houses, balisong workshops, barong Tagalog weavers, and local delicacies. The town is one of the best-preserved colonial heritage districts in Batangas.

Heritage walk: ~1₱2 hours  |  Balisong (butterfly knife) shopping

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

📌

Visiting the Basilica

  • Dress modestly: The basilica is an active place of worship. Shoulders and knees must be covered. Bring a shawl or sarong if needed — these are sometimes available at the entrance.
  • Mass schedule: Daily masses are held in the basilica. Check the schedule and avoid visiting during mass if you want to explore the interior freely. Sunday masses draw large congregations.
  • The 144 steps: The staircase up to the basilica is steep. Take your time — the view from the top is worth the climb. There is no elevator.
  • Photography: Photography is generally permitted inside the basilica but be respectful during services. The facade and the view from the steps are the most photogenic spots.
📌

Taal Heritage Town

  • Ancestral houses: Several of Taal's ancestral houses are open to visitors — the Agoncillo House and the Marcela Agoncillo Historical Landmark are particularly significant. Marcela Agoncillo sewed the first Philippine flag here.
  • Balisong (butterfly knife): Taal is the balisong capital of the Philippines. Visit the workshops to see the knives being made and buy authentic handcrafted pieces directly from the makers.
  • Barong Tagalog weaving: Taal is also famous for its pia and jusi fabric weaving — the traditional materials for the barong Tagalog national dress. Visit the weavers' workshops.
  • Local food: Try longganisang Taal (Taal-style sausage), tapang Taal, and the local kakanin (rice cakes) from the market stalls near the basilica.
📌

Combining with Other Destinations

  • Taal Volcano: About 30 minutes north of Taal town via Talisay. Do the volcano hike in the morning, then visit the basilica in the afternoon — the perfect Batangas day.
  • Tagaytay Ridge: About 45 minutes north of Taal town. Combine all three — volcano, basilica, and Tagaytay bulalo dinner — for the ultimate Batangas day trip.
  • Laiya Beach: About 1.5 hours east of Taal town. Extend your Batangas trip with a beach day at Laiya — white sand, clear water, and coral reefs.
  • Batangas City: About 30 minutes south — the main city of Batangas province, with the ferry terminal for boats to Puerto Galera in Mindoro.
Mini Itinerary

How to Spend a Day in Taal & Batangas

The ultimate Batangas day trip — Taal Volcano in the morning, Taal Basilica and heritage town in the afternoon, bulalo dinner at Tagaytay in the evening.

Morning
6:00 AM

Taal Volcano Boat & Hike

Start early at Talisay for the boat crossing and crater hike. Complete the volcano experience by 11 AM — the morning light on the crater lake is extraordinary and the trail is cooler early.

11:30 AM

Tawilis Lunch in Talisay

Have lunch at a lakeside restaurant in Talisay — fresh tawilis (Taal Lake sardines), grilled fish, and rice. The freshest tawilis in the Philippines is served here.

Afternoon
1:00 PM

Taal Basilica — The 144 Steps

Drive 30 minutes to Taal town. Climb the 144 steps to the basilica entrance. Explore the magnificent interior — the gilded altars, the soaring coral-stone nave, and the centuries of religious art.

2:30 PM

Heritage Town Walk

Walk through Taal's heritage streets — visit the Agoncillo House, browse the balisong workshops, and pick up barong Tagalog fabric from the weavers. Try the local longganisang Taal from the market.

5:00 PM

Tagaytay Sunset & Bulalo Dinner

Drive up to Tagaytay Ridge for the sunset view of Taal Volcano from above. Have bulalo dinner at a ridge restaurant — the perfect end to a perfect Batangas day.
