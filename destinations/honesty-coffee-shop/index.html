Introduction & Story

The Store That Runs on Trust

Located in the quiet municipality of Ivana on Batan Island, the Honesty Coffee Shop (also known as the Honesty Store) is one of the most talked-about stops on any Batanes itinerary — not for its food or its views, but for what it represents. The store is completely unmanned. There is no cashier, no attendant, and no security camera.

Visitors walk in, help themselves to local snacks, bottled drinks, coffee, and Batanes souvenirs, check the price list on the wall, and leave their payment in a box. The system has been running since 1999 and has become a symbol of the extraordinary honesty, trust, and integrity of the Ivatan people — the indigenous community of Batanes.

For many visitors, the Honesty Coffee Shop is the most emotionally moving stop in all of Batanes. In a world of surveillance cameras and locked display cases, this simple unmanned store is a profound statement about human nature and community trust. It has been featured in international media and is considered one of the most unique cultural experiences in Southeast Asia.

Location & Getting There

From Basco to Honesty Coffee Shop

The Honesty Coffee Shop is located in Ivana municipality, southern Batan Island — about 12 km south of Basco town. It's a standard stop on the South Batan Island tour.

01

Fly to Basco, Batanes

Philippine Airlines and SkyJet Airlines fly from NAIA Manila to Basco Airport. Flight time is about 1.5₱2 hours. Book well in advance — Batanes flights sell out quickly, especially MarchMay.

Flight: ~1.5₱2 hours  |  ~₱3,000₱8,000 round trip
02

Rent a Motorbike or Join a Tour

From Basco, rent a motorbike or join a guided South Batan Island tour. The Honesty Coffee Shop is a standard stop on the South Batan route, along with Marlboro Hills, Valugan Boulder Beach, and Diura Fishing Village.

Motorbike: ~₱500–₱700/day  |  Guided tour: ~₱800–₱1,500
03

Ride South to Ivana

From Basco, ride south along the scenic coastal road through Mahatao to Ivana municipality. The Honesty Coffee Shop is located along the main road in Ivana — look for the small wooden structure with the distinctive signage. It's easy to spot.

Basco to Ivana: ~25₱30 min  |  Along the main road in Ivana
04

Stop, Shop & Pay Honestly

Park outside, walk in, browse the items, check the price list, take what you want, and leave your exact payment in the collection box. It's that simple. And that profound. Take a moment to appreciate what this place represents before you leave.

🚌 Coffee: ~₱20–₱50  |  Snacks: ~₱20–₱100  |  Souvenirs: varies

Essential Visitor Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Visit

—

What's Available

  • Local coffee and drinks — Batanes coffee, bottled water, soft drinks, and local juices.
  • Ivatan snacks — local cookies, dried fish, and traditional Batanes food products.
  • Souvenirs — Batanes ref magnets, keychains, woven items, and other local crafts.
  • Postcards and books — about Batanes culture, history, and the Ivatan people.
  • Price list is posted on the wall inside the store — check it carefully and pay the correct amount.
📌

The Honor System

  • Pay the exact amount. The honor system only works if everyone pays honestly. Don't shortchange the store.
  • No change is given — bring small bills and coins. If you don't have exact change, round up generously.
  • Don't take without paying. This store is a symbol of Ivatan trust — respect it completely.
  • Leave a note in the comment book if you're moved by the experience — many visitors do.
  • The store is monitored by the community — not by cameras, but by the collective trust and watchfulness of the Ivatan people.
📌

Practical Tips

  • Bring small bills and coins. The payment box doesn't give change — exact payment is expected.
  • Visit as part of the South Batan tour — combine with Marlboro Hills, Valugan Boulder Beach, and Diura Fishing Village for a full day.
  • Buy local souvenirs here rather than at tourist shops — your money goes directly to the Ivatan community.
  • Don't rush. Sit down, have a coffee, and take in the atmosphere. The Honesty Coffee Shop deserves more than a 5-minute stop.
  • Respect the space. Don't rearrange items, don't take photos of the payment box, and leave the store as you found it.
Visitor Guide

How to Experience the Honesty Coffee Shop

A complete guide to the Honesty Coffee Shop and the full South Batan Island tour — the best way to experience Batanes culture and landscape in one day.

At the Store
Step 1

Arrive & Take It In

Before entering, pause and look at the store. It's a simple wooden structure — no lock on the door, no attendant inside, no security camera. Let that sink in. Then walk in.

Step 2

Browse & Choose

Browse the shelves — local coffee, snacks, drinks, and souvenirs. Check the price list on the wall. Take what you want and set it aside. Take your time — there's no rush and no one watching the clock.

Step 3

Pay Honestly

Count your items, calculate the total from the price list, and place your exact payment in the collection box. If you don't have exact change, round up. This is not just a transaction — it's a participation in a community's way of life.

Step 4

Sit, Reflect & Sign the Book

Sit outside with your coffee or snack and take in the quiet Ivatan countryside. Before you leave, sign the visitor comment book inside — your words become part of the store's ongoing story of trust and human connection.

Nearby

Marlboro Hills (Rakuh-a-Idi)

Rolling green hills with wild horses and Pacific Ocean views. Just minutes from the Honesty Coffee Shop. Ride a horse across the hills for the full Batanes experience.

Nearby

Valugan Boulder Beach

A beach covered entirely in massive volcanic boulders shaped by Pacific waves. One of the most unusual and dramatic beaches in the Philippines.

Nearby

Diura Fishing Village

A traditional Ivatan fishing village where fishermen still use tataya outrigger boats. Watch the boats come in and experience authentic Ivatan coastal life.

Evening

Basco Lighthouse at Sunset

End your day at the Basco Lighthouse on Naidi Hills. The white tower against the golden sky, with the Pacific below — the perfect close to a perfect Batanes day.
