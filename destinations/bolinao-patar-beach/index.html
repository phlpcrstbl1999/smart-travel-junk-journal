Introduction

Pangasinan's Most Dramatic Coastline

Located at the western tip of Bolinao, Pangasinan, Patar Beach is a long stretch of white sand and golden rock formations facing the open South China Sea. Unlike the calm, sheltered beaches of Hundred Islands, Patar is a wild, windswept coast — waves roll in from the open ocean, the sand is coarse and golden, and the rock formations along the shoreline create a dramatic, almost otherworldly landscape.

The beach is famous for its ancient rock formations — massive boulders and coral limestone outcroppings that have been sculpted by centuries of wind and waves into extraordinary shapes. At low tide, tide pools form between the rocks, teeming with sea urchins, starfish, and small fish. The Cape Bolinao Lighthouse, built in 1892, stands at the tip of the cape and is one of the most photographed landmarks in Pangasinan.

Patar Beach is also known for its spectacular sunsets — facing west over the South China Sea, the beach offers unobstructed views of the sun sinking below the horizon, painting the sky and the golden rocks in shades of orange, pink, and purple.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Patar Beach

Patar Beach is located in Bolinao, Pangasinan, about 280 km northwest of Manila. It's about 1 hour west of Alaminos City (Hundred Islands).

01

Bus from Manila to Bolinao

Five Star Bus operates direct trips from Cubao to Bolinao, Pangasinan. Travel time is about 6₱7 hours. Alternatively, take a bus to Alaminos and then a jeepney or van to Bolinao (about 1 hour).

Direct to Bolinao: ~6₱7 hours  |  ~₱400–₱550
02

Tricycle or Habal-habal to Patar

From Bolinao town center, hire a tricycle or habal-habal (motorcycle taxi) to Patar Beach. The ride takes about 20₱30 minutes along a scenic coastal road. Negotiate the fare before boarding.

Tricycle: ~₱100–₱150  |  — ~20₱30 minutes
03

From Hundred Islands (Alaminos)

If you're combining with Hundred Islands, take a jeepney or van from Alaminos to Bolinao (about 1 hour), then a tricycle to Patar Beach. This makes a perfect two-day Pangasinan itinerary.

Alaminos to Bolinao: ~1 hour  |  ~₱50–₱80
04

Drive from Manila

Driving from Manila via NLEXSCTEXTPLEX to Bolinao takes about 4₱5 hours. Having your own vehicle gives you the freedom to explore Patar Beach, the lighthouse, and Bolinao Falls at your own pace.

Drive: ~4₱5 hours  |  Parking available at the beach

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

🌞

Best Time to Visit

  • November — May (Dry Season): Best time — clear skies, calm enough for swimming, and spectacular sunsets. The golden rock formations glow beautifully in dry season light.
  • Sunset (5:00₱6:30 PM): Patar Beach faces west — the sunsets here are among the most dramatic in Pangasinan. Plan to be on the beach at least an hour before sunset.
  • Low Tide: Visit at low tide to explore the tide pools between the rock formations. Check tide tables before your visit.
  • Avoid June — October: Typhoon season brings strong waves and rough conditions. Swimming is not recommended.
📌

What to Do

  • Walk the rock formations at low tide — the ancient coral limestone boulders create a surreal landscape perfect for photography.
  • Visit Cape Bolinao Lighthouse — a 19th-century lighthouse at the tip of the cape. Climb for panoramic views of the coastline.
  • Swim and snorkel in the calmer sections of the beach — avoid the areas with strong currents and breaking waves.
  • Watch the sunset from the rock formations — the combination of golden rocks, crashing waves, and a blazing sky is unforgettable.
  • Explore the tide pools — sea urchins, starfish, small fish, and colorful anemones fill the pools between the rocks at low tide.
⚠

Safety & Practical Tips

  • Be careful of strong currents. Patar Beach faces the open South China Sea — waves and currents can be strong, especially during high tide. Swim only in designated safe areas.
  • Wear water shoes when walking on the rock formations — the coral limestone is sharp and uneven.
  • Bring cash — there are very few facilities at Patar Beach. Stock up on food, water, and supplies in Bolinao town before heading out.
  • Don't take coral or shells. The rock formations and marine life are protected — leave everything as you found it.
  • Bring sunscreen and a hat — there is very little shade on the beach and the sun is intense.
Visitor Guide

How to Experience Patar Beach

A complete guide to exploring Patar Beach and combining it with the best of Bolinao for a full day of discovery.

At Patar Beach
Morning

Arrive & Explore the Rock Formations

Arrive in the morning when the light is soft and the beach is uncrowded. Walk along the shoreline and explore the ancient rock formations — each one is unique, sculpted by centuries of wind and waves into extraordinary shapes.

Mid-Morning

Cape Bolinao Lighthouse

Walk or ride to the Cape Bolinao Lighthouse at the tip of the cape. The 1892 lighthouse offers sweeping views of the coastline and the open South China Sea. The surrounding area has dramatic cliff views.

Afternoon

Swimming & Tide Pool Exploration

Swim in the calmer sections of the beach and explore the tide pools at low tide. The pools between the rock formations are filled with marine life — a natural aquarium accessible without a boat.

Late Afternoon

Sunset from the Rocks

Position yourself on the rock formations for sunset. The combination of golden rocks, crashing waves, and a blazing western sky is one of the most spectacular natural shows in Pangasinan. Bring a tripod for long-exposure shots.

Nearby in Bolinao
30 min away

Bolinao Falls

A series of stunning waterfalls with natural swimming pools in the jungle. The cool, clear water is a perfect contrast to the salty beach. Multiple tiers to explore — each with its own pool and cascade.

1 hr away

Hundred Islands National Park

124 islands and islets in the Lingayen Gulf. Island hopping, snorkeling, cliff jumping, and cave exploration. The perfect complement to a Patar Beach visit.

In Bolinao Town

Bolinao Church & Museum

The Saint James the Apostle Parish Church — a 17th-century Spanish colonial church in Bolinao town. The adjacent museum houses pre-colonial artifacts and historical exhibits.
