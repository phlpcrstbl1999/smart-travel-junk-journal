Introduction

Pangasinan's Hidden Jungle Waterfalls

Tucked away in the lush jungle of Bolinao, Pangasinan, Bolinao Falls is a series of three tiered waterfalls — each with its own natural swimming pool fed by cool, crystal-clear mountain water. The falls are surrounded by dense tropical vegetation, creating a cool, shaded sanctuary that feels worlds away from the hot, sunny beaches of the Pangasinan coast.

The three tiers offer different experiences. Falls 1 is the most accessible and most visited — a wide, powerful cascade dropping into a deep, clear pool perfect for swimming. Falls 2 is a short hike upstream and is quieter, with a more intimate pool surrounded by mossy rocks. Falls 3 is the most remote and least visited — a reward for those willing to make the longer trek through the jungle.

Bolinao Falls is a perfect complement to a Patar Beach visit — the cool, fresh water of the falls is a refreshing contrast to the salty, sun-drenched coast. Many visitors combine both in a single day, spending the morning at the falls and the afternoon at the beach for sunset.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Bolinao Falls

Bolinao Falls is located in Bolinao, Pangasinan, about 280 km northwest of Manila. It's about 30 minutes from Patar Beach and 1 hour from Hundred Islands.

01

Bus from Manila to Bolinao

Five Star Bus operates direct trips from Cubao to Bolinao, Pangasinan. Travel time is about 6₱7 hours. Alternatively, take a bus to Alaminos and then a jeepney or van to Bolinao (about 1 hour).

Direct to Bolinao: ~6₱7 hours  |  ~₱400–₱550
02

Tricycle to Bolinao Falls

From Bolinao town center, hire a tricycle to Bolinao Falls. The ride takes about 15₱20 minutes. Tell the driver you're going to Bolinao Falls 1 — the main entrance. The driver can wait and take you to Patar Beach afterward.

Tricycle: ~₱80–₱120  |  — ~15₱20 minutes
03

Register & Pay Entrance Fee

At the entrance, register and pay the small entrance fee. A local guide is available for hire — recommended if you want to visit Falls 2 and Falls 3, as the trail can be slippery and confusing without guidance.

Entrance fee: ~₱30–₱50  |  Guide: ~₱150–₱200
04

Combine with Patar Beach

Visit Bolinao Falls in the morning when the jungle is cool and the light filters beautifully through the canopy. Then head to Patar Beach in the afternoon for swimming, rock formation exploration, and sunset. A perfect Bolinao day.

Patar Beach: ~30 min away  |  Perfect for afternoon + sunset

Essential Visitor Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Visit

🌞

Best Time to Visit

  • Year-round: Bolinao Falls flows throughout the year, but the volume and power of the falls varies by season.
  • June — October (Rainy Season): The falls are at their most powerful and dramatic — full, thundering cascades. However, the trails can be slippery and the pools may be murky after heavy rain.
  • November — May (Dry Season): Clearer water, safer trails, and better swimming conditions. The falls are calmer but still beautiful.
  • Morning visits: The jungle is coolest and the light filters most beautifully through the canopy in the morning. Arrive early to beat the crowds.
📌

Swimming & Trekking Tips

  • Wear water shoes or old sneakers — the trail to the falls involves river crossings and slippery rocks. Flip-flops are not safe.
  • Bring a change of clothes — you will get wet. Pack dry clothes in a waterproof bag.
  • Hire a local guide for Falls 2 and Falls 3 — the trail is not well-marked and can be confusing without guidance.
  • Don't swim alone near the base of the falls — the current can be strong, especially after rain.
  • Life jackets are available for rent at Falls 1 — recommended for non-swimmers and children.
🎒

What to Pack

  • Swimwear — you'll definitely want to swim in the natural pools.
  • Waterproof bag or dry bag — protect your phone, camera, and valuables from water.
  • Insect repellent — the jungle trail has mosquitoes, especially in the morning.
  • Packed lunch and water — there are small food stalls near Falls 1, but options are limited. Bring your own for Falls 2 and 3.
  • Cash — no ATMs in the area. Withdraw in Bolinao town or Alaminos before heading out.
Visitor Guide

How to Experience Bolinao Falls

A complete guide to exploring all three tiers of Bolinao Falls and combining it with the best of Bolinao for a full day.

At the Falls
Step 1

Falls 1 — The Main Cascade

Start at Falls 1 — the most accessible and most impressive. A wide, powerful cascade drops into a deep, clear pool. Swim, float, and enjoy the cool water. The surrounding rocks are perfect for sitting and taking in the scene.

Step 2

Falls 2 — The Quiet Pool

Follow the trail upstream (about 15₱20 minutes) to Falls 2. This tier is quieter and more intimate — a smaller cascade feeding a mossy, shaded pool. Far fewer visitors make it here, so you'll likely have it to yourself.

Step 3

Falls 3 — The Hidden Reward

Continue upstream (another 20₱30 minutes with a guide) to Falls 3 — the most remote and least visited tier. The trek involves river crossings and some scrambling, but the reward is a pristine, secluded waterfall that feels completely untouched.

Step 4

Return & Lunch

Return to Falls 1 for a final swim, then head to the small food stalls near the entrance for lunch. Try the local inihaw na tilapia (grilled tilapia) — fresh, affordable, and delicious after a morning in the jungle.

Nearby in Bolinao
30 min away

Patar Beach

Wild, windswept coastline with dramatic rock formations and spectacular sunsets. Perfect for an afternoon after your morning at the falls. The contrast of jungle and coast makes for a perfect Bolinao day.

30 min away

Cape Bolinao Lighthouse

A 19th-century lighthouse at the tip of Cape Bolinao. Sweeping views of the South China Sea and the Pangasinan coastline. Combine with a Patar Beach visit.

1 hr away

Hundred Islands National Park

124 islands in the Lingayen Gulf. Island hopping, snorkeling, cliff jumping, and cave exploration. The perfect next-day destination after Bolinao.
