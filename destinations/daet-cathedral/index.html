Introduction & History

Four Centuries of Faith in Bicol

The Parroquia de San Juan Bautista — commonly known as Daet Cathedral — is the seat of the Roman Catholic Diocese of Daet and the oldest and most significant church in Camarines Norte. The parish was founded by Augustinian missionaries in 1578, making it one of the earliest Catholic parishes established in the Bicol Region during the Spanish colonial period.

The current church structure, rebuilt and renovated multiple times over the centuries following earthquakes and typhoon damage, retains its colonial character with a classic Baroque-influenced facade, twin bell towers, and a spacious interior that reflects the deep Catholic faith of the Bicolano people. The church faces the Daet town plaza — the social and civic heart of the provincial capital.

For travelers passing through Daet on their way to Bagasbas Beach or Calaguas Islands, the cathedral is a worthwhile stop — a moment of quiet reflection and historical connection in a town that serves as the gateway to some of Bicol's most exciting natural destinations.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Daet Cathedral

The Daet Cathedral is located at the Daet town plaza — the center of Daet, Camarines Norte, about 330 km from Manila.

01

Overnight Bus from Manila to Daet

Take an overnight bus from Manila (Cubao or Pasay) to Daet, Camarines Norte. Philtranco, DLTB Co., and Superlines operate this route. The journey takes about 7₱8 hours, arriving in Daet by early morning.

Bus: ~7₱8 hrs  |  ~₱500–₱800 one-way
02

Walk or Tricycle from the Terminal

The Daet bus terminal is close to the town center. The cathedral is at the town plaza — a short walk or tricycle ride from the terminal. Ask any local for directions to the plaza; the cathedral is impossible to miss.

Walk: ~10₱15 min  |  Tricycle: ~₱20–₱30
03

Combine with Bagasbas Beach

The cathedral is the perfect first stop on a Daet itinerary. After visiting, take a tricycle to Bagasbas Beach (3 km, ~10 minutes) for surfing or a beach walk. The two sites complement each other perfectly — heritage and nature in one morning.

Tricycle to Bagasbas: ~10 min, ~₱30–₱50
04

Daet as a Base for Calaguas

Daet is the main gateway to Calaguas Islands. Most tour operators for Calaguas are based in Daet town. Visit the cathedral in the morning, surf at Bagasbas in the afternoon, and depart for Calaguas the following morning.

Calaguas boat: departs ~5 AM from Paracale Port

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

📌

Best Time to Visit

  • Year-round: The cathedral is open daily and can be visited at any time of year. It is an active parish with regular masses.
  • Early morning (6:00₱8:00 AM): Morning mass is a beautiful time to visit — the church is filled with local parishioners and the light through the windows is soft and golden.
  • Fiesta de San Juan Bautista (June 24): The feast day of the patron saint is celebrated with processions, masses, and street festivities — the most vibrant time to visit Daet.
  • Holy Week: The cathedral is the center of Daet's Holy Week observances — solemn processions and religious activities fill the town plaza.
—

Visiting the Cathedral

  • Dress modestly — shoulders and knees should be covered inside the church. A shawl or cover-up is recommended.
  • Check mass schedules before visiting — the cathedral holds multiple masses daily. Visiting during mass is welcome but be respectful of the service.
  • Explore the plaza surrounding the cathedral — the Daet town plaza has historical markers, a monument to local heroes, and a pleasant park atmosphere.
  • Free to enter — donations are appreciated to support the parish's heritage conservation.
📌

Daet Food & Culture

  • Try Bicolano food in Daet — Bicol Express, laing, and fresh seafood are the local specialties. Several good restaurants are within walking distance of the plaza.
  • Daet Public Market — a short walk from the cathedral, the market is lively in the morning with fresh produce, local snacks, and Bicolano delicacies.
  • Pasalubong: Look for pili nuts — Bicol's most famous product — at market stalls and pasalubong shops near the plaza.
  • Combine with Bagasbas and Calaguas for a complete Camarines Norte experience — heritage, surf, and pristine island in one trip.
Mini Itinerary

A Day in Daet & Camarines Norte

Daet is compact and easy to explore in a day — combine the cathedral, Bagasbas Beach, and local food for a complete Camarines Norte experience.

Morning
6:30 AM

Morning Mass at the Cathedral

Attend early morning mass at the Parroquia de San Juan Bautista. The church is peaceful and beautiful at this hour — soft light, the scent of incense, and the quiet devotion of local parishioners. Explore the interior and the plaza after mass.

8:00 AM

Daet Public Market Breakfast

Walk to the Daet Public Market for breakfast — puto, bibingka, fresh taho, and local snacks. The market is lively and colorful in the morning, a genuine slice of Bicolano daily life.

9:30 AM

Bagasbas Beach

Take a tricycle to Bagasbas Beach (10 minutes). Rent a surfboard or book a lesson, swim in the Pacific waves, or simply walk the long stretch of dark volcanic sand. The beach is at its best in the morning before the midday heat sets in.

Afternoon
12:30 PM

Lunch: Bicol Express & Laing

Return to Daet for lunch at a local restaurant. Order the full Bicolano spread — Bicol Express, laing, and fresh grilled fish. Wash it down with fresh buko juice. Daet has excellent local restaurants within walking distance of the plaza.

2:00 PM

Pasalubong Shopping

Browse the pasalubong shops near the plaza for pili nuts — Bicol's most famous product, available in various preparations: raw, roasted, candied, and in pastries. Also look for pinipig and local kakanin (rice cakes).

4:00 PM

Prepare for Calaguas (Optional)

If continuing to Calaguas Islands, use the afternoon to arrange your tour package, buy supplies, and get an early night. Boats to Calaguas depart at 5 AM — a good night's sleep is essential before the crossing.
