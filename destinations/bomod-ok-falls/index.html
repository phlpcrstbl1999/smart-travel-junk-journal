Introduction & Overview

Sagada's Hidden Waterfall

Bomod-ok Falls (Big Falls) is located in Sagada, Mountain Province — a powerful 200-foot waterfall that plunges into a wide natural pool surrounded by lush forest. It is one of the most beautiful waterfalls in the Cordillera and a highlight of any Sagada visit.

The trek to Bomod-ok takes about 2 hours each way from Sagada town, passing through traditional Igorot rice terraces, pine forest, and the village of Fidelisan — a small community of traditional Kankanaey houses perched on the mountainside. The trek itself is as rewarding as the destination, offering beautiful views of the Sagada highlands and an intimate encounter with the living agricultural landscape.

The pool at the base of the falls is excellent for swimming — the water is cold, clear, and refreshing after the 2-hour trek. The falls are most powerful during and after the rainy season, but are beautiful year-round.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Bomod-ok Falls

Bomod-ok Falls is accessed from Sagada town — a 2-hour trek through rice terraces and forest. Sagada is reached via Baguio City.

01

Bus to Baguio, then Sagada

Take a bus from Manila to Baguio City (~5₱6 hours), then a bus or van to Sagada (~5₱6 hours). Stay overnight in Sagada before the Bomod-ok trek.

Manila to Sagada: ~10₱12 hrs total
02

Register at Tourism Office

Register at the Sagada Tourism Office and hire a licensed local guide. A guide is mandatory for the Bomod-ok trek — they know the trail and provide important cultural context about the villages and terraces along the way.

Environmental fee: ~₱50–₱100  |  Guide: ~₱300–₱500
03

Trek to Bomod-ok

The 2-hour trek from Sagada town passes through the Fidelisan rice terraces and pine forest before descending to the falls. The trail is well-marked but can be slippery after rain. Wear sturdy footwear.

Trek: ~2 hrs each way  |  Bring swimwear
04

Swim at the Falls

Spend time at the falls and natural pool — swim in the cold, clear water, rest on the rocks, and enjoy the sound of the 200-foot cascade. Allow at least 1₱2 hours at the falls before the return trek.

🚌 Allow 5₱6 hrs total (trek + falls + return)

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

📌

Best Time to Visit

  • March — May (Dry Season): Best trail conditions and clearest water. The trek through the rice terraces is most beautiful when the fields are flooded or green.
  • October — November: The falls are most powerful after the rainy season — the cascade is at its most dramatic.
  • Morning start: Begin the trek early to avoid the afternoon heat and to have the falls to yourself before other groups arrive.
  • Avoid heavy rain: The trail can become very slippery and the river crossing near the falls can be dangerous in heavy rain.
🧦

Trekking Tips

  • Wear sturdy footwear: The trail involves uneven terrain, river crossings, and steep sections. Hiking boots or trail shoes are recommended.
  • Bring swimwear and a towel: The pool at the base of the falls is excellent for swimming. The cold water is a perfect reward after the 2-hour trek.
  • The Fidelisan terraces: The rice terraces along the trail are beautiful — take time to appreciate them and ask your guide about the Kankanaey farming culture.
  • Trekking poles are helpful on the steep descent to the falls.
🎒

What to Pack & Know

  • Packed lunch and water: Bring enough food and water for the full day — there are no vendors at the falls.
  • Waterproof bag for your camera and valuables — the river crossing and swimming can get your gear wet.
  • Combine with Sagada highlights: Do the Hanging Coffins and Sumaguing Cave on Day 1, and Bomod-ok Falls on Day 2 for the complete Sagada experience.
  • Sagada coffee: Buy local Arabica coffee as pasalubong — it's the best souvenir from Sagada.
Mini Itinerary

A Day at Bomod-ok Falls

Combine the Bomod-ok Falls trek with the Fidelisan rice terraces and a Sagada sunrise for a perfect Mountain Province day.

Morning
5:30 AM

Sagada Sunrise

Wake before dawn for the Sagada sunrise from the viewpoint above town. The mountains and valleys below turn gold as the sun rises — one of the most beautiful sunrises in the Cordillera.

7:00 AM

Breakfast & Register

Have breakfast at a local cafe — try the Sagada Arabica coffee and local Igorot dishes. Register at the tourism office and meet your guide for the Bomod-ok trek.

8:00 AM

Trek to Bomod-ok

Begin the 2-hour trek through the Fidelisan rice terraces and pine forest. The trail offers beautiful views of the Sagada highlands and passes through the traditional Kankanaey village of Fidelisan.

Afternoon
10:00 AM

Bomod-ok Falls

Arrive at Bomod-ok Falls and spend 1₱2 hours at the falls. Swim in the cold, clear pool, rest on the rocks, and enjoy the sound and sight of the 200-foot cascade.

12:00 PM

Lunch by the Falls

Have your packed lunch at the falls — eating beside a 200-foot waterfall in the Cordillera mountains is a memorable experience.

2:00 PM

Return Trek & Sagada Town

Trek back to Sagada town (~2 hours). Buy Sagada coffee and local crafts before the evening bus back to Baguio.
