Introduction & Overview

One of the World's Largest Malls

SM Mall of Asia (MOA) is a massive shopping and entertainment complex in Pasay, Metro Manila, built on reclaimed land along the shores of Manila Bay. With a gross floor area of over 407,000 square meters, it is consistently ranked among the largest malls in the world.

Beyond shopping, MOA is an entertainment destination — it houses an IMAX theater, an ice skating rink, a concert grounds (one of the largest outdoor concert venues in the Philippines), an SMX Convention Center, and the famous MOA Arena (a 20,000-seat indoor arena).

The mall's greatest attraction may be its outdoor bayside promenade — a wide walkway facing Manila Bay where the sunset views are extraordinary. The promenade is lined with restaurants and cafes, making it one of the most popular evening destinations in Metro Manila.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

Getting to SM Mall of Asia

SM Mall of Asia is in Pasay, Metro Manila — accessible by LRT, bus, taxi, or Grab.

01

LRT Line 1 — EDSA Station + Bus

Take LRT Line 1 to EDSA Station, then take a jeepney or bus along Roxas Boulevard to MOA. Alternatively, take a Grab from the EDSA station.

LRT: EDSA Station  |  Jeepney/bus to MOA: ~₱12–₱15
02

Grab or Taxi

Grab and taxis are the most convenient options. MOA is well-served by Grab — book in advance during peak hours and weekends.

Grab/Taxi: ~₱100–₱200 from Makati  |  Book via Grab app
03

Bayside Promenade

The outdoor bayside promenade is the highlight of MOA — walk along the waterfront facing Manila Bay. The promenade is free to access and is most beautiful at sunset.

Best at sunset: ~5:30₱6:30 PM  |  Free to access
04

MOA Arena & Events

Check the MOA Arena and Concert Grounds schedule — major concerts, sporting events, and exhibitions are held here regularly. Book tickets in advance for popular events.

MOA Arena: 20,000 seats  |  Concert Grounds: outdoor events

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

📌

The Bayside Promenade

  • Sunset views: The MOA bayside promenade offers some of the best Manila Bay sunset views in Metro Manila — arrive by 5:30 PM for the best light.
  • Promenade restaurants: Dozens of restaurants and cafes line the promenade — from casual Filipino food to international dining with bay views.
  • Weekend evenings: The promenade is most vibrant on weekend evenings — families, couples, and groups enjoying the bay breeze and the sunset.
  • Free to access: The promenade is free — you don't need to enter the mall to enjoy the waterfront.
📌

Entertainment

  • IMAX Theater: One of the largest IMAX screens in the Philippines · book tickets online for popular films.
  • Ice skating rink: The SM MOA ice skating rink is one of the few in Metro Manila — popular with families and groups.
  • MOA Arena: A 20,000-seat indoor arena hosting major concerts, boxing matches, and sporting events.
  • Concert Grounds: The outdoor concert grounds host major outdoor events — check the schedule for concerts during your visit.
📌

Practical Tips

  • Weekday visits: MOA is extremely crowded on weekends — visit on a weekday for a more comfortable experience.
  • Parking: MOA has extensive parking but it fills up quickly on weekends. Consider taking Grab to avoid parking stress.
  • Combine with Manila Baywalk: MOA is at the southern end of the Manila Bay waterfront — combine with the Manila Baywalk and Rizal Park for a full bay experience.
  • Food court: The MOA food court has an excellent selection of Filipino and international food at affordable prices.

A Manila Bay Evening

Afternoon
3:00 PM

SM Mall of Asia

Arrive at SM Mall of Asia in the afternoon. Explore the mall, catch a movie at the IMAX theater, or browse the shops.

5:00 PM

Bayside Promenade Sunset

Head to the bayside promenade for the Manila Bay sunset. Find a spot at one of the waterfront restaurants or cafes and watch the sky turn orange and pink over the bay.

Evening
7:00 PM

Dinner at MOA

Have dinner at one of the many restaurants in the mall or on the promenade. The bayside restaurants offer the best atmosphere — dining with Manila Bay views after dark.

Evening Stroll

Take an evening stroll along the promenade — the bay is beautiful at night, with the lights of the ships and the city reflected on the water.
