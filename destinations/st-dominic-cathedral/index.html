Introduction & History

The Cathedral of the Nueva Vizcaya Highlands

Located in the heart of Bayombong — the capital of Nueva Vizcaya province — the Saint Dominic Cathedral (also known as the Cathedral Parish of Saint Dominic de Guzman) is the mother church of the Diocese of Bayombong and the most significant religious and heritage structure in the province.

The parish was founded by Dominican missionaries in 1739, making it one of the oldest Catholic communities in the Cagayan Valley highland region. The current cathedral structure reflects centuries of construction, renovation, and restoration — blending Spanish colonial architectural elements with later additions that reflect the evolving needs and resources of the highland diocese.

The cathedral stands on the Bayombong town plaza — the central square of the provincial capital — and serves as the focal point of the city's religious and civic life. The surrounding plaza is a pleasant public space with gardens, benches, and views of the cathedral facade. The interior features original religious artwork, wooden altars, and stained glass windows that create a serene and contemplative atmosphere.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to St. Dominic Cathedral

St. Dominic Cathedral is located on the Bayombong town plaza in Bayombong, Nueva Vizcaya — the provincial capital, about 250 km north of Manila.

01

Bus from Manila to Bayombong

Autobus Transport and Victory Liner operate buses from Cubao or Pasay to Bayombong, Nueva Vizcaya. Travel time is about 6₱7 hours. Buses run throughout the day and overnight. Bayombong is the main hub for all Nueva Vizcaya destinations.

Travel time: ~6₱7 hours  |  ~₱400–₱600 one-way
02

Arrive at Bayombong Bus Terminal

The bus terminal is in the center of Bayombong. From the terminal, the cathedral is just a short walk or tricycle ride to the town plaza. The cathedral's bell tower is visible from most parts of the city center.

Walk from terminal: ~10₱15 min  |  Tricycle: ~₱20–₱30
03

Arrive at the Town Plaza

The cathedral faces the Bayombong town plaza — a pleasant public square with gardens and benches. The cathedral entrance is on the plaza side. Entry is free and the cathedral is open daily for visitors and mass.

Free entry  |  Open daily for visitors and mass
04

Use Bayombong as Your Base

Bayombong is the ideal base for exploring Nueva Vizcaya. From here, you can visit Capisaan Cave (Kasibu), Imugan Falls (Santa Fe), and other provincial attractions. Good accommodation and food options are available in the city.

Good accommodation in Bayombong  |  Gateway to all NV destinations

Essential Visitor Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Visit

🌞

Best Time to Visit

  • Morning (7:00₱10:00 AM): The cathedral is peaceful before the midday crowds. Morning light through the stained glass windows is beautiful.
  • Late Afternoon (4:00₱6:00 PM): The facade catches warm golden light. The plaza is lively with locals enjoying the cool highland evening air.
  • Feast of St. Dominic (August 8): The town fiesta — colorful celebrations, processions, and cultural events centered on the cathedral.
  • Year-round: Bayombong's highland climate is pleasant throughout the year — cooler than Manila and comfortable for walking.
📌

What to See

  • The facade — the cathedral's front elevation facing the plaza is the most photogenic view. Include the plaza gardens in the foreground for context.
  • The interior — original wooden altars, religious paintings, and stained glass windows. The nave is simple but dignified.
  • The bell tower — the detached or adjacent bell tower is a characteristic feature of Philippine colonial churches.
  • The town plaza — the surrounding plaza is a pleasant public space that gives context to the cathedral's role in Bayombong's civic life.
📌

Etiquette & Practical Tips

  • Dress modestly — cover shoulders and knees when entering the cathedral. This is an active place of worship.
  • Be quiet inside and respectful of worshippers. Turn off phone ringers before entering.
  • Use Bayombong as your base for exploring Nueva Vizcaya — good accommodation, restaurants, and transport connections to Capisaan Cave and Imugan Falls.
  • Visit the Bayombong Public Market nearby for local produce, Ibaloi crafts, and authentic Nueva Vizcaya food.
  • Donations are appreciated at the cathedral entrance to support maintenance and community programs.
Visitor Guide

How to Experience St. Dominic Cathedral

A complete guide to the cathedral and a suggested Nueva Vizcaya itinerary using Bayombong as your base.

At the Cathedral
Step 1

The Town Plaza

Begin at the Bayombong town plaza. Walk around the plaza to appreciate the cathedral's setting — the gardens, the benches, the surrounding civic buildings. The cathedral has been the center of Bayombong's public life for nearly three centuries.

Step 2

The Facade

Study the cathedral facade — the architectural details, the bell tower, and the entrance portal. The facade reflects the Spanish colonial heritage of the Dominican missionaries who founded the parish in 1739.

Step 3

The Interior

Enter the cathedral and explore the interior — the nave, the altars, the religious artwork, and the stained glass windows. The atmosphere is serene and contemplative. If mass is in progress, sit quietly and observe.

Step 4

Bayombong City Walk

After the cathedral, explore Bayombong city center — the public market, the local restaurants, and the highland atmosphere. Try local Nueva Vizcaya dishes and pick up Ibaloi crafts as souvenirs.

Nueva Vizcaya Day Trips
Day Trip 1

Capisaan Cave System (Kasibu)

One of the longest cave systems in Luzon. Multiple chambers, underground rivers, and dramatic formations. About 1₱1.5 hours from Bayombong via Kasibu. A full-day adventure.

Day Trip 2

Imugan Falls (Santa Fe)

Multi-tiered waterfall in the Caraballo Mountains. About 1.5₱2 hours from Bayombong via Santa Fe. Trek through indigenous community land with a local guide.

Nearby

Ambaguio & Tinoc

Remote highland municipalities with traditional Ibaloi and Kalanguya communities, rice terraces, and mountain scenery. For adventurous travelers who want to go deeper into Nueva Vizcaya.
