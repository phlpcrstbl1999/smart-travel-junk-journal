Introduction

Quirino's Hidden Cave Sanctuary

Located in the municipality of Aglipay, Quirino province, the Aglipay Caves National Park is a protected area encompassing a network of limestone caves set within a forested landscape in the foothills of the Sierra Madre mountain range. The park was established to protect both the cave ecosystem and the surrounding forest, which serves as habitat for diverse wildlife including bats, cave swiftlets, and various endemic species.

The cave system features multiple chambers with stalactite and stalagmite formations, underground passages, and the atmospheric darkness that makes cave exploration such a unique experience. The caves are home to large colonies of bats — a key part of the local ecosystem — and the sight and sound of thousands of bats emerging at dusk is one of the most memorable natural spectacles in Quirino province.

What makes Aglipay Caves particularly special is its setting within a national park — the surrounding forest is protected and largely intact, giving the area a genuine wilderness character. The combination of cave exploration, forest trekking, and wildlife observation makes Aglipay a rewarding destination for nature lovers and adventure travelers who want to experience Quirino province beyond its more famous attractions.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Aglipay Caves

Aglipay Caves National Park is located in Aglipay, Quirino province — about 300 km north of Manila. The nearest major city is Cabarroguis, the capital of Quirino.

01

Bus from Manila to Cabarroguis

Autobus Transport and other bus lines operate services from Cubao, Quezon City to Cabarroguis, Quirino. Travel time is about 7₱8 hours. Buses typically depart in the evening for an overnight journey.

Travel time: ~7₱8 hours  |  ~₱500–₱700 one-way
02

Cabarroguis to Aglipay

From Cabarroguis, take a jeepney or van to Aglipay municipality — about 30₱45 minutes. From Aglipay town center, hire a tricycle or habal-habal to the national park entrance.

Jeepney to Aglipay: ~30₱45 min  |  Tricycle to park: ~₱50–₱80
03

Register at the Park Office

Register at the DENR park office at the entrance and pay the entrance fee. Local guides are available and recommended — they know the cave system well and can ensure your safety throughout the exploration.

Entrance fee: ~₱50–₱100  |  Guide: ~₱150–₱250
04

Combine with Quirino Day Tour

Aglipay Caves is best combined with other Quirino attractions. Visit Governor Rapids and Siitan Nature's Park for a full Quirino adventure — the province has much to offer beyond the caves.

Combine with Governor Rapids & Siitan  |  Full day recommended

Essential Visitor Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Visit

🌞

Best Time to Visit

  • November — May (Dry Season): Best time — dry trails, accessible cave passages, and comfortable temperatures for forest trekking.
  • Dusk (5:00₱6:30 PM): Watch thousands of bats emerge from the cave entrance at sunset — one of the most spectacular natural events in Quirino province.
  • Morning visits: The cave is coolest and most atmospheric in the morning. Start early to explore before the afternoon heat.
  • Avoid heavy rain periods: Wet cave floors and slippery trails make exploration more difficult and potentially dangerous.
🔦

Cave & Wildlife Tips

  • Bring a headlamp with fresh batteries — cave lighting is minimal. A backup flashlight is strongly recommended.
  • Wear non-slip shoes — cave floors are wet and uneven. Sandals and flip-flops are not safe.
  • Don't disturb the bats. The bat colonies are a protected part of the cave ecosystem — loud noises and bright lights can stress the animals.
  • Don't touch the formations. Stalactites and stalagmites are irreplaceable — even touching them causes damage over time.
  • Watch the bat emergence at dusk from a respectful distance — the spectacle is best observed quietly without flash photography.
🎒

What to Pack

  • Headlamp and backup flashlight with fresh batteries.
  • Non-slip trekking shoes — suitable for both cave floors and forest trails.
  • Insect repellent — the forest surrounding the caves has mosquitoes.
  • Water and snacks — no food stalls inside the park. Bring enough for the full day.
  • Cash — no ATMs in Aglipay. Withdraw in Cabarroguis before heading out.
  • Light rain jacket — the forest can be wet even in dry season.
Visitor Guide

How to Experience Aglipay Caves

A complete guide to the caves and a suggested Quirino province day tour.

At the Caves
Morning

Forest Trek to the Cave Entrance

Begin with a short forest trek from the park office to the cave entrance. The trail passes through protected forest — listen for birds and watch for wildlife. The cave entrance is a dramatic opening in the limestone hillside.

Mid-Morning

Cave Exploration

Explore the cave chambers with your guide. The formations — stalactites, stalagmites, columns, and cave curtains — are the highlights. Your guide will point out the most significant features and explain the geology of the cave system.

Afternoon

Forest Walk & Wildlife Observation

After the cave, explore the surrounding national park forest. The area is rich in birdlife and the forest itself is beautiful — tall trees, ferns, and the sounds of the Sierra Madre wilderness.

Dusk

Bat Emergence

Position yourself near the cave entrance before sunset and wait for the bat emergence. As the light fades, thousands of bats stream out of the cave in a continuous ribbon against the darkening sky — one of the most extraordinary natural spectacles in Quirino.

Quirino Day Tour
Morning

Aglipay Caves National Park

Start your Quirino day at the caves. Explore the chambers and the surrounding forest in the cool morning hours.

Midday

Governor Rapids

Head to Governor Rapids on the Cagayan River — a series of dramatic rapids and river scenery perfect for swimming, kayaking, and picnicking. About 1 hour from Aglipay.

Afternoon

Siitan Nature's Park

Visit Siitan Nature's Park — a scenic riverside park along the Siitan River with swimming holes, forest trails, and a peaceful natural setting. A perfect end to a Quirino adventure day.
