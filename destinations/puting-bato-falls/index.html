Introduction & Overview

Apayao's Hidden Waterfall Gem

Puting Bato Falls (White Rock Falls) is located in Luna, Apayao — one of the most remote and least-visited provinces in the Cordillera Administrative Region. The falls get their name from the brilliant white limestone rock formations over which the water cascades, creating a striking visual contrast with the surrounding green forest.

The waterfall drops into a wide, clear pool that is perfect for swimming. The white limestone walls and floor of the pool give the water a distinctive turquoise-green color, and the surrounding forest is lush and undisturbed. The area is home to indigenous Isneg (Apayao) communities who serve as guides and stewards of the natural environment.

Apayao is one of the least-visited provinces in the Philippines, which means Puting Bato Falls remains pristine and uncrowded — a genuine off-the-beaten-path discovery for adventurous travelers willing to make the journey.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Puting Bato Falls

Puting Bato Falls is in Luna, Apayao — reached via Kabugao, the Apayao capital, or via Cagayan Valley. The journey requires significant planning.

01

Bus to Tuguegarao or Laoag

Take a bus from Manila to Tuguegarao, Cagayan (~10₱12 hours) or Laoag, Ilocos Norte (~8₱9 hours). Both cities serve as gateways to Apayao from different directions.

Bus to Tuguegarao: ~10₱12 hrs  |  ~₱600–₱900
02

Van to Kabugao, Apayao

From Tuguegarao, take a van to Kabugao, the capital of Apayao (~3₱4 hours). The road passes through the Cagayan Valley and climbs into the Cordillera foothills. Kabugao is the base for exploring Apayao.

Van: ~₱150–₱250  |  — ~3₱4 hrs
03

Arrange Transport to Luna

From Kabugao, hire a 4WD or motorcycle to Luna municipality where Puting Bato Falls is located. Arrange this through your accommodation in Kabugao or through the local tourism office.

4WD/motorcycle: ~₱500–₱1,500  |  Guide required
04

Trek to the Falls

A short trek from the road leads to Puting Bato Falls. A local Isneg guide will lead the way and provide context about the area's natural and cultural significance. Swimming in the pool is the highlight of the visit.

Trek: ~30₱60 min  |  Swimming permitted

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

📌

Best Time to Visit

  • March — May (Dry Season): Best road conditions and clearest water. The white limestone is most vivid when the water level is lower.
  • Avoid rainy season (JuneOctober) — mountain roads in Apayao become extremely difficult and the falls may be flooded.
  • Plan 3 days minimum: The journey from Manila to Apayao and back requires significant time. Budget at least 3 days for the full trip.
  • Register with the Apayao Tourism Office in Kabugao before heading to the falls — they can arrange guides and transport.
📌

Nature & Wildlife

  • Pristine wilderness: Apayao is one of the least-disturbed provinces in the Philippines. The forests around Puting Bato are home to endemic birds, rare plants, and diverse wildlife.
  • Isneg culture: The indigenous Isneg (Apayao) people are the traditional stewards of this land. Respect their customs and ask permission before photographing community members.
  • No littering: Carry out all waste. The pristine condition of Puting Bato Falls depends on visitors treating it with respect.
  • Swimming: The pool at the base of the falls is excellent for swimming. The white limestone floor gives the water a beautiful color.
🎒

What to Pack

  • Cash only — bring enough from Manila or Tuguegarao. There are no ATMs in most of Apayao.
  • All supplies: Food, water, medicine, and toiletries should be brought from Kabugao or Tuguegarao. Options are very limited in remote Apayao.
  • Waterproof bag for your valuables — river crossings may be part of the trek.
  • Combine with Apayao River and Pudtol Hanging Bridge for a complete Apayao adventure.
Mini Itinerary

3 Days in Apayao

Combine Puting Bato Falls with the Apayao River and Pudtol Hanging Bridge for a complete Apayao wilderness adventure.

Day 1
Early AM

Arrive in Kabugao

Arrive in Kabugao, Apayao after the journey from Manila via Tuguegarao. Check in to your accommodation and register with the tourism office. Arrange transport and guides for the next two days.

Afternoon

Explore Kabugao

Explore Kabugao town — the quiet provincial capital of Apayao. Visit the local market, talk to the Isneg community members, and prepare for the next day's adventure.

Day 2
Morning

Puting Bato Falls

Travel to Luna and trek to Puting Bato Falls. Spend the morning swimming in the white limestone pool and exploring the surrounding forest with your Isneg guide.

Afternoon

Apayao River

Visit the Apayao River — one of the most pristine rivers in the Philippines, flowing through the heart of the province. Swimming, kayaking, and river trekking are all possible.

Evening

Pudtol Hanging Bridge

Visit the Pudtol Hanging Bridge — a dramatic suspension bridge over the Apayao River that offers thrilling views of the river gorge below.
