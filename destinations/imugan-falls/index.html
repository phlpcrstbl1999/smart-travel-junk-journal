Introduction

Nueva Vizcaya's Mountain Waterfall

Located in the municipality of Santa Fe, Nueva Vizcaya, Imugan Falls is one of the most beautiful waterfalls in the Caraballo Mountain region. The falls cascade in multiple tiers through dense tropical forest, feeding a series of natural swimming pools with cool, crystal-clear mountain water.

The falls are named after Imugan — a barangay in Santa Fe that sits at the gateway to the Caraballo Mountains. The area is part of the Imugan-Mt. Palali Protected Landscape, a protected area that encompasses the mountain forests, rivers, and biodiversity of this remote corner of Nueva Vizcaya. The trek to the falls passes through traditional Ibaloi and Kalanguya communities, offering a glimpse into the indigenous cultures of the Caraballo highlands.

Imugan Falls is a relatively undiscovered destination — far fewer visitors make the journey here compared to more famous waterfalls in the Philippines. This means the falls and the surrounding forest remain in pristine condition, and visitors can often have the pools entirely to themselves. The combination of the trek, the forest, the indigenous communities, and the falls themselves makes Imugan one of the most rewarding waterfall experiences in northern Luzon.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Imugan Falls

Imugan Falls is located in Santa Fe, Nueva Vizcaya — about 250 km north of Manila. The nearest major town is Bayombong, the capital of Nueva Vizcaya.

01

Bus from Manila to Bayombong

Autobus Transport and Victory Liner operate buses from Cubao or Pasay to Bayombong, Nueva Vizcaya. Travel time is about 6₱7 hours. Buses run throughout the day and overnight.

Travel time: ~6₱7 hours  |  ~₱400–₱600 one-way
02

Bayombong to Santa Fe

From Bayombong, take a jeepney or van to Santa Fe municipality — about 1₱1.5 hours along a scenic mountain road. From Santa Fe town center, hire a habal-habal to Barangay Imugan — the jump-off point for the falls.

Jeepney to Santa Fe: ~1₱1.5 hrs  |  Habal-habal to Imugan: ~30 min
03

Register & Hire a Local Guide

Register at the Barangay Imugan Hall and pay the environmental fee. Hiring a local guide is strongly recommended — the trail to the falls is not well-marked and passes through community land. Guides are available at the barangay hall.

Environmental fee: ~₱30–₱50  |  Guide: ~₱150–₱250
04

Trek to the Falls

The trek to Imugan Falls takes about 1₱2 hours depending on your pace and the trail conditions. The path passes through forest, crosses streams, and climbs through the mountain terrain. Wear trekking shoes and bring plenty of water.

Trek time: ~1₱2 hours  |  Bring at least 1.5L water per person

Essential Visitor Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Visit

🌞

Best Time to Visit

  • November — May (Dry Season): Best time — clear trails, safer river crossings, and the most beautiful pool conditions for swimming.
  • After light rains: The falls are most powerful and dramatic after recent rainfall — the cascades are fuller and the sound is more impressive.
  • Avoid heavy rain periods: Flash floods can make the trail and river crossings dangerous. Check weather forecasts before heading out.
  • Morning starts: Begin the trek early to avoid afternoon rains and to have the falls to yourself before any other visitors arrive.
🧦

Trekking Tips

  • Wear trekking shoes with good grip — the trail involves river crossings and muddy sections. Sandals are not safe.
  • Hire a local guide — the trail is not well-marked and passes through community land. Guides also provide cultural context about the Ibaloi and Kalanguya communities.
  • Bring a change of clothes — you will get wet at the falls. Pack dry clothes in a waterproof bag.
  • Respect the community. The trail passes through indigenous community land — ask permission before photographing residents and follow your guide's instructions.
🎒

What to Pack

  • Trekking shoes with good grip and ankle support.
  • Swimwear — the natural pools are perfect for swimming.
  • Waterproof bag for your phone, camera, and valuables.
  • Insect repellent — the forest trail has mosquitoes, especially in the morning.
  • Packed lunch and water — there are no food stalls near the falls. Bring enough for the full day.
  • Cash — no ATMs in Santa Fe. Withdraw in Bayombong before heading out.
Visitor Guide

How to Experience Imugan Falls

A complete guide to the trek, the falls, and combining it with the best of Nueva Vizcaya.

At the Falls
Step 1

Register & Begin the Trek

Register at the Barangay Imugan Hall, pay your environmental fee, and meet your guide. The trek begins through the barangay and into the forest. Your guide will introduce you to the community and the landscape as you walk.

Step 2

Forest Trek & River Crossings

The trail winds through dense tropical forest, crossing streams and climbing through the mountain terrain. The forest is rich with birdlife and the sound of running water grows louder as you approach the falls.

Step 3

First Tier — The Main Falls

Arrive at the first and most impressive tier of Imugan Falls. The cascade drops into a wide, deep pool of crystal-clear mountain water. Swim, float, and take in the surrounding jungle. The sound of the falls and the cool water are deeply refreshing after the trek.

Step 4

Upper Tiers

With your guide, climb to the upper tiers of the falls. Each tier has its own pool and cascade — some are accessible by scrambling up the rocks beside the falls. The upper tiers are quieter and more intimate than the main pool below.

Nueva Vizcaya
Nearby

Capisaan Cave System

One of the longest cave systems in Luzon — multiple chambers, underground rivers, and dramatic formations. About 1₱2 hours from Santa Fe via Kasibu. A perfect multi-day Nueva Vizcaya adventure.

In Bayombong

St. Dominic Cathedral

The historic cathedral in Bayombong — a beautiful Spanish colonial church in the heart of the provincial capital. Worth visiting before or after your mountain adventures.

In Bayombong

Explore Bayombong City

The capital of Nueva Vizcaya is a pleasant highland city with good food, accommodation, and a relaxed atmosphere. The Bayombong Public Market is excellent for local produce and Ibaloi crafts.
