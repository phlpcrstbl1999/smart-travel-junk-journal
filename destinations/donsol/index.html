Introduction & Overview

The Whale Shark Capital of the World

Donsol is a municipality in Sorsogon at the southern tip of Luzon, where the Donsol River meets the sea. It gained international fame in 1998 when WWF-Philippines documented the world's largest known aggregation of whale sharks (butanding) in its waters — transforming a quiet fishing town into one of the most celebrated wildlife destinations in Asia.

Unlike feeding programs that attract whale sharks with bait, Donsol's encounters are entirely wild and natural. The whale sharks come to feed on plankton blooms in the bay, and visitors snorkel alongside them in open water with strict no-touch, no-flash guidelines enforced by trained Butanding Interaction Officers (BIOs). It is widely regarded as the most ethical whale shark encounter experience in the world.

The season runs from November to June, with peak sightings from January to May. Donsol also offers firefly watching on the Donsol River — one of the most magical natural spectacles in the Philippines.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Donsol

Donsol is in Sorsogon at the southern tip of Luzon — about 600 km from Manila. It is reached by bus or by flying to Legazpi City and driving south.

01

Fly to Legazpi City

Fly from Manila to Legazpi Airport in Albay (~1 hour). From the airport, take a bus or van south to Sorsogon City (~2 hours), then another van to Donsol (~45 minutes).

Flight: ~1 hr  |  Legazpi to Donsol: ~3 hrs total
02

Alternatively: Bus from Manila

Take an overnight bus from Manila to Sorsogon City (~10₱12 hours). Philtranco and DLTB Co. operate this route. From Sorsogon City, take a van to Donsol (~45 minutes).

Bus: ~10₱12 hrs  |  ~₱700–₱1,000
03

Register at the Donsol Tourism Office

All whale shark interaction participants must register at the Donsol Tourism Office and attend a mandatory orientation. BIOs (Butanding Interaction Officers) are assigned to each boat. Registration opens early — arrive by 6:00 AM during peak season.

Registration: ~₱300–₱500  |  — Arrive early
04

Whale Shark Interaction Tour

Board a bangka with your BIO and head into Donsol Bay. The BIO spots whale sharks from the bow and signals when to enter the water. Encounters are in open water — no cages, no feeding, no touching. Pure wild interaction.

Boat hire: ~₱2,000₱3,000  |  — Half-day tour

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

🌞

Best Time for Whale Sharks

  • January — May (Peak Season): Highest concentration of whale sharks in Donsol Bay. Book accommodation well in advance.
  • November — June (Full Season): Whale sharks are present throughout this period, with numbers peaking mid-season.
  • Arrive early: Registration at the tourism office opens at 6:00 AM. Boats depart in the morning when seas are calmest.
  • Sightings not guaranteed — whale sharks are wild animals. However, Donsol has one of the highest encounter rates in the world.
🌿

Responsible Interaction Rules

  • No touching: Maintain a minimum 3-meter distance from the body and 4 meters from the tail at all times.
  • No flash photography — flash startles and stresses the animals. Use natural light settings.
  • No sunscreen before entering the water — use reef-safe alternatives only.
  • Follow your BIO's instructions at all times. BIOs are trained and certified — their word is final in the water.
✨

Fireflies & More

  • Firefly watching on the Donsol River: One of the most magical natural experiences in the Philippines · thousands of fireflies illuminate the mangrove trees after dark. Book a river tour for the evening after your whale shark encounter.
  • Combine with Lake Bulusan: The volcanic crater lake is about 1.5 hours from Donsol — a beautiful addition to a Sorsogon itinerary.
  • Subic Beach Sorsogon is also nearby — a pristine white sand beach perfect for relaxing after your wildlife encounters.
Mini Itinerary

2 Days in Sorsogon

Combine Donsol whale sharks with Lake Bulusan and Subic Beach for the perfect Sorsogon adventure.

Day 1 — Donsol
5:30 AM

Register at Tourism Office

Arrive early at the Donsol Tourism Office to register and attend the mandatory orientation. Boats depart as soon as registration is complete and conditions are assessed.

7:00 AM

Whale Shark Interaction

Head into Donsol Bay with your BIO. Spend the morning searching for and swimming alongside whale sharks in open water. The experience of being in the ocean with these gentle giants is unforgettable.

Evening

Firefly Watching on the Donsol River

After dinner, board a small boat for the firefly watching tour on the Donsol River. As darkness falls, the mangrove trees light up with thousands of synchronously flashing fireflies — one of the most magical sights in the Philippines.

Day 2 — Sorsogon
Morning

Lake Bulusan

Drive to Lake Bulusan in Bulusan Volcano Natural Park (~1.5 hours). The serene volcanic crater lake surrounded by lush rainforest is one of the most beautiful natural settings in Bicol.

Afternoon

Subic Beach Sorsogon

Head to Subic Beach in Matnog for the afternoon — a pristine white sand beach with crystal-clear water, perfect for swimming and relaxing after two days of adventure.

Evening

Return to Sorsogon City

Return to Sorsogon City for dinner and your onward journey. Try pili nut products — Sorsogon is famous for its pili nuts, and the local sweets and pastries make excellent pasalubong.
