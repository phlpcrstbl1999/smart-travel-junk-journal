Introduction

Tarlac's Hilltop Spiritual Sanctuary

Perched on a hill in San Jose, Tarlac, the Monasterio de Tarlac is a Benedictine monastery that has become one of the most visited pilgrimage sites in Central Luzon. The monastery is home to a relic of the True Cross — a fragment of the cross on which Jesus Christ was crucified, according to Catholic tradition — making it a site of profound spiritual significance for Filipino Catholics.

The monastery complex features a beautiful chapel, a towering statue of the Risen Christ that is visible from the surrounding countryside, and landscaped grounds with the Stations of the Cross along a winding path up the hill. The hilltop location provides panoramic views of the Tarlac countryside — rice fields, mountains, and the distant skyline of Tarlac City stretching to the horizon.

The Monasterio de Tarlac is both a pilgrimage destination and a tourist attraction — drawing devout Catholics who come to venerate the relic and pray, as well as visitors who come for the architecture, the views, and the peaceful atmosphere of the hilltop setting. The monastery is particularly popular during Holy Week, when thousands of pilgrims make the journey to venerate the relic.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Monasterio de Tarlac

Monasterio de Tarlac is located in San Jose, Tarlac — about 120 km north of Manila. It's about 30₱40 minutes from Tarlac City.

01

Bus from Manila to Tarlac City

Victory Liner, Genesis Transport, and other bus lines operate services from Cubao or Pasay to Tarlac City. Travel time is about 2₱2.5 hours via NLEX and SCTEX.

Travel time: ~2₱2.5 hours  |  ~₱150–₱220 one-way
02

Tarlac City to San Jose

From Tarlac City, take a jeepney or van to San Jose municipality — about 30₱40 minutes. From San Jose town center, hire a tricycle to the Monasterio de Tarlac. The monastery is visible from the road — the hilltop statue of the Risen Christ is unmistakable.

Jeepney to San Jose: ~30₱40 min  |  Tricycle to monastery: ~₱30–₱50
03

Drive from Manila

Driving from Manila via NLEXSCTEX takes about 1.5₱2 hours. Exit at the Tarlac interchange and follow signs to San Jose and the Monasterio de Tarlac. Parking is available at the monastery grounds.

Drive: ~1.5₱2 hours  |  Parking available at the monastery
04

Arrive at the Monastery

Entry to the monastery grounds is free. The chapel, the Stations of the Cross path, and the hilltop viewpoint are all accessible to visitors. Dress modestly — the monastery is an active religious community. Open daily from early morning to evening.

Free entry  |  Open daily ~6AM₱6PM  |  Dress modestly

Essential Visitor Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Visit

🌞

Best Time to Visit

  • Holy Week: The most spiritually significant time to visit — thousands of pilgrims come to venerate the relic of the True Cross. Book accommodation well in advance.
  • Early Morning (6:00₱9:00 AM): The monastery is peaceful and the morning light on the hilltop is beautiful. Fewer visitors than midday.
  • Late Afternoon (4:00₱6:00 PM): The golden hour light on the Risen Christ statue and the panoramic views of the countryside are spectacular.
  • Year-round: The monastery is open daily and accessible throughout the year.
📌

What to See & Do

  • The Chapel: Visit the chapel to see the relic of the True Cross and pray. The interior is simple but beautiful — a fitting setting for the sacred relic.
  • Stations of the Cross: Walk the Stations of the Cross path up the hill — a meditative and physically engaging spiritual exercise with views opening up at each station.
  • The Risen Christ Statue: The towering statue at the hilltop is the most iconic image of the Monasterio de Tarlac — visible from kilometers away.
  • Panoramic Views: From the hilltop, enjoy sweeping views of the Tarlac countryside — rice fields, mountains, and the distant city skyline.
📌

Etiquette & Practical Tips

  • Dress modestly. The monastery is an active religious community — cover shoulders and knees. Bring a shawl or scarf if needed.
  • Be quiet and respectful inside the chapel and near the relic. This is a sacred site for many Filipino Catholics.
  • The Stations of the Cross path involves climbing — wear comfortable shoes with good grip.
  • Bring water — the hilltop can be hot and exposed. Stay hydrated during the Stations of the Cross walk.
Visitor Guide

How to Experience Monasterio de Tarlac

A complete guide to the monastery and a suggested Tarlac day tour.

At the Monastery
Step 1

The Chapel & Relic

Begin at the chapel to see the relic of the True Cross. The chapel is simple and serene — a fitting setting for quiet prayer and reflection. The relic is displayed in a reliquary and is accessible to visitors for veneration.

Step 2

Stations of the Cross

Walk the Stations of the Cross path up the hill. The path winds through the monastery grounds, with each station marked by a sculpture or relief. The climb takes about 20₱30 minutes and offers increasingly dramatic views of the countryside.

Step 3

The Risen Christ Statue

Reach the hilltop and stand at the base of the Risen Christ statue. The statue is impressive up close — and the panoramic view from the hilltop is one of the best in Tarlac province.

Step 4

Panoramic Views & Reflection

Spend time at the hilltop taking in the views and reflecting. The combination of the spiritual significance of the site and the natural beauty of the setting creates a deeply peaceful and restorative atmosphere.

Tarlac Day Tour
Morning

Capas National Shrine

Start your Tarlac day at the Death March memorial — museum, memorial wall, and obelisk. A solemn and historically significant start to the day.

Midday

Monasterio de Tarlac

Chapel visit, Stations of the Cross, Risen Christ statue, and panoramic views. Allow 1.5₱2 hours for a thorough and peaceful visit.

Afternoon

Sumino Farm

End your day at Sumino Farm — a relaxing agri-tourism destination with farm activities and fresh produce. A perfect contrast to the morning's solemn and spiritual experiences.
