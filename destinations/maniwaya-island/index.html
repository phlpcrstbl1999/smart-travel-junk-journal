Introduction & About

The Island That Time Forgot

Tucked off the coast of Santa Cruz in Marinduque, Maniwaya Island is one of the most beautiful and least-visited islands in the Philippines. Its beaches rival those of Palawan and Boracay — but without the crowds, the resorts, or the noise.

The island is surrounded by healthy coral reefs teeming with marine life, making it a superb destination for snorkeling and freediving. The main beach stretches for hundreds of meters of fine white sand, with shallow, calm waters perfect for swimming. At low tide, a sandbar emerges connecting parts of the island — a magical natural phenomenon.

Maniwaya is part of the Three Islands group in Santa Cruz, along with Polo Island and Elephant Island. A boat tour of all three in a single day is one of Marinduque's most popular activities — and one of the most rewarding.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Maniwaya Island

Maniwaya Island is reached via Santa Cruz, Marinduque. The journey combines a bus, a ferry, and a short boat ride — all part of the adventure.

01

Bus to Lucena, then Ferry to Marinduque

From Manila, take a bus to Lucena City (~3₱4 hours), then a ferry from Dalahican Port to Balanacan Port in Marinduque (~3 hours). Ferries run several times daily.

Bus: ~3₱4 hrs  |  Ferry: ~3 hrs  |  ~₱400–₱650 total
02

Van or Jeepney to Santa Cruz

From Balanacan Port, take a van or jeepney to Santa Cruz — the jump-off point for Maniwaya Island. The ride takes about 45 minutes to 1 hour along the scenic coastal road.

Van to Santa Cruz: ~45₱60 min  |  ~₱60–₱100
03

Boat from Santa Cruz to Maniwaya

From the Santa Cruz port, hire a bangka (outrigger boat) to Maniwaya Island. The crossing takes about 20₱30 minutes. You can hire a boat for the whole day to visit all three islands.

Boat: ~20₱30 min  |  ~₱800–₱1,200 for a full-day boat
04

Alternatively: Fly to Marinduque

Fly from Manila to Marinduque Airport in Gasan (~45 min), then take a van to Santa Cruz (~30 min) and a boat to Maniwaya. This cuts total travel time significantly.

Flight: ~45 min  |  Van + boat: ~1 hr total

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

🌞

Best Time to Visit

  • November — May (Dry Season): Best time — calm seas, clear water, and perfect beach weather. Ideal for snorkeling and island hopping.
  • March — April: Combine your beach trip with the Moriones Festival in Boac for a complete Marinduque experience.
  • Avoid June — October: Typhoon season brings rough seas and suspended boat services. Not ideal for island visits.
  • Weekdays: Visit on weekdays to have the beach almost entirely to yourself.
🎒

What to Pack

  • Snorkeling gear — bring your own mask and fins for the best experience. Rentals are available but limited.
  • Reef-safe sunscreen — protect the coral reefs. Regular sunscreen is harmful to marine ecosystems.
  • Cash — there are no ATMs on the island. Bring enough for food, boat hire, and entrance fees.
  • Food and water — options on the island are very limited. Pack snacks and extra drinking water.
  • Dry bag — to protect your phone and valuables during the boat ride.
📌

Island Tips

  • Book a full-day boat to visit all three islands — Maniwaya, Polo, and Elephant Island — in one trip. Worth every peso.
  • Arrive early. The sandbar at low tide is one of the island's highlights — check tide times before you go.
  • Pack out your trash. Maniwaya is pristine — help keep it that way. Leave no trace.
  • Stay overnight if possible. A few basic cottages are available on the island — waking up to that beach is unforgettable.
Mini Itinerary

How to Spend a Day on Maniwaya Island

A full day is enough to experience the best of Maniwaya and the Three Islands. Start early to make the most of the calm morning waters.

Morning
7:00 AM

Depart Santa Cruz by Boat

Hire a bangka from the Santa Cruz port early in the morning. The sea is calmest in the morning, and you'll arrive at Maniwaya with the whole beach to yourself before day-trippers arrive.

7:30 AM

Arrive at Maniwaya Beach

Set up on the main beach and take in the view — white sand, turquoise water, and the surrounding islands in the distance. If the tide is low, walk out to the sandbar that emerges between the islands.

9:00 AM

Snorkeling the Coral Reef

The reef just off Maniwaya's shore is one of the healthiest in Marinduque. Snorkel among colorful fish, sea turtles, and vibrant coral formations. The water is clear and shallow — perfect for beginners.

Afternoon
11:30 AM

Lunch on the Island

Enjoy a simple lunch of grilled fish and rice at one of the small cottages on the island. Fresh catch cooked over charcoal, eaten with your feet in the sand — it doesn't get better than this.

1:00 PM

Island Hop to Polo & Elephant Islands

Ask your boatman to take you to Polo Island and Elephant Island — the other two of the Three Islands. Each has its own character: Polo for its lagoon, Elephant for its dramatic rock formations.

4:00 PM

Return to Santa Cruz

Head back to Santa Cruz before sunset. The late afternoon light on the water during the return boat ride is stunning. From Santa Cruz, take a van back to Boac or your accommodation.
