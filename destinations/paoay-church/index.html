Introduction & History

A Baroque Masterpiece in Coral Stone

Built between 1694 and 1710 by Augustinian friars, the Saint Augustine Church of Paoay (also known as San Agustin Church) is one of the most architecturally significant churches in the Philippines. Its massive 24 coral-stone buttresses — some rising over 7 meters high — were designed to withstand the region's frequent earthquakes, giving birth to the unique "Earthquake Baroque" architectural style.

The church is a stunning fusion of Baroque, Gothic, and Oriental influences — European design adapted to local materials and seismic realities. The walls are made of coral stone and brick, held together by a mortar of sand, lime, and molasses. The bell tower, standing 26 meters tall, is detached from the main structure — a deliberate design choice to prevent collapse during earthquakes.

In 1993, Paoay Church was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site as part of the "Baroque Churches of the Philippines" collection. Today, it remains an active parish church, hosting daily masses and serving the local community — a living monument to faith, resilience, and Filipino craftsmanship.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Paoay Church

Paoay Church is located in Paoay, Ilocos Norte, about 490 km north of Manila. It's just 20 km from Laoag City — the capital of Ilocos Norte.

01

Fly Manila to Laoag

The fastest route is to fly from NAIA or Clark Airport to Laoag International Airport. Philippine Airlines and Cebu Pacific operate daily flights. From Laoag, Paoay is just a 20-minute tricycle ride away.

Flight: ~1 hour  |  ~₱1,500₱3,500 one-way
02

Take an Overnight Bus from Manila

Partas, Farinas, and Florida Bus Lines run overnight buses from Cubao or Pasay to Laoag City. Buses depart around 9₱10 PM and arrive in Laoag by 7₱8 AM. Book a deluxe seat for comfort.

Travel time: ~10₱12 hours  |  ~₱800–₱1,200 one-way
03

Laoag to Paoay Church

From Laoag City, take a tricycle or jeepney to Paoay town. The church is right in the town center — impossible to miss. You can also rent a tricycle for the day to visit the church, sand dunes, and Lake Paoay in one trip.

Tricycle: ~₱150–₱200  |  — ~20₱25 minutes
04

Arrive at Paoay Church

The church is located at Brgy. Nalasin, Paoay, right along the main road. There's a small parking area and a visitor center nearby. Entry is free, but donations are appreciated to support church maintenance.

Free entry  |  Open daily 6:00 AM — 6:00 PM

Essential Visitor Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Visit

🌞

Best Time to Visit

  • Early Morning (6:00₱8:00 AM): Soft golden light, fewer tourists, and peaceful atmosphere. Perfect for photography and quiet reflection.
  • Late Afternoon (4:00₱6:00 PM): Warm sunset light bathes the coral-stone facade in amber tones. The buttresses cast dramatic shadows.
  • Avoid Midday (11:00 AM₱2:00 PM): Harsh overhead light and extreme heat. The church has no air conditioning inside.
  • Special Events: Visit during the Paoay Town Fiesta (May 4) or Christmas season for festive decorations and cultural performances.
📷

Photography Tips

  • Bring a wide-angle lens — the facade is massive and hard to capture in one frame up close.
  • Shoot from the side to capture the buttresses in perspective — they're the church's most iconic feature.
  • Golden hour is magic — the coral stone glows warm orange at sunrise and sunset.
  • Respect mass times — photography inside is allowed, but not during services. Check the schedule at the entrance.
  • Drone photography requires a permit — ask at the parish office in advance.
📌

Etiquette & Respect

  • This is an active church. Dress modestly — no sleeveless tops, shorts, or mini-skirts. Bring a shawl or scarf to cover shoulders.
  • Silence inside the church. Speak softly and turn off phone ringers. This is a place of worship, not just a tourist site.
  • No flash photography during mass. Be mindful of worshippers and ceremonies in progress.
  • Donations are appreciated. Drop a few pesos in the donation box to support church maintenance and restoration.
  • Don't climb on the buttresses or touch the coral-stone walls — they're fragile and centuries old.
Visitor Guide

How to Experience Paoay Church

A complete guide to exploring the church, understanding its architecture, and visiting nearby heritage sites in one day.

Arrival
Step 1

Approach from the Front Plaza

Walk through the open plaza in front of the church. Take in the full view of the facade — the massive buttresses, the bell tower standing separately to the left, and the intricate coral-stone carvings above the entrance.

Step 2

Study the Buttresses

Walk along the sides of the church to see the 24 buttresses up close. Each one is unique — some are pyramid-shaped, others cylindrical. Notice how they lean slightly outward to brace the walls against earthquakes.

Step 3

Enter the Church Interior

Step inside to see the simple, austere interior — whitewashed walls, wooden pews, and a gilded altar. The contrast between the ornate exterior and minimalist interior is striking. Look up at the wooden ceiling beams.

Step 4

Visit the Bell Tower

Walk to the detached bell tower on the left side of the church. It stands 26 meters tall and was built separately to prevent collapse during earthquakes. Climb to the top if it's open for a view of the town and surrounding fields.

Step 5

Explore the Museum

Visit the small Paoay Church Museum inside the convent building next to the church. It houses religious artifacts, old photographs, and historical documents about the church's construction and restoration.

Nearby Sites
10 min away

Lake Paoay (Paoay Lake)

A serene freshwater lake just 2 km from the church. Perfect for a quiet walk, sunset viewing, or a picnic. Local fishermen bring in their catch here every evening.

15 min away

Paoay Sand Dunes

Just 5 km from the church — a vast desert landscape of golden sand dunes. Book a 4x4 ride or try sandboarding. The contrast of a 300-year-old church and a desert in one town is surreal.

25 min away

Malacaang of the North

The former residence of President Ferdinand Marcos, now a museum. Located along the shores of Paoay Lake. Exhibits include personal belongings, vintage cars, and historical photos.

30 min away

Laoag City Heritage Tour

Visit the Sinking Bell Tower, Saint William Cathedral, and the Tobacco Monopoly Monument. Try the famous Laoag empanada at the Empanada Park.
