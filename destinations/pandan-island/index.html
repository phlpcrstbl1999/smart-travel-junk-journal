Introduction

A Diver's Island Sanctuary

Pandan Island is a small, privately managed dive resort island off the coast of San Jose, Occidental Mindoro — one of the most celebrated dive destinations in the Philippines. The island is home to a single eco-resort that has been operating since the 1980s, maintaining the island's pristine condition and the health of its surrounding reefs through strict conservation practices.

The diving around Pandan Island is exceptional — over 20 dive sites within easy boat distance, ranging from shallow coral gardens teeming with reef fish to dramatic wall dives that plunge into the deep blue of the Sulu Sea. The visibility is consistently excellent — often exceeding 30 meters — and the marine life is extraordinary: sea turtles, reef sharks, barracuda, Napoleon wrasse, and dense schools of tropical fish are regular sightings.

What makes Pandan Island special is its exclusivity and conservation ethic. The resort limits the number of guests to protect the reef, there are no day-trippers, and the dive guides are knowledgeable and passionate about marine conservation. The island itself is beautiful — white sand beaches, coconut palms, and the kind of peaceful, unhurried atmosphere that serious divers seek. It is also the closest resort island to Apo Reef — liveaboard trips to the reef can be arranged from here.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Pandan Island

Pandan Island is accessed from San Jose, Occidental Mindoro — reached by flying from Manila or by ferry from Batangas.

01

Fly Manila to San Jose, Mindoro

Fly from Manila (NAIA) to San Jose Airport, Occidental Mindoro — about 1 hour. Cebu Pacific and other carriers operate this route. From San Jose Airport, the resort arranges transfers to the island by boat.

Manila to San Jose: ~1 hour  |  Resort boat transfer arranged on booking
02

Ferry from Batangas to San Jose

Take an overnight RORO ferry from Batangas City Port to San Jose, Occidental Mindoro. The crossing takes about 10₱12 hours. A budget-friendly option for those with more time.

Batangas to San Jose: ~10₱12 hours  |  ~₱600–₱900  |  Overnight
03

Book the Resort in Advance

Pandan Island has a single resort with limited rooms — book well in advance, especially for peak season (NovemberMay). The resort includes accommodation, meals, and diving in its packages. Contact the resort directly for current rates and availability.

Book directly with the resort  |  All-inclusive packages available
04

Apo Reef Day Trip

Pandan Island is the closest resort to Apo Reef — the resort can arrange day trips or overnight liveaboard trips to the reef. Combining a Pandan Island stay with an Apo Reef trip is the ultimate Occidental Mindoro diving experience.

Apo Reef day trip: ~2₱3 hours by boat  |  Liveaboard also available

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Dive

📌

Best Time to Visit

  • November — May: The dry season brings calm seas, excellent visibility, and the best diving conditions. MarchMay is peak season — book months in advance.
  • Whale shark season: Whale sharks are occasionally sighted around Pandan Island and Apo Reef, particularly between February and April.
  • Avoid JuneOctober: The southwest monsoon brings rough seas and reduced visibility. The resort may close or limit operations during the worst of the monsoon season.
  • Minimum stay: Most guests stay 3₱5 nights to make the most of the diving. A minimum stay may be required — check with the resort when booking.
📌

Top Dive Sites

  • Pandan Wall: The island's signature dive — a dramatic wall that drops from 5 meters to beyond 40 meters, covered in sea fans, black coral, and sponges. Regular sightings of turtles and reef sharks.
  • Coral Garden: A shallow, pristine coral garden ideal for beginners and snorkelers — dense hard and soft coral formations with an extraordinary diversity of reef fish.
  • Shark Point: A cleaning station where grey reef sharks and whitetip sharks congregate — one of the most reliable shark dive sites in Occidental Mindoro.
  • Apo Reef (day trip): The ultimate dive — arrange a day trip or liveaboard to the world's second largest coral reef, just 2₱3 hours by boat from Pandan Island.
📌

Island Life

  • No day-trippers: Pandan Island is a resort-only destination — only guests of the resort are permitted on the island. This keeps the beaches and reefs pristine and the atmosphere peaceful.
  • All-inclusive: The resort typically includes accommodation, meals, and diving in its packages — a convenient and good-value option for dedicated dive trips.
  • Non-divers welcome: The island's beaches, snorkeling, kayaking, and island walks make it a great destination even for non-divers accompanying diving partners.
  • Limited connectivity: Mobile signal and internet are limited on the island — embrace the digital detox. This is part of the appeal.
Mini Itinerary

How to Spend 4 Days at Pandan Island

Four days at Pandan Island — diving the local sites, a day trip to Apo Reef, and the peaceful rhythm of island life — is the perfect Occidental Mindoro dive escape.

Days 1₱2
Day 1

Arrive & Orientation Dive

Fly to San Jose and transfer to the island. After settling in, do an orientation dive on the Coral Garden — a gentle introduction to the island's marine life and a chance to check your buoyancy before the deeper dives.

Day 2

Pandan Wall & Shark Point

Two dives on the island's signature sites — the dramatic Pandan Wall in the morning and Shark Point in the afternoon. Evening: fresh seafood dinner on the beach as the sun sets over the Sulu Sea.

Days 3₱4
Day 3

Apo Reef Day Trip

The highlight of the trip — an early departure for the 2₱3 hour boat ride to Apo Reef. Two or three dives on the outer wall and coral gardens of the world's second largest reef. Return to Pandan Island by evening.

Day 4

Morning Dive & Departure

One final morning dive before checkout — the Coral Garden at dawn, when the reef is at its most active. Transfer back to San Jose for the flight to Manila.
