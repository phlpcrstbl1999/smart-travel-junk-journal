Introduction & History

Abra's Augustinian Heritage Gem

The Tayum Church (Saint Augustine Parish) in Tayum, Abra is one of the oldest churches in the Cordillera Administrative Region, established by Augustinian missionaries during the Spanish colonial period. The church stands as a testament to the early evangelization of the Ilocano and Tingguian peoples of Abra.

The church's architecture features the thick stone walls, bell tower, and colonial facade typical of Augustinian churches in the Ilocos and Cordillera regions. The structure has survived centuries of earthquakes, typhoons, and the passage of time, and continues to serve as an active parish church for the community of Tayum.

Tayum is located just 10 km from Bangued, making it an easy and rewarding addition to any Abra heritage itinerary. The church plaza is peaceful and well-maintained, and the surrounding town of Tayum retains a quiet, unhurried provincial character.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Tayum Church

Tayum Church is in Tayum, Abra — just 10 km from Bangued city. It is best visited as part of a Bangued heritage day trip.

01

Bus from Manila to Bangued

Take a bus from Manila to Bangued, Abra (~8₱9 hours). Bangued is the base for exploring Abra's heritage sites including Tayum Church.

Bus: ~8₱9 hrs  |  ~₱500–₱700
02

Tricycle or Jeepney to Tayum

From Bangued, take a tricycle or jeepney to Tayum (~10 km, ~20 minutes). The road between Bangued and Tayum is well-paved and passes through flat Abra valley farmland.

Tricycle: ~₱50–₱80  |  Jeepney: ~₱15–₱20
03

Explore the Church & Plaza

The Tayum Church is located in the town center facing the plaza. Walk around the exterior to appreciate the colonial stonework, then step inside the church. The plaza is quiet and peaceful.

Free entry  |  — Allow 30₱60 minutes
04

Combine with Bangued Cathedral

Visit Bangued Cathedral on the same day — the two churches are just 10 km apart and make a natural heritage pair. Use Bangued as your base and visit Tayum as a half-day excursion.

Bangued + Tayum = perfect Abra heritage day

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

📌

Best Time to Visit

  • Feast of Saint Augustine (August 28): The church's patron feast day — a community celebration with processions and festivities in the Tayum plaza.
  • March — May (Dry Season): Best weather for exploring Abra's heritage sites.
  • Combine with Bangued: Visit Tayum Church and Bangued Cathedral on the same day — both are within 10 km of each other.
  • Any time of year is suitable — the church is open daily and the town is always welcoming to visitors.
—

Visiting the Church

  • Dress modestly — cover shoulders and knees when entering the church interior.
  • The bell tower is one of the most photogenic elements of the church — best photographed from the plaza in the morning light.
  • Talk to the locals: The people of Tayum are proud of their church and happy to share its history. The parish priest or local elders can provide fascinating historical context.
  • Photography of the exterior is freely permitted. Ask permission before photographing inside during services.
📌

Planning Your Abra Trip

  • Bangued as base: Stay in Bangued and visit Tayum Church as a half-day excursion. Bangued has the best accommodation options in Abra.
  • Kaparkan Falls: The extraordinary travertine waterfall in Tineg is the main reason most travelers visit Abra. Combine it with the heritage churches for a complete Abra experience.
  • Ilocano food: Abra shares the Ilocano food culture — try bagnet, pinakbet, and empanada at local restaurants in Bangued.
  • Tingguian weaving: Look for traditional Tingguian woven textiles at the Bangued market — beautiful and unique souvenirs from Abra.
Mini Itinerary

Abra Heritage Day

Combine Tayum Church with Bangued Cathedral and the Abra River for a perfect heritage day in the Cordillera foothills.

Morning
8:00 AM

Bangued Cathedral

Start at Bangued Cathedral in the morning. Explore the church and the city plaza, then have breakfast at a local eatery near the market.

10:00 AM

Tayum Church

Take a tricycle to Tayum Church (~20 minutes). Explore the colonial church and its peaceful plaza. The morning light on the stone facade is beautiful for photography.

11:30 AM

Tayum Town Exploration

Walk around Tayum town and talk to the locals. The town has a quiet, authentic provincial character that is increasingly rare in the Philippines.

Afternoon
1:00 PM

Lunch in Bangued

Return to Bangued for lunch. Try bagnet and pinakbet at a local Ilocano restaurant near the city plaza.

3:00 PM

Bangued Market & River

Browse the Bangued public market for Tingguian handicrafts and local produce, then walk along the Abra River in the late afternoon.

Evening

Prepare for Kaparkan

If continuing to Kaparkan Falls, arrange your 4WD transport for the next morning. The Kaparkan trip starts at dawn from Bangued.
