Introduction

Nueva Ecija's Mountain Waterfall

Located in the municipality of Gabaldon, Nueva Ecija, Gabaldon Falls is a series of tiered waterfalls in the foothills of the Sierra Madre mountain range. The falls cascade through dense tropical forest, feeding a series of natural swimming pools with cool, clear mountain water — a perfect contrast to the heat of the Nueva Ecija lowlands.

Gabaldon municipality sits at the eastern edge of Nueva Ecija, where the flat rice fields give way to the rugged terrain of the Sierra Madre. The falls are fed by mountain streams that flow year-round, ensuring a consistent water supply even during the dry season. The surrounding forest is part of the Sierra Madre ecosystem — one of the most biodiverse in the Philippines · and the trek to the falls passes through beautiful secondary forest rich in birdlife.

Gabaldon Falls is a relatively undiscovered destination — far fewer visitors make the journey here compared to more famous waterfalls in the Philippines. This means the falls and the surrounding forest remain in excellent condition, and visitors can often have the pools entirely to themselves. It is the perfect afternoon destination after visiting Minalungao National Park and Pantabangan Dam.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Gabaldon Falls

Gabaldon Falls is located in Gabaldon, Nueva Ecija — about 150 km north of Manila. The nearest major city is Cabanatuan City.

01

Bus from Manila to Cabanatuan

Genesis Transport and Baliwag Transit operate buses from Cubao or Pasay to Cabanatuan City. Travel time is about 2.5₱3 hours. Cabanatuan is the main hub for all Nueva Ecija destinations.

Travel time: ~2.5₱3 hours  |  ~₱150–₱250 one-way
02

Cabanatuan to Gabaldon

From Cabanatuan, take a bus or van to Gabaldon municipality — about 1.5₱2 hours along a road that passes through rice fields and into the Sierra Madre foothills. The scenery becomes increasingly beautiful as you approach Gabaldon.

Bus/van to Gabaldon: ~1.5₱2 hours  |  ~₱80–₱120
03

Gabaldon Town to the Falls

From Gabaldon town center, hire a tricycle or habal-habal to the falls jump-off point — about 15₱20 minutes. From the jump-off, a short trek of 20₱30 minutes through the forest leads to the main falls.

Habal-habal to jump-off: ~₱50–₱80  |  Trek to falls: ~20₱30 min
04

Combine with Nueva Ecija Day Tour

Gabaldon Falls is the perfect afternoon destination after visiting Minalungao National Park in the morning and Pantabangan Dam at midday. Having your own vehicle makes the full day tour most convenient.

Combine with Minalungao & Pantabangan Dam  |  Own vehicle recommended

Essential Visitor Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Visit

🌞

Best Time to Visit

  • Year-round: Gabaldon Falls flows throughout the year, fed by mountain springs. Volume varies by season but the falls are always beautiful.
  • November — May (Dry Season): Clearer water, safer trails, and better swimming conditions. The pools are at their most inviting.
  • After moderate rains: The falls are most powerful and dramatic — fuller cascades and more impressive pools.
  • Morning visits: The forest is coolest and the light most beautiful in the morning. Arrive early to have the falls to yourself.
📌

Swimming & Trekking

  • Wear trekking shoes — the trail involves rocky and potentially muddy sections.
  • Bring a change of clothes — you will want to swim in the natural pools. Pack dry clothes in a waterproof bag.
  • Hire a local guide if available — they know the best pools and can show you the upper tiers of the falls.
  • Don't swim alone near the base of the main falls — the current can be strong, especially after rain.
🎒

What to Pack

  • Swimwear — the natural pools are perfect for swimming.
  • Trekking shoes with good grip for the forest trail.
  • Waterproof bag for your phone and valuables.
  • Insect repellent — the forest trail has mosquitoes.
  • Packed lunch and water — no food stalls near the falls.
  • Cash — no ATMs in Gabaldon. Withdraw in Cabanatuan before heading out.
Visitor Guide

How to Experience Gabaldon Falls

A complete guide to the falls and the perfect Nueva Ecija day tour using Gabaldon as your refreshing finale.

At the Falls
Step 1

Forest Trek

Begin the 20₱30 minute trek from the jump-off through the Sierra Madre secondary forest. The trail is pleasant and the forest is rich in birdlife. The sound of the falls grows louder as you approach.

Step 2

Main Falls & Pool

Arrive at the main falls — a beautiful cascade dropping into a wide, clear pool. Swim, float, and enjoy the cool mountain water. The surrounding forest creates a shaded, peaceful atmosphere.

Step 3

Upper Tiers

Explore the upper tiers of the falls with your guide. Each tier has its own pool and cascade — some accessible by scrambling up the rocks beside the falls. The upper tiers are quieter and more intimate.

Step 4

Picnic & Return

Have a picnic beside the falls before beginning the return trek. The cool water, forest sounds, and mountain air make Gabaldon Falls one of the most restorative natural experiences in Nueva Ecija.

Full Nueva Ecija Day
Morning

Minalungao National Park

Start at the limestone gorge — boat tour, swimming, and cliff jumping. The most dramatic natural scenery of the day.

Midday

Pantabangan Dam

Dam viewpoint, reservoir boat tour, and lakeside picnic. The haunting submerged church ruins are unforgettable during low water season.

Afternoon

Gabaldon Falls

End your Nueva Ecija day at the falls — forest trek, swimming, and a peaceful picnic. The perfect refreshing close to a full day of adventure.
