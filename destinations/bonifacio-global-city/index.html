Introduction & Overview

The Philippines' Most Livable Urban District

Bonifacio Global City (BGC) is a 240-hectare planned urban district in Taguig, Metro Manila — built on the former Fort Bonifacio US military base. It is consistently ranked as the most livable and walkable district in the Philippines, with wide tree-lined boulevards, dedicated bike lanes, and a pedestrian-friendly street grid.

BGC is home to the Mind Museum (the Philippines' premier science museum), Bonifacio High Street (an open-air lifestyle strip), the BGC Arts Center, world-class international restaurants, and some of the finest street art in Southeast Asia — with murals by local and international artists covering building facades throughout the district.

The district is also known for its weekend markets — the Mercato Centrale night market and the BGC Weekend Market are among the best food markets in Metro Manila, drawing thousands of visitors every weekend.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

Getting to Bonifacio Global City

BGC is in Taguig, Metro Manila — accessible by bus, taxi, or ride-hailing from most parts of the metro.

01

BGC Bus from Ayala or Makati

The BGC Bus runs from Ayala Avenue in Makati to BGC — a convenient and affordable option. The bus stops at multiple points within BGC including Bonifacio High Street.

BGC Bus: ~₱12–₱15  |  — ~15₱20 min from Ayala
02

Grab or Taxi

Grab (ride-hailing) and taxis are the most convenient options from most parts of Metro Manila. BGC is well-served by Grab — book in advance during peak hours.

Grab/Taxi: ~₱150–₱300 from Makati  |  Book via Grab app
03

Walking Within BGC

BGC is designed for walking — the wide, tree-lined boulevards and pedestrian-friendly streets make it easy to explore on foot. The Mind Museum, Bonifacio High Street, and the BGC Arts Center are all within walking distance of each other.

Walkable district  |  Bike rentals available
04

Weekend Markets

The Mercato Centrale (FridaySunday nights) and BGC Weekend Market (weekends) are the best food markets in Metro Manila. Check schedules and locations before visiting.

Mercato Centrale: FriSun nights  |  Weekend Market: SatSun

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

📌

BGC Highlights

  • The Mind Museum: The Philippines' premier science museum — 250+ interactive exhibits across five galleries. Book tickets in advance online.
  • Bonifacio High Street: The open-air lifestyle strip — restaurants, boutiques, art installations, and the best people-watching in BGC.
  • Street art: BGC has some of the finest street murals in Southeast Asia — walk the streets and look for works by local and international artists.
  • BGC Arts Center: A performing arts venue hosting theater, dance, and music performances — check the schedule for shows during your visit.
🍽

Food & Markets

  • Mercato Centrale: The best night market in Metro Manila — dozens of food stalls serving everything from Filipino classics to international cuisine. Friday to Sunday nights.
  • Bonifacio High Street restaurants: World-class international dining — Japanese, Korean, Italian, American, and Filipino restaurants line the High Street.
  • BGC Weekend Market: A weekend market with local food vendors, crafts, and live music — a great way to experience BGC's community atmosphere.
  • Hole-in-the-wall finds: Explore the side streets off High Street for more affordable local dining options.
📌

Practical Tips

  • Best visited on weekends: BGC is most vibrant on weekends when the markets are running and the streets are full of life.
  • Evening visits: BGC is beautiful at night — the illuminated buildings, the street art, and the outdoor dining scene are at their best after dark.
  • Combine with Makati: BGC and Makati are adjacent — combine both in a single day for the complete modern Manila experience.
  • The Mind Museum: Book tickets online in advance — the museum sells out on weekends.
Mini Itinerary

A Day in BGC

Combine The Mind Museum, Bonifacio High Street, and the Mercato Centrale for the perfect BGC day.

Morning
10:00 AM

The Mind Museum

Start at The Mind Museum when it opens. Allow 2₱3 hours for the five galleries — the Atom Gallery, Life Gallery, Universe Gallery, Earth Gallery, and Technology Gallery.

1:00 PM

Lunch on Bonifacio High Street

Have lunch at one of the many restaurants on Bonifacio High Street. The open-air setting and variety of cuisines make it one of the best lunch spots in Metro Manila.

Afternoon & Evening
3:00 PM

Street Art Walk

Walk the streets of BGC looking for street art murals — the district has some of the finest public art in Southeast Asia. Pick up a street art map from the BGC Arts Center.

7:00 PM

Mercato Centrale

End the day at Mercato Centrale (FridaySunday) — the best night market in Metro Manila. Graze through dozens of food stalls and enjoy the BGC evening atmosphere.
