Introduction

Nueva Vizcaya's Underground Wonder

Located in the municipality of Kasibu, Nueva Vizcaya, the Capisaan Cave System is one of the most significant cave networks in the Philippines. With an explored length of over 8 kilometers, the system consists of multiple interconnected caves featuring dramatic stalactite and stalagmite formations, underground rivers, and vast chambers that have taken millions of years to form.

The cave system sits within the Caraballo Mountain range — the rugged highland region that separates the Cagayan Valley from Central Luzon. The caves were formed by the dissolution of limestone by slightly acidic groundwater over millions of years, creating the extraordinary formations visible today. The underground river that flows through sections of the cave system adds another dimension to the experience — visitors can wade or swim through passages illuminated only by their headlamps.

The Capisaan Cave System is considered one of the longest cave systems in Luzon and remains largely unexplored beyond its accessible sections. Speleologists and adventure cavers continue to map new passages, and the full extent of the system is still unknown. For visitors, the accessible sections offer a genuinely wild and adventurous caving experience far removed from the more developed cave attractions of the Philippines.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Capisaan Cave

The Capisaan Cave System is located in Kasibu, Nueva Vizcaya — about 280 km north of Manila. The nearest major town is Bayombong, the capital of Nueva Vizcaya.

01

Bus from Manila to Bayombong

Autobus Transport and Victory Liner operate buses from Cubao or Pasay to Bayombong, Nueva Vizcaya. Travel time is about 6₱7 hours. Buses run throughout the day and overnight.

Travel time: ~6₱7 hours  |  ~₱400–₱600 one-way
02

Bayombong to Kasibu

From Bayombong, take a jeepney or van to Kasibu municipality — about 30₱45 minutes. From Kasibu town center, hire a habal-habal (motorcycle taxi) or tricycle to the cave entrance. The road to the cave is rough in sections.

Jeepney to Kasibu: ~30₱45 min  |  Habal-habal to cave: ~20₱30 min
03

Register & Hire a Local Guide

Register at the Kasibu Municipal Tourism Office before heading to the cave. A local guide is mandatory — the cave system is complex and dangerous without guidance. Guides are available at the tourism office or at the cave entrance.

Entrance fee: ~₱50–₱100  |  Guide: ~₱200–₱400 (mandatory)
04

Prepare for the Cave

The cave requires wading through water in some sections. Wear old clothes and shoes you don't mind getting wet and muddy. Bring a waterproof bag for your valuables. Headlamps are essential — bring your own or rent at the entrance.

Headlamp rental: ~₱50  |  Wear old clothes and shoes

Essential Visitor Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Visit

🌞

Best Time to Visit

  • November — May (Dry Season): Best time — lower water levels in the underground river make navigation easier and safer.
  • Avoid rainy season (June — October): Heavy rains can cause the underground river to rise rapidly, making the cave dangerous or inaccessible.
  • Morning starts: Begin your cave tour early — the full exploration takes 3₱5 hours and you want to finish before afternoon rains.
  • Weekdays: Fewer visitors and more personal attention from guides. The cave is more atmospheric without crowds.
🔦

Cave Safety

  • Never enter without a guide. The cave system is complex — passages branch in multiple directions and it is easy to get lost without local knowledge.
  • Bring at least two light sources per person — a headlamp plus a backup flashlight. Cave darkness is absolute if your light fails.
  • Wear a helmet — low ceilings and protruding formations are common. Helmets are available for rent at the entrance.
  • Don't touch the formations. Stalactites and stalagmites take thousands of years to form — even touching them can cause irreversible damage.
  • Tell someone your plans before entering — leave your itinerary and expected return time with your accommodation host.
🎒

What to Pack

  • Old clothes and shoes you don't mind getting wet, muddy, and potentially ruined.
  • Waterproof bag or dry bag for your phone, camera, and valuables.
  • Headlamp with fresh batteries — bring a backup light source.
  • Helmet — available for rent at the entrance but bring your own if possible.
  • Water and snacks — the cave tour is physically demanding. Stay hydrated.
  • Cash — no ATMs in Kasibu. Withdraw in Bayombong before heading out.
Visitor Guide

How to Experience Capisaan Cave

A complete guide to exploring the cave system and combining it with the best of Nueva Vizcaya.

Cave Exploration
Step 1

Gear Up & Enter

Put on your helmet, check your headlamp, and secure your valuables in a waterproof bag. Your guide will brief you on safety procedures and the route before entering. The cave entrance is a dramatic opening in the limestone cliff face.

Step 2

The First Chambers

The initial chambers feature impressive stalactite and stalagmite formations. Your guide will point out the most significant formations and explain how they were formed. The scale of the chambers is immediately impressive.

Step 3

The Underground River

Wade through the underground river passages — the water is cool and clear, reaching knee to waist depth in some sections. Your headlamp illuminates the formations above and the river below. This is the most memorable section of the cave.

Step 4

The Deep Chambers

Continue deeper into the system with your guide. The formations become more dramatic — massive columns, delicate cave curtains, and crystal formations that have grown undisturbed for millennia. The silence is profound.

Step 5

Return & Debrief

Return to the entrance with your guide. Clean up at the nearby stream and debrief with your guide about what you saw. Many visitors are surprised by how physically demanding the cave tour is — allow time to rest before the journey back to Bayombong.

Nueva Vizcaya
Nearby

Imugan Falls

A stunning multi-tiered waterfall in the mountains of Nueva Vizcaya. About 1₱2 hours from Kasibu. A perfect complement to the cave experience — cool, clear water after a muddy cave tour.

In Bayombong

St. Dominic Cathedral

The historic cathedral in Bayombong — the capital of Nueva Vizcaya. A beautiful Spanish colonial church worth visiting before or after your cave adventure.

In Bayombong

Nueva Vizcaya State University

The main university of the province, with a pleasant campus and a small museum about Nueva Vizcaya's history and culture. A good stop in Bayombong.
