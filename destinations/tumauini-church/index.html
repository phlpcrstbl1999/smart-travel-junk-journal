Introduction & History

The Church with Terracotta Tile Decorations

Located in the municipality of Tumauini, Isabela, the Saint Matthias Parish Church is one of the most architecturally distinctive churches in the Philippines. Built by Augustinian missionaries and completed in 1783, the church is renowned for its extraordinary facade — decorated with intricate terracotta tile patterns that are unique in Philippine colonial architecture.

The terracotta tiles covering the church facade create a rich, textured surface of geometric and floral patterns that give the building a warmth and character unlike any other colonial church in the country. The tiles were crafted locally by Ibanag artisans, blending Spanish Baroque design with indigenous craftsmanship — a fusion that makes Tumauini Church a truly one-of-a-kind heritage structure.

The church is built from brick and coral stone and features a massive bell tower standing separately from the main structure — a design common in earthquake-prone regions of the Philippines. The interior is simple but dignified, with original wooden altars and religious artwork that have survived centuries of typhoons, earthquakes, and the ravages of World War II. The church is on the UNESCO World Heritage Tentative List as part of the Baroque Churches of the Philippines.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Tumauini Church

Tumauini Church is located in Tumauini, Isabela — about 30 km north of Ilagan City, the capital of Isabela province.

01

Fly or Bus to Cauayan or Tuguegarao

Fly from NAIA Manila to Cauayan Airport (Isabela) — the closest airport, about 1 hour from Tumauini. Alternatively, fly to Tuguegarao (Cagayan) and travel south. Overnight buses from Cubao also serve Ilagan and Cauayan.

Flight to Cauayan: ~1 hour  |  Bus: ~9₱10 hours  |  ₱700–₱3,500
02

Cauayan or Ilagan to Tumauini

From Cauayan City or Ilagan City, take a jeepney or van heading north toward Tumauini municipality. The ride takes about 30₱45 minutes from Ilagan. Tell the driver you're going to the Tumauini Church.

Jeepney from Ilagan: ~30₱45 min  |  ~₱30–₱50
03

Arrive at Tumauini Town Plaza

The church is located right on the Tumauini town plaza — the central square of the municipality. It's impossible to miss. There's a small parking area beside the church and the bell tower is visible from the main road.

Free entry  |  Open daily for visitors and mass
04

Combine with Isabela Heritage Tour

Tumauini Church is best visited as part of an Isabela heritage day tour. Combine with the Ilagan Japanese Tunnel and Magat Dam for a full day of Isabela history and natural scenery.

Combine with Ilagan Tunnel & Magat Dam  |  Full day tour recommended

Essential Visitor Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Visit

🌞

Best Time to Visit

  • Morning (7:00₱10:00 AM): Soft light illuminates the terracotta tile facade beautifully. The church is peaceful before tour groups arrive.
  • Late Afternoon (3:00₱5:00 PM): Warm golden light brings out the rich terracotta tones of the facade. Excellent for photography.
  • November — April (Dry Season): Best weather for exploring Isabela. Clear skies and comfortable temperatures.
  • Feast of Saint Matthias (May 14): The town fiesta — colorful celebrations, processions, and cultural events around the church.
📷

Photography Tips

  • Focus on the terracotta tiles — the intricate patterns on the facade are the church's most unique feature. Get close for detail shots.
  • Shoot from the plaza for the full facade composition — include the bell tower in the frame for context.
  • Golden hour is essential — the warm light brings out the rich terracotta tones in a way that midday light cannot.
  • Interior photography is allowed when mass is not in progress — the wooden altars and ceiling are worth documenting.
📌

Etiquette & Respect

  • This is an active parish church. Dress modestly — cover shoulders and knees. Bring a shawl or scarf if needed.
  • Be quiet inside and respectful of worshippers. Turn off phone ringers before entering.
  • Don't touch the terracotta tiles — they are centuries old and fragile. Even oils from hands can cause damage over time.
  • Donations are appreciated at the church entrance to support maintenance and restoration.
  • Ask the parish office about guided tours — local guides can provide rich historical context about the church's construction and significance.
Visitor Guide

How to Experience Tumauini Church

A complete guide to the church and a suggested Isabela heritage day tour combining the best of the province.

At the Church
Step 1

The Facade — Terracotta Tiles

Stand in the plaza and study the facade. The terracotta tile patterns cover the entire surface — geometric borders, floral motifs, and decorative panels that create a rich, warm texture unlike any other church in the Philippines. Walk close to appreciate the craftsmanship.

Step 2

The Bell Tower

Walk to the detached bell tower beside the church. Like many Philippine colonial churches, the tower stands separately to prevent earthquake damage. Climb if access is permitted for views of the town and surrounding Cagayan Valley.

Step 3

The Interior

Enter the church and explore the interior — original wooden altars, religious paintings, and the simple but dignified nave. The contrast between the ornate exterior and the austere interior is striking and characteristic of Philippine Baroque churches.

Step 4

The Convent & Grounds

Walk around the church grounds and the adjacent convent building. The old stone walls, the shaded courtyard, and the views of the surrounding town give a sense of the church's central role in Tumauini's history and community life.

Isabela Day Tour
Morning

Tumauini Church

Start your Isabela heritage day at Tumauini Church. Explore the facade, interior, and bell tower. Allow 45₱60 minutes for a thorough visit.

Midday

Ilagan Japanese Tunnel

Drive south to Ilagan City and visit the Japanese Tunnel — a World War II underground tunnel system used by Japanese forces during the occupation. A sobering and historically significant site.

Afternoon

Magat Dam

Head west to the Magat Dam — one of the largest dams in the Philippines. The reservoir and surrounding landscape are beautiful, and the dam's engineering scale is impressive. A perfect end to an Isabela heritage day.
