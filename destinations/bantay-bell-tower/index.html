Introduction & History

A 400-Year-Old Watchtower Above Vigan

Built in 1591 by Augustinian friars, the Bantay Bell Tower is one of the oldest and most historically significant structures in the Philippines. Unlike most bell towers that are attached to their churches, the Bantay Bell Tower stands completely detached from the Saint Augustine Church of Bantay — a deliberate design choice that served a dual purpose: a place of worship and a military watchtower.

During the Spanish colonial era, the tower was used to warn the townspeople of approaching pirates and invaders from the sea. Sentinels stationed at the top would ring the bells to signal danger, giving residents time to seek shelter. The tower's elevated position — sitting on a hill overlooking the Abra River and the surrounding plains — made it an ideal vantage point for surveillance.

The tower is built from brick and coral stone, standing approximately 20 meters tall. Its thick walls and narrow windows were designed to withstand both cannon fire and earthquakes. Today, visitors can climb to the top for a 360-degree panoramic view of Vigan City, the Abra River, and the Ilocos Sur countryside — one of the best views in northern Luzon.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Vigan to Bantay Bell Tower

The Bantay Bell Tower is located in Bantay, Ilocos Sur — just 2 km north of Vigan's heritage zone. It's a quick and easy side trip from the city.

01

Get to Vigan First

Bantay is best visited as part of a Vigan trip. Take an overnight bus from Cubao or Pasay (Manila) to Vigan — Partas, Dominion, and Florida Bus Lines operate this route. Travel time is 8₱9 hours.

Manila to Vigan: ~8₱9 hours  |  ~₱700–₱1,100
02

Tricycle from Vigan Heritage Zone

From the Vigan heritage zone or your hotel, hire a tricycle to Bantay. The ride takes about 10 minutes. Ask the driver to take you to Bantay Church and Bell Tower — every driver knows it.

Tricycle: ~₱50–₱80  |  — ~10 minutes
03

Walk from Bantay Church

The bell tower is located just 50 meters from the Saint Augustine Church of Bantay. After visiting the church, walk across the open plaza to reach the tower. The entrance is at the base of the tower.

Walking distance from church: ~50 meters  |  Free entry
04

Combine with a Vigan Day Tour

Most Vigan day tours include a stop at Bantay Bell Tower. You can also rent a bicycle from the heritage zone (₱50–₱80/hour) and cycle to Bantay — a scenic 15-minute ride along the Abra River road.

Bike from Vigan: ~15 minutes  |  Include in Vigan day tour

Essential Visitor Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Visit

🌞

Best Time to Visit

  • Early Morning (6:00₱8:00 AM): The best light for photography — golden hour illuminates the brick tower beautifully. Fewer tourists and cooler temperatures.
  • Late Afternoon (4:00₱6:00 PM): Catch the sunset from the top of the tower. The view of Vigan and the Abra River at golden hour is breathtaking.
  • November — April (Dry Season): Clear skies mean unobstructed panoramic views from the top. Avoid rainy season when the climb can be slippery.
  • Weekday mornings: Avoid weekend afternoons when tour groups arrive. The tower is most peaceful on weekday mornings.
📌

Climbing the Tower

  • The climb is steep and narrow. The interior staircase is original — worn brick steps with low ceilings. Take your time and hold the rope railing.
  • Wear closed-toe shoes with good grip. Sandals and flip-flops are not recommended for the climb.
  • Not recommended for those with vertigo or mobility issues. The top platform has low railings and open views.
  • Bring a camera or phone — the 360 view from the top is one of the best in Ilocos Sur. On clear days, you can see all the way to the South China Sea.
  • The climb takes about 5₱10 minutes depending on your pace. The tower has about 4₱5 floors of stairs.
📌

Etiquette & Respect

  • The bell tower is a National Cultural Treasure. Do not touch, scratch, or deface the walls. The brick and coral stone are centuries old and irreplaceable.
  • Bantay Church is an active parish. If you visit the church alongside the tower, dress modestly and speak softly inside.
  • No littering. The tower grounds are maintained by the local community — respect the space.
  • Ask before photographing locals who may be praying or working near the church grounds.
  • Donations are welcome at the church to support maintenance of both the church and the tower.
Visitor Guide

How to Experience Bantay Bell Tower

A complete guide to exploring the tower, understanding its history, and combining it with nearby Vigan heritage sites for a full day of discovery.

At the Tower
Step 1

Approach & Exterior

Walk around the base of the tower before entering. Notice the thick brick walls, the narrow loophole windows designed for archers, and the weathered coral stone that has survived 400 years of earthquakes and typhoons. The tower's detachment from the church is immediately striking.

Step 2

Enter & Begin the Climb

Enter through the low doorway at the base. The interior is dark and cool — a stark contrast to the heat outside. The original brick staircase winds upward in a tight spiral. Hold the rope railing and take each step carefully — the bricks are worn smooth by centuries of footsteps.

Step 3

The Bell Level

Pause at the bell level to see the original colonial-era bells — still intact after 400 years. Read the inscriptions on the bells and imagine the sentinels who rang them to warn of approaching pirates. The sound of these bells once determined whether the townspeople lived or died.

Step 4

The Top: 360 Panorama

Reach the top platform for a breathtaking 360-degree view. To the west, the Abra River winds through the plains. To the south, Vigan's heritage zone stretches out — you can see the cathedral tower and the red-tiled roofs of the ancestral houses. On clear days, the South China Sea is visible on the horizon.

Step 5

Visit Bantay Church

After descending, walk 50 meters to the Saint Augustine Church of Bantay. Built in the same era as the tower, the church has a simple but beautiful interior. The combination of the church and the detached tower is one of the most photogenic heritage compositions in the Philippines.

Nearby in Vigan
10 min away

Calle Crisologo

The iconic cobblestone street of Vigan — lined with 16th₱19th century ancestral houses. Best visited at dawn or dusk when the gas lamps glow and the crowds are thin.

12 min away

Saint Paul Metropolitan Cathedral

A massive Baroque church built in 1641 in the heart of Vigan. One of the most impressive church facades in the Philippines, adjacent to Plaza Salcedo.

15 min away

Syquia Mansion Museum

The ancestral home of President Elpidio Quirino, now a museum. Well-preserved 19th-century Ilocano-Chinese Mestizo interiors and period furniture.

20 min away

Burnay Pottery Workshops

Watch artisans shape clay using traditional foot-powered wheels in the Pagburnayan district — a 400-year-old craft unique to Vigan. Buy authentic pieces to take home.
