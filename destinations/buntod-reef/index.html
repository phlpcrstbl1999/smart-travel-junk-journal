Introduction & Overview

Masbate's Underwater Garden

The Buntod Reef Marine Sanctuary is a protected marine area located just off the coast of Masbate City, accessible by a short 15₱20 minute boat ride. The sanctuary encompasses a healthy coral reef ecosystem that serves as habitat for hundreds of species of tropical fish, sea turtles, reef sharks, and other marine life.

One of the sanctuary's most distinctive features is its white sandbar — a long, narrow strip of white sand that emerges from the water at low tide, creating a stunning natural formation surrounded by turquoise water. The sandbar is a popular spot for picnics, photography, and simply wading in the shallow, crystal-clear water.

The reef itself offers excellent snorkeling and diving with visibility often exceeding 15 meters. The coral gardens are among the most diverse in Masbate, and the sanctuary's protected status has allowed the ecosystem to recover and thrive. It is one of the most accessible and rewarding marine experiences in the Bicol Region.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Buntod Reef

Buntod Reef is just off the coast of Masbate City — one of the most accessible marine sanctuaries in the Bicol Region, reachable by a short boat ride from the city port.

01

Fly to Masbate City

Fly from Manila to Masbate Airport (~1 hour). Cebu Pacific and Philippine Airlines operate this route. From the airport, take a tricycle to Masbate City proper (~15 minutes).

Flight: ~1 hr  |  ~₱1,500₱3,500 one-way
02

Head to the Masbate City Port

From Masbate City, take a tricycle to the city port or Buntod jump-off point. Boat operators at the port offer trips to Buntod Reef. Negotiate the rate for a round-trip boat hire including waiting time.

Tricycle to port: ~₱20–₱30  |  — ~10 min
03

Boat to Buntod Reef

Board a pump boat for the 15₱20 minute ride to Buntod Reef. The boat will anchor near the sandbar and reef. Bring your own snorkeling gear or rent from operators at the port. Life jackets are provided.

🚌 Boat: ~₱500–₱800 round trip (whole boat)  |  — ~15₱20 min
04

Entrance & Sanctuary Fee

Pay the sanctuary entrance fee upon arrival. The fee supports the maintenance and protection of the reef. Snorkeling equipment rental is available on-site. Allow 2₱4 hours to fully explore the reef and sandbar.

Entrance: ~₱50–₱100  |  Snorkel rental: ~₱100–₱150

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

🌞

Best Time to Visit

  • March — June (Dry Season): Best visibility and calmest sea conditions. The sandbar is most accessible and the water is clearest.
  • Low tide visits: The sandbar is most dramatic at low tide when it fully emerges. Check tide tables before planning your trip.
  • Morning visits: Calmer winds and better light for snorkeling and photography. Afternoon winds can pick up and reduce visibility.
  • Avoid rough weather: The boat ride can be uncomfortable in choppy conditions. Check the weather forecast before heading out.
📌

Snorkeling & Diving Tips

  • Reef-safe sunscreen only — chemical sunscreens damage coral reefs. Use mineral-based (zinc oxide or titanium dioxide) sunscreen or wear a rash guard.
  • Don't touch the coral — even a light touch can kill coral polyps that took decades to grow. Maintain buoyancy and keep fins away from the reef.
  • Bring your own mask and fins if possible — rental quality varies. A well-fitting mask makes a huge difference to the snorkeling experience.
  • Underwater camera: The coral gardens and fish life are highly photogenic. A waterproof camera or phone case is worth bringing.
🎒

What to Pack & Know

  • Picnic supplies: The sandbar is a perfect picnic spot. Bring food and drinks from Masbate City — there are no vendors at the reef.
  • Waterproof bag for your valuables — the boat ride can get splashy and the sandbar is surrounded by water.
  • Combine with Ticao Island: Buntod Reef and Ticao Island whale shark tours make an excellent 2-day Masbate marine adventure.
  • Masbate beef: After your reef visit, try the local beef dishes in Masbate City — the province is the cattle capital of the Philippines.
Mini Itinerary

A Day of Marine Adventure in Masbate

Combine Buntod Reef with Masbate City's heritage sites and local food for a perfect day in the cattle capital of the Philippines.

Morning
7:00 AM

Depart for Buntod Reef

Head to the Masbate City port early to catch the calmest sea conditions. Board your pump boat and make the 15₱20 minute crossing to Buntod Reef. The morning light on the water is beautiful.

7:30 AM

Snorkeling at the Reef

Spend 2₱3 hours snorkeling the coral gardens of Buntod Reef. Look for sea turtles, reef sharks, parrotfish, clownfish, and the incredible diversity of coral formations. The visibility is best in the morning.

10:00 AM

Sandbar Picnic

Rest on the white sandbar for a picnic. The sandbar is surrounded by shallow turquoise water — perfect for wading, relaxing, and photography. At low tide, the sandbar stretches impressively into the sea.

Afternoon
12:00 PM

Return to Masbate City

Take the boat back to Masbate City and have lunch at a local restaurant. Order inihaw na baka (grilled beef) or beef caldereta — Masbate's cattle heritage makes the beef here exceptional.

2:00 PM

Masbate Cathedral

Visit the Masbate Cathedral (Saint Anthony of Padua Parish) in the city center — a beautiful colonial-era church that is the spiritual heart of the province. The cathedral's facade and interior are worth exploring.

4:00 PM

Masbate City Waterfront

Stroll along the Masbate City waterfront as the afternoon light softens. Watch the fishing boats return to port and enjoy the relaxed pace of this provincial city before your evening departure.
