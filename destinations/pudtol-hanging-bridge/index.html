Introduction & Overview

Apayao's Thrilling River Crossing

The Pudtol Hanging Bridge spans the Apayao River in the municipality of Pudtol, Apayao — a suspension bridge that serves as both a vital community crossing and one of the most dramatic natural viewpoints in the Cordillera Administrative Region.

Standing on the bridge, you look down at the crystal-clear Apayao River flowing through a forested gorge far below. The surrounding landscape of ancient forest, Cordillera mountains, and the pristine river creates a view of extraordinary natural beauty. The bridge sways gently as you cross — adding to the thrill of the experience.

The bridge is an important connection for the Isneg indigenous communities on both sides of the river, and crossing it gives visitors a sense of the daily life and landscape of one of the Philippines' most remote and least-visited provinces.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Pudtol Hanging Bridge

The Pudtol Hanging Bridge is in Pudtol, Apayao — reached via Kabugao, the Apayao capital, from Tuguegarao in Cagayan Valley.

01

Bus to Tuguegarao, Cagayan

Take a bus from Manila to Tuguegarao, Cagayan (~10₱12 hours). Tuguegarao is the main gateway to Apayao from the east.

Bus: ~10₱12 hrs  |  ~₱600–₱900
02

Van to Kabugao, Apayao

From Tuguegarao, take a van to Kabugao (~3₱4 hours). Kabugao is the base for all Apayao adventures including the Pudtol Hanging Bridge.

Van: ~₱150–₱250  |  — ~3₱4 hrs
03

Transport to Pudtol

From Kabugao, arrange transport to Pudtol municipality where the hanging bridge is located. The local tourism office can help arrange vehicles and guides for the trip.

Transport: ~₱300–₱800  |  — ~1₱2 hrs from Kabugao
04

Cross the Bridge

Walk to the Pudtol Hanging Bridge and cross it for the full experience. The views of the Apayao River gorge from the bridge are spectacular. Combine with a swim in the river below for the complete experience.

Free to cross  |  Swimming in river below

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

📌

Crossing the Bridge

  • Don't rush: Take your time crossing the bridge and stop in the middle for the best views of the river gorge below.
  • Hold the cables: The bridge sways as you walk — hold the side cables for stability, especially if you're nervous about heights.
  • Photography: The view from the bridge is extraordinary — the river below, the forest on both banks, and the mountains in the distance. Bring a wide-angle lens.
  • Respect the bridge: It is a working community crossing used daily by Isneg residents. Don't jump or shake the bridge unnecessarily.
📌

Nature & Surroundings

  • Swim in the Apayao River: After crossing the bridge, descend to the river bank for a swim in the crystal-clear water below the bridge.
  • Birdwatching: The forested gorge around the bridge is excellent for birdwatching — endemic Cordillera species can be spotted from the bridge and the river banks.
  • Combine with Puting Bato Falls: The falls and the bridge are both in Apayao and can be visited on the same multi-day trip from Kabugao.
  • Sunset views: The bridge offers spectacular sunset views over the Apayao River valley — stay until evening if possible.
🎒

What to Pack

  • Cash only — bring enough from Manila or Tuguegarao. ATM access in Apayao is very limited.
  • Swimwear — for swimming in the Apayao River below the bridge.
  • Camera with good grip — the bridge sways and you'll want both hands free for photography. A neck strap is essential.
  • Sturdy footwear — the bridge surface can be slippery. Wear shoes with good grip.
Mini Itinerary

Apayao Adventure Day

Combine the Pudtol Hanging Bridge with the Apayao River and Puting Bato Falls for a complete Apayao wilderness adventure.

Morning
7:00 AM

Puting Bato Falls

Start the day at Puting Bato Falls in Luna — the white limestone waterfall and turquoise pool are best visited in the morning when the light is clearest.

10:00 AM

Apayao River Boat Tour

Board a traditional bangka for a river tour on the Apayao River. Glide through forested gorges and past Isneg villages on one of the most pristine rivers in the Philippines.

Afternoon
1:00 PM

Lunch by the River

Have lunch at a riverside spot — fresh fish from the Apayao River cooked over an open fire. A simple and deeply satisfying meal in an extraordinary setting.

2:30 PM

Pudtol Hanging Bridge

Cross the Pudtol Hanging Bridge for the full experience. Stand in the middle of the bridge and look down at the Apayao River gorge below — one of the most dramatic viewpoints in the Cordillera.

4:00 PM

River Swimming

Descend to the river bank below the bridge for a final swim in the crystal-clear Apayao River. The late afternoon light on the water is beautiful.
