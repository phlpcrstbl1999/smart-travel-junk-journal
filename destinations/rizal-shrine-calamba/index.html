Introduction

The Birthplace of the Philippine National Hero

The Rizal Shrine in Calamba, Laguna is the ancestral home of Dr. Jos Protasio Rizal Mercado y Alonso Realonda — the Philippines' national hero, born here on June 19, 1861. The house is a beautifully restored example of a 19th-century Filipino principalia home — a two-story bahay na bato (stone-and-wood house) with a nipa palm roof, wide wooden floors, and the distinctive architecture of prosperous Laguna families of the Spanish colonial era.

Rizal grew up in this house in Calamba, surrounded by the fertile rice fields and the shores of Laguna de Bay. His childhood here — the stories his mother told him, the landscape he observed, the injustices he witnessed — shaped the consciousness that would produce Noli Me Tangere and El Filibusterismo, the two novels that ignited the Philippine Revolution. The house is now a National Cultural Treasure and one of the most important heritage sites in the Philippines.

The shrine complex includes the ancestral house (a faithful reconstruction of the original, which was demolished during the American period), a garden with a life-size statue of Rizal, and a museum with personal artifacts, documents, and exhibits on Rizal's life, works, and martyrdom. The adjacent Calamba City Hall plaza features a large Rizal monument and is the center of the annual Rizal Day celebrations.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Rizal Shrine Calamba

The Rizal Shrine is located in Calamba City, Laguna — about 54 km south of Manila. It's easily accessible by bus or car via SLEX.

01

Bus from Manila to Calamba

Take a JAC Liner, DLTB, or Jam Liner bus from Buendia (Pasay) or Cubao to Calamba, Laguna. Buses run frequently throughout the day. From the Calamba terminal, take a tricycle to the Rizal Shrine (about 10 minutes).

Manila to Calamba: ~1.5₱2 hours  |  ~₱80–₱120
02

Drive from Manila via SLEX

Drive via SLEX — Calamba exit. The shrine is near the Calamba City Hall in the town center. Having your own vehicle makes it easy to combine with Pagsanjan Falls (30 minutes away) or Enchanted Kingdom in Santa Rosa.

Drive: ~1₱1.5 hours  |  Parking available at the shrine
03

Explore the Shrine Complex

The shrine complex includes the ancestral house, the garden, and the museum. Guided tours are available and highly recommended — the guides provide rich context about Rizal's childhood, his family, and the historical significance of each room and artifact.

Entrance fee: ~₱50–₱75  |  Allow 1₱2 hours
04

Calamba City Hall Plaza

After the shrine, walk to the adjacent Calamba City Hall plaza to see the large Rizal monument and the historic Calamba Church (San Juan Bautista Parish). The plaza is the heart of Calamba's heritage district and a pleasant place to walk.

5-minute walk from the shrine  |  Great photo opportunities

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

📌

Visiting Hours & Fees

  • Open TuesdaySunday: The shrine is closed on Mondays. Opening hours are typically 8:00 AM — 5:00 PM. Confirm current hours before visiting.
  • Entrance fee: A small entrance fee (around ₱50–₱75) covers access to the ancestral house and museum. Guided tours are included or available for a small additional fee.
  • June 19 (Rizal's Birthday): The shrine hosts special events and ceremonies on Rizal's birthday — a particularly meaningful time to visit.
  • December 30 (Rizal Day): National holiday commemorating Rizal's execution in 1896. Major ceremonies are held at the shrine and at Luneta Park in Manila.
📌

What to See

  • The Ancestral House: A faithful reconstruction of the original bahay na bato where Rizal was born and grew up. Period furniture, family portraits, and personal artifacts recreate the atmosphere of 19th-century principalia life.
  • Rizal's Bedroom: The room where the young Jos slept, studied, and first developed his extraordinary intellectual gifts. His mother Teodora Alonso taught him to read here.
  • The Museum: Exhibits on Rizal's life, his novels, his travels in Europe, his medical practice, his execution at Luneta, and his enduring legacy as the Philippines' national hero.
  • The Garden: A peaceful garden with a life-size statue of Rizal and the original well from the family property.
📌

Combining with Other Destinations

  • Pagsanjan Falls: About 30 minutes east of Calamba — combine for a full Laguna day trip. Visit Rizal Shrine in the morning, then head to Pagsanjan for the afternoon boat ride.
  • Enchanted Kingdom: About 15 minutes north of Calamba in Santa Rosa — perfect for families combining heritage and theme park in one day.
  • Tagaytay Ridge: About 30 minutes south of Calamba — combine for a heritage-and-highland day trip with bulalo dinner at Tagaytay.
  • Los Baos: About 20 minutes east — the University of the Philippines Los Baos campus, hot springs, and the IRRI (International Rice Research Institute) are nearby.
Mini Itinerary

How to Spend a Day in Calamba & Laguna

Calamba is the perfect base for a full Laguna day trip — combine the Rizal Shrine with Pagsanjan Falls or Enchanted Kingdom for a complete experience.

Morning
8:00 AM

Rizal Shrine Opening

Arrive at the shrine when it opens at 8 AM. The morning light is beautiful in the garden and the ancestral house is less crowded early. Take the guided tour for the full historical context.

9:30 AM

Calamba City Hall Plaza

Walk to the adjacent plaza to see the Rizal monument and the historic San Juan Bautista Parish Church. The church dates to the Spanish colonial period and is closely associated with Rizal's family.

10:30 AM

Calamba Hot Springs

Calamba is famous for its natural hot springs — there are several resort complexes in the area offering hot spring pools. A relaxing mid-morning soak before heading to the next destination.

Afternoon
12:00 PM

Lunch in Calamba

Have lunch at one of the restaurants near the Calamba plaza. Laguna is known for its buko pie (coconut pie) — buy some from the famous roadside stalls along the highway for pasalubong.

1:30 PM

Pagsanjan Falls (Option A)

Drive 30 minutes to Pagsanjan for the afternoon boat ride through the gorge. Register at the boat station by 2 PM for a late afternoon trip — the gorge light is beautiful in the afternoon.

1:30 PM

Enchanted Kingdom (Option B)

Drive 15 minutes north to Enchanted Kingdom in Santa Rosa for an afternoon of rides and attractions. Perfect for families or groups who want to combine heritage and fun in one day.
