Introduction

Aurora's Crown Jewel Waterfall

Located in the municipality of San Luis, Aurora province, Ditumabo Mother Falls — locally known as Inang Bato — is one of the tallest and most impressive waterfalls in Luzon. The falls drop approximately 70 meters into a wide, deep pool surrounded by towering rainforest, creating a mist-filled natural amphitheater that is both humbling and exhilarating.

What makes Ditumabo truly special is the journey to reach it. The trek involves wading and swimming through the Ditumabo River — crossing the river multiple times, navigating through boulders, and pushing through the current in some sections. The river itself is crystal clear and refreshing, and the trek through the Sierra Madre rainforest is an adventure in its own right.

The falls are located within the Northern Sierra Madre Natural Park — one of the largest and most biodiverse protected areas in the Philippines. The surrounding forest is home to rare wildlife, endemic birds, and plant species found nowhere else on Earth. Visiting Ditumabo is not just a waterfall experience — it is an immersion in one of the Philippines' most extraordinary natural ecosystems.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Ditumabo Falls

Ditumabo Mother Falls is located in San Luis, Aurora — about 220 km northeast of Manila. The nearest major town is Baler, the capital of Aurora province.

01

Bus from Manila to Baler

Genesis Transport and Joy Bus operate direct buses from Cubao, Quezon City to Baler, Aurora. Travel time is about 5₱6 hours. Buses run throughout the day — book in advance on weekends.

Travel time: ~5₱6 hours  |  ~₱400–₱600 one-way
02

Baler to San Luis

From Baler, take a tricycle or jeepney to San Luis municipality — about 30₱45 minutes. From San Luis town center, hire a habal-habal to the Ditumabo Falls jump-off point in Barangay Ditumabo.

Jeepney to San Luis: ~30₱45 min  |  Habal-habal to jump-off: ~20 min
03

Register & Hire a Guide

Register at the barangay hall and pay the environmental fee. A local guide is mandatory — the river trek involves multiple crossings and the trail is not marked. Guides are available at the jump-off point.

Environmental fee: ~₱50  |  Guide: ~₱200–₱300 (mandatory)
04

The River Trek

The trek to the falls takes about 1.5₱2 hours each way and involves wading and swimming through the Ditumabo River. Wear water shoes, secure your valuables in a waterproof bag, and be prepared to get completely wet.

Trek time: ~1.5₱2 hours each way  |  Bring waterproof bag

Essential Visitor Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Visit

🌞

Best Time to Visit

  • November — May (Dry Season): Best time — lower, clearer river water makes the trek safer and more enjoyable. The falls are still powerful and beautiful.
  • After moderate rains: The falls are most dramatic and powerful after recent rainfall — the cascade is fuller and the mist more intense.
  • Avoid typhoon season (June — October): Flash floods can make the river trek extremely dangerous. Never attempt the trek during or after heavy rain.
  • Start early: Begin the trek by 7₱8 AM to reach the falls in the morning light and return before afternoon rains.
📌

River Trek Tips

  • Wear water shoes with good grip — the river bottom is rocky and slippery. Sandals and flip-flops are not safe.
  • Secure all valuables in a waterproof bag — you will be fully submerged in some sections of the river.
  • Follow your guide's instructions at all times — they know the safe crossing points and the current conditions.
  • Don't attempt the trek alone. The river can be deceptive — what looks calm can have strong undercurrents.
  • Swim in the pool at the base of the falls — the mist, the sound, and the cool water make it one of the most refreshing experiences in Aurora.
🎒

What to Pack

  • Water shoes with ankle support and good grip.
  • Waterproof bag or dry bag — essential for phone, camera, and valuables.
  • Change of clothes — you will be completely wet. Pack dry clothes in a separate waterproof bag.
  • Insect repellent — the rainforest trail has mosquitoes.
  • Packed lunch and water — no food stalls near the falls. Bring enough for the full day.
  • Cash — no ATMs in San Luis. Withdraw in Baler before heading out.
Visitor Guide

How to Experience Ditumabo Falls

A complete guide to the river trek and a suggested Aurora province itinerary.

At the Falls
Step 1

Gear Up & Enter the River

Change into your water shoes, secure your valuables in a waterproof bag, and enter the Ditumabo River with your guide. The water is immediately cool and clear — a refreshing start to the adventure.

Step 2

The River Trek

Wade, swim, and scramble through the river for 1.5₱2 hours. The trek involves multiple crossings, boulder scrambles, and sections where you swim against the current. Your guide leads the way and ensures your safety throughout.

Step 3

First View of the Falls

As you round the final bend in the river, the falls come into view — a 70-meter cascade of white water dropping into a wide, mist-filled pool. The sound hits you before the sight. Take a moment to absorb the scale before approaching.

Step 4

Swim in the Pool

Swim to the base of the falls and feel the mist and spray on your face. The pool is deep and clear — you can swim directly under the cascade if you're brave enough. The experience is overwhelming in the best possible way.

Aurora Day Trip
Morning

Ditumabo Mother Falls

Start early for the river trek to the falls. Allow a full morning — the trek takes 1.5₱2 hours each way plus time at the falls.

Afternoon

Sabang Beach

Head to Sabang Beach in Baler for an afternoon of surfing or swimming. The long stretch of Pacific-facing beach is one of the most beautiful in Aurora.

Evening

Baler Church

Visit the historic Baler Church — the site of the famous 1898 Siege of Baler — before dinner in Baler town. A perfect close to an Aurora adventure day.
