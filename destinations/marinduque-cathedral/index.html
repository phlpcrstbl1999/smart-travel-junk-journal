Introduction & History

A Fortress Church on a Hill

The Immaculate Conception Cathedral Parish, also known as the Boac Cathedral, is one of the most distinctive churches in the Philippines. Perched on a rocky hill overlooking the Boac River, its thick stone walls and fortress-like design reflect the turbulent colonial era when churches doubled as refuges during raids.

Construction began in the late 17th century and was completed in 1792. The church is dedicated to the Immaculate Conception of the Virgin Mary and serves as the seat of the Roman Catholic Diocese of Boac. Its massive stone buttresses, bell tower, and commanding hilltop position make it one of the most photographed landmarks in MIMAROPA.

Every Holy Week, the cathedral becomes the epicenter of the world-famous Moriones Festival — a colorful street pageant where locals dress as Roman soldiers in elaborate masks and costumes, reenacting the story of Longinus. It is one of the most unique religious festivals in Asia.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Marinduque

Marinduque is an island province in MIMAROPA, reachable by ferry from Lucena or by plane. Boac, the capital, is where the cathedral stands.

01

Bus to Lucena City

From Manila (Cubao or Buendia), take a bus to Lucena Grand Terminal. Several bus lines operate this route daily. The ride takes about 3₱4 hours depending on traffic.

Travel time: ~3₱4 hours  |  ~₱200–₱350
02

Ferry from Lucena to Marinduque

From Lucena, take a tricycle to Dalahican Port and board a ferry to Balanacan Port in Marinduque. Ferries run several times daily and the crossing takes about 3 hours.

Ferry: ~3 hours  |  ~₱200–₱300
03

Van or Jeepney to Boac

From Balanacan Port, take a van or jeepney to Boac town proper. The cathedral is prominently visible on the hill — you can walk up the stone steps from the town plaza.

Van to Boac: ~30₱45 min  |  ~₱50–₱80
04

Alternatively: Fly to Marinduque

PAL Express and other carriers operate flights from Manila to Marinduque Airport (Gasan). From the airport, take a tricycle or van to Boac — about 30 minutes away.

Flight: ~45 min  |  Tricycle to Boac: ~30 min

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

🌞

Best Time to Visit

  • Holy Week (MarchApril): The Moriones Festival transforms Boac into a living pageant. Book accommodation months in advance — the island fills up fast.
  • November — February (Dry Season): Best weather for island exploration. Clear skies and calm seas for ferry crossings.
  • Avoid June — October: Typhoon season. Ferry services may be suspended during bad weather.
  • Early Morning: Visit the cathedral at 6₱7 AM for peaceful reflection before tour groups arrive.
🎒

What to Pack

  • Modest clothing — required inside the cathedral. Bring a shawl or cover-up.
  • Comfortable shoes — the stone steps up to the cathedral are steep and uneven.
  • Cash — ATMs are limited in Boac. Bring enough for your entire stay.
  • Sunscreen & insect repellent — essential for island travel.
  • Camera — the view of Boac River from the cathedral grounds is stunning.
📌

Local Tips & Etiquette

  • Respect mass schedules. The cathedral holds regular masses — check times before visiting to avoid disrupting services.
  • Climb the bell tower for a panoramic view of Boac and the surrounding hills — ask the caretaker for access.
  • Explore the old convent adjacent to the church, which houses religious artifacts and historical records.
  • Try local food — Marinduque is known for its pansit habhab (noodles eaten from banana leaf) and fresh seafood.
Mini Itinerary

How to Spend a Day in Boac

Boac is compact and walkable. A well-planned day lets you experience the cathedral, the town's heritage, and Marinduque's natural beauty.

Morning
6:30 AM

Morning Mass at the Cathedral

Attend early morning mass at the Immaculate Conception Cathedral. The interior is serene at this hour — soft light filtering through the windows, the scent of incense, and the quiet devotion of local parishioners.

8:00 AM

Explore the Cathedral Grounds

Walk the perimeter of the cathedral, admire the massive stone buttresses, and climb the bell tower for a sweeping view of the Boac River and the town below. The old convent beside the church is also worth a look.

9:30 AM

Boac Town Heritage Walk

Stroll through Boac town proper — the old plaza, ancestral houses, and the riverside promenade. The town has a quiet, unhurried charm that feels worlds away from Manila.

Afternoon
11:00 AM

Lunch: Pansit Habhab

Try pansit habhab — Marinduque's signature noodle dish served on a banana leaf and eaten without utensils. Find it at local eateries near the town plaza. Pair it with fresh buko juice.

1:00 PM

Day Trip to Maniwaya Island

Head to Santa Cruz and take a boat to Maniwaya Island — one of Marinduque's most beautiful beaches with powdery white sand and clear turquoise water. A perfect afternoon escape.

5:00 PM

Sunset at the Cathedral Hill

Return to Boac and climb back up to the cathedral for sunset. The view of the river and the surrounding hills bathed in golden light is one of Marinduque's most memorable sights.
