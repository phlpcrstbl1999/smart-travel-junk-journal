Introduction

The Lake That Swallowed a Town

Completed in 1974, the Pantabangan Dam on the Pampanga River in Pantabangan, Nueva Ecija created one of the largest reservoirs in the Philippines. The dam serves multiple purposes — hydroelectric power generation, irrigation for the vast rice fields of Central Luzon, and flood control for the lowland provinces downstream.

What makes Pantabangan uniquely compelling is its history. When the reservoir was filled, it submerged the original Pantabangan town — including homes, farms, a church, and the entire community infrastructure. The residents were relocated to a new town on higher ground. During periods of low water, the ruins of the old town — including the top of the old church — emerge from the water, creating one of the most haunting and photogenic scenes in Nueva Ecija.

The reservoir itself is a destination of great natural beauty — the vast expanse of blue water surrounded by the Sierra Madre mountains creates a landscape that feels more like a highland lake than a man-made structure. Boat tours of the reservoir are available, offering views of the submerged ruins, the dam structure, and the mountain scenery. The area is also popular for fishing, kayaking, and lakeside picnics.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Pantabangan Dam

Pantabangan Dam is located in Pantabangan, Nueva Ecija — about 160 km north of Manila. The nearest major city is Cabanatuan City.

01

Bus from Manila to Cabanatuan

Genesis Transport and Baliwag Transit operate buses from Cubao or Pasay to Cabanatuan City. Travel time is about 2.5₱3 hours. Cabanatuan is the main hub for all Nueva Ecija destinations.

Travel time: ~2.5₱3 hours  |  ~₱150–₱250 one-way
02

Cabanatuan to Pantabangan

From Cabanatuan, take a bus or van to Pantabangan municipality — about 1.5₱2 hours along a scenic mountain road. The road passes through the Sierra Madre foothills with increasingly dramatic scenery as you approach the reservoir.

Bus/van to Pantabangan: ~1.5₱2 hours  |  ~₱80–₱120
03

Arrive at the Dam & Reservoir

The dam and reservoir viewpoints are accessible from the main road. Register at the NPC (National Power Corporation) office for access to the dam area. Boat tours of the reservoir can be arranged at the lakeside.

Dam viewpoint: Free  |  — Boat tour: ~₱500–₱800  |  Bring valid ID
04

Combine with Nueva Ecija Day Tour

Pantabangan Dam is best combined with Minalungao National Park and Gabaldon Falls for a full Nueva Ecija adventure day. Having your own vehicle is the most convenient way to cover all three sites.

Combine with Minalungao & Gabaldon Falls  |  Own vehicle recommended

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Visit

📌

Best Time to Visit

  • March — May (Low Water Season): The best time to see the submerged ruins — the old church spire and town remnants emerge from the water as the reservoir level drops.
  • November — February: Cooler temperatures and clearer skies make for the most comfortable visits and the best photography conditions.
  • Sunrise: The reservoir at sunrise — with the mountains reflected in the still water — is one of the most beautiful natural scenes in Nueva Ecija.
  • Avoid typhoon season (June — October): The reservoir rises significantly and the submerged ruins are not visible. Access roads may also be affected.
—

What to See & Do

  • Boat tour of the reservoir: The best way to see the submerged ruins and appreciate the scale of the reservoir. Arrange at the lakeside.
  • Submerged church ruins: During low water periods, the top of the old Pantabangan church emerges from the water — one of the most haunting and photogenic scenes in Central Luzon.
  • Dam viewpoint: Walk along the dam crest for views of both the reservoir and the downstream valley.
  • Fishing: The reservoir is popular for fishing — tilapia, catfish, and carp are abundant. Local fishermen can arrange fishing trips.
📌

Practical Tips

  • Bring a valid ID for registration at the NPC office.
  • Bring cash — no ATMs in Pantabangan. Withdraw in Cabanatuan before heading out.
  • Pack a picnic — the lakeside setting is perfect for a meal with a view. Food options in Pantabangan are limited.
  • Wear sunscreen and a hat — the reservoir is exposed and the sun can be intense.
  • Check water levels before visiting if you want to see the submerged ruins — local tourism offices can advise on current conditions.
Visitor Guide

How to Experience Pantabangan Dam

A complete guide to the dam and reservoir, and a suggested Nueva Ecija day tour.

At the Dam
Step 1

Dam Viewpoint

Begin at the dam crest viewpoint. The scale of the structure is impressive — the dam wall holds back the vast reservoir on one side while the downstream valley drops away on the other. Walk the crest for different perspectives.

Step 2

Reservoir Boat Tour

Arrange a boat tour of the reservoir. The boat takes you across the vast expanse of water toward the submerged ruins area. The combination of the mountain scenery, the clear water, and the haunting ruins creates an extraordinary experience.

Step 3

Submerged Church Ruins

During low water periods, approach the area where the old Pantabangan church spire emerges from the water. The sight of a church tower rising from a lake — a reminder of the town that was sacrificed for the dam — is deeply moving and unforgettable.

Step 4

Lakeside Picnic

Find a spot on the lakeside for a picnic with the reservoir as your backdrop. The combination of the vast water, the surrounding mountains, and the peaceful atmosphere makes Pantabangan one of the most serene picnic spots in Nueva Ecija.

Nueva Ecija Day Tour
Morning

Minalungao National Park

Start at the limestone gorge — boat tour, swimming, and cliff jumping. Allow 3₱4 hours for the full park experience.

Midday

Pantabangan Dam

Dam viewpoint, reservoir boat tour, and lakeside picnic. Allow 2₱3 hours for a thorough visit.

Afternoon

Gabaldon Falls

End your day at Gabaldon Falls — a beautiful waterfall with natural swimming pools. A refreshing close to a full Nueva Ecija adventure day.
