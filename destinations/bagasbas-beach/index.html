Introduction & About

Where Bicol Meets the Pacific

Bagasbas Beach stretches for several kilometers along the Pacific-facing coast of Daet, Camarines Norte — just 3 km from the town center. The beach is known for its consistent surf, generated by Pacific swells that roll in year-round, making it one of the most reliable surfing spots in the Bicol Region and a favorite among both beginners and experienced surfers.

Unlike the white-sand beaches of Palawan or Boracay, Bagasbas has dark volcanic sand — a characteristic of many Pacific-facing Bicol beaches. The waves are powerful and the atmosphere is energetic, with surf schools, board rentals, and beachfront cottages lining the shore. The beach is at its most dramatic during the habagat (southwest monsoon) season, when large swells produce the best surfing conditions.

Bagasbas is also the gateway to Calaguas Islands — many travelers combine a surf session at Bagasbas with an overnight camping trip to the pristine Calaguas Islands, making Daet one of the most versatile beach destinations in Bicol.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Bagasbas Beach

Bagasbas Beach is just 3 km from Daet town center, Camarines Norte — about 330 km from Manila, reachable by overnight bus.

01

Overnight Bus from Manila to Daet

Take an overnight bus from Manila (Cubao or Pasay) to Daet, Camarines Norte. Philtranco, DLTB Co., and Superlines operate this route. The journey takes about 7₱8 hours. Buses depart in the evening and arrive in Daet by early morning.

Bus: ~7₱8 hrs  |  ~₱500–₱800 one-way
02

Tricycle from Daet to Bagasbas

From the Daet bus terminal, take a tricycle directly to Bagasbas Beach — just 3 km away. The ride takes about 10 minutes. Tricycles are plentiful and the beach is well-known to all local drivers.

Tricycle: ~10 min  |  ~₱30–₱50
03

Surf Schools & Board Rentals

Several surf schools operate along the beach, offering lessons for beginners and board rentals for experienced surfers. Lessons typically run 1₱2 hours and include board and instructor. Most schools are open daily from early morning to late afternoon.

Surf lesson: ~₱300–₱500/hr  |  Board rental: ~₱150–₱200/hr
04

Accommodation at the Beach

Several guesthouses, cottages, and small resorts line the beachfront at Bagasbas. Options range from basic fan rooms to air-conditioned cottages. Staying beachside lets you catch early morning waves and watch the sunset from your doorstep.

🏖 Beachfront rooms: ~₱500–₱1,500/night  |  Camping also available

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

🏄

Surfing at Bagasbas

  • Best surf season: June — October (Habagat). Southwest monsoon swells produce the largest and most consistent waves — ideal for intermediate and advanced surfers.
  • Year-round waves: Even during the dry season, Bagasbas has rideable waves suitable for beginners and intermediate surfers.
  • Beginner-friendly: The beach break is forgiving for learners. Most surf schools offer 1-hour lessons that get beginners standing on their first day.
  • Early morning (6:00₱9:00 AM): Best conditions — offshore winds, cleaner waves, and fewer surfers in the water.
🎒

What to Pack

  • Rash guard — essential for sun protection during long surf sessions. The Bicol sun is intense.
  • Reef shoes — the dark volcanic sand can get very hot at midday. Useful for walking to and from the water.
  • Cash — surf schools, board rentals, and beachfront eateries are mostly cash-only.
  • Sunscreen — apply generously and reapply after every session in the water.
  • Dry bag — to protect your phone and valuables while surfing.
📌

Beach & Safety Tips

  • Swim between the flags — designated swimming areas are marked by flags. The surf zone can have strong currents outside these areas.
  • Never surf alone — always surf with others and let someone on shore know you're in the water.
  • Combine with Calaguas Islands — Bagasbas is the perfect base for a Calaguas overnight trip. Surf one day, island-hop the next.
  • Try Bicol food in Daet — the town has excellent local restaurants serving Bicol Express, laing, and fresh seafood.
Mini Itinerary

How to Spend a Weekend in Camarines Norte

Combine Bagasbas Beach surfing with a Calaguas Islands overnight for the ultimate Camarines Norte weekend.

Day 1 — Bagasbas
6:00 AM

Early Morning Surf Session

Hit the waves at Bagasbas Beach early morning when conditions are cleanest. Rent a board or book a lesson with one of the beachfront surf schools. The 6₱9 AM window has the best offshore winds and fewest surfers.

9:30 AM

Breakfast at a Beachfront Eatery

Eat at one of the small restaurants along the beach — sinangag (garlic fried rice), fresh fish, and taho are the morning staples. Watch the waves while you eat and plan your afternoon session.

3:00 PM

Afternoon Surf & Sunset

Another surf session in the late afternoon as the light turns golden. Watch the sunset from the beach — the dark sand and powerful waves create a dramatic, moody atmosphere as the sun drops into the horizon.

Day 2 — Calaguas
5:00 AM

Depart for Calaguas Islands

Take a tricycle to the port and board a bangka for the 2₱3 hour crossing to Calaguas Islands. The contrast between Bagasbas's wild surf beach and Calaguas's pristine calm cove is one of the great travel contrasts in the Philippines.

8:00 AM — 4:00 PM

Calaguas Day Trip

Spend the day at Mahabang Buhangin — swimming, snorkeling, and relaxing on the pristine white sand. Return to Daet by late afternoon and catch an evening bus back to Manila.

7:00 PM

Dinner in Daet & Bus Home

Eat dinner at a local restaurant in Daet town — try the Bicol Express and fresh seafood one last time before boarding your overnight bus back to Manila.
