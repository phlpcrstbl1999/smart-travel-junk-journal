Introduction

Manila's Closest Waterfall Escape

Daranak Falls is the most beloved waterfall near Metro Manila — a magnificent 15-meter cascade on the Daranak River in Tanay, Rizal, at the foot of the Sierra Madre mountain range. The falls plunge into a wide, deep natural swimming pool of crystal-clear, cool mountain water — one of the most refreshing natural swimming spots within easy reach of the capital.

The falls are set within a well-maintained eco-park managed by the Tanay local government. The park has picnic areas, cottages, changing rooms, and a small restaurant — making it ideal for family outings, barkada trips, and company picnics. The surrounding forest is part of the Sierra Madre — the longest mountain range in the Philippines and one of the most biodiverse ecosystems in Southeast Asia.

Just a short walk upstream from Daranak is the smaller but equally beautiful Batlag Falls — a narrower, more intimate cascade that is less visited and often more peaceful. Most visitors combine both falls in a single trip, making Tanay one of the best waterfall day trips from Manila. The area is also the gateway to the Masungi Georeserve — one of the most spectacular conservation areas in the Philippines, just 30 minutes away.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Daranak Falls

Daranak Falls is in Tanay, Rizal — about 60 km east of Manila. The drive takes about 1.5₱2 hours via Marcos Highway or the MarikinaInfanta Highway.

01

Drive from Manila via Marcos Highway

Drive via Marcos Highway (Marikina) — Tanay, Rizal. From Tanay town proper, follow the signs to Daranak Falls — it's about 3 km from the town center. The road is paved all the way to the eco-park entrance.

Manila to Daranak: ~1.5₱2 hours  |  Parking at the eco-park
02

Bus + Jeepney from Manila

Take a Genesis or Philtranco bus from Cubao to Tanay, Rizal. From the Tanay terminal, take a tricycle to Daranak Falls (about 10₱15 minutes). Buses run regularly from early morning.

Cubao to Tanay: ~1.5₱2 hours  |  ~₱80–₱120  |  Tricycle: ~₱50
03

Enter the Eco-Park

Pay the entrance fee at the gate. The eco-park has a well-maintained path down to the falls — about a 5-minute walk from the entrance. Cottage rentals, picnic tables, and changing rooms are available inside.

Entrance: ~₱50–₱80  |  Cottage rental: ~₱300–₱500  |  Allow 3₱4 hours
04

Combine with Batlag Falls

A short walk upstream from Daranak leads to Batlag Falls — a narrower, more secluded cascade that most visitors miss. The combined Daranak + Batlag visit is the best way to experience the Tanay waterfall area. Ask the park staff for directions to Batlag.

Walk to Batlag: ~15₱20 min  |  Less crowded, more peaceful

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

📌

Best Time to Visit

  • November — May (Dry Season): The best time for swimming — water levels are manageable and the pool is at its clearest. The falls are still powerful but the current in the pool is gentle enough for swimming.
  • After heavy rain: The falls are most dramatic after rain — the volume of water increases dramatically and the cascade is more powerful. However, the pool current can be strong — exercise caution.
  • Weekdays: Daranak is extremely popular on weekends — the pool can get very crowded. Weekday visits are far more peaceful and the water is cleaner.
  • Early morning: Arrive by 8₱9 AM to get the best light on the falls and before the crowds arrive. The morning mist in the Sierra Madre forest is magical.
📌

Swimming Tips

  • Life jackets available: Life jackets are available for rent at the eco-park — recommended for non-swimmers and children. The pool is deep near the base of the falls.
  • Don't swim directly under the falls: The force of the water at the base of the falls is powerful and can be dangerous. Swim in the wider pool area away from the direct cascade.
  • Bring a change of clothes: You will get wet — even if you don't swim, the mist from the falls reaches the surrounding area. Pack dry clothes and a towel.
  • No alcohol in the pool: Alcohol is prohibited in the swimming area. The eco-park enforces this rule strictly for safety reasons.
📌

What to Bring

  • Swimwear: Essential — the natural pool is the main attraction. Bring a rash guard for sun protection if you plan to spend extended time in the water.
  • Waterproof bag: Protect your phone and valuables from the mist and splashing. Waterproof phone cases are available from vendors near the entrance.
  • Food and drinks: A small restaurant operates inside the eco-park but bringing your own food for a picnic is allowed and recommended. The park has picnic tables and cottages.
  • Cash only: The entrance fee and cottage rentals are cash-only. There are no ATMs at the falls — bring enough cash from Manila or Tanay town.
Mini Itinerary

How to Spend a Day in Tanay, Rizal

Combine Daranak Falls with Batlag Falls and Masungi Georeserve for the ultimate Rizal nature day — one of the best day trips from Manila.

Morning
6:00 AM

Depart Manila Early

Leave Manila by 6 AM to beat the Marcos Highway traffic. The drive to Tanay takes about 1.5₱2 hours. Arriving early means you get the falls to yourself before the weekend crowds arrive.

8:00 AM

Daranak Falls — Morning Swim

Enter the eco-park and head straight to the falls. The morning light filtering through the Sierra Madre forest canopy onto the cascade is extraordinary. Swim in the natural pool while it's still quiet.

10:00 AM

Batlag Falls Walk

Walk upstream to Batlag Falls — a 15₱20 minute trail through the forest. The smaller, more intimate cascade is often deserted in the morning. A peaceful contrast to the more popular Daranak.

Afternoon
12:00 PM

Picnic Lunch at the Eco-Park

Have lunch at one of the picnic cottages inside the eco-park. The sound of the falls in the background makes for one of the most pleasant picnic settings near Manila.

2:00 PM

Masungi Georeserve (Pre-Booked)

Drive 30 minutes to Masungi Georeserve for the afternoon Discovery Trail — one of the most spectacular conservation experiences in the Philippines. Must be booked weeks in advance.

6:00 PM

Dinner in Antipolo

Drive to Antipolo for dinner at one of the hilltop restaurants with views of Metro Manila — the perfect end to a full Rizal nature day. Padi's Point and Crescent Moon Caf are local favorites.
