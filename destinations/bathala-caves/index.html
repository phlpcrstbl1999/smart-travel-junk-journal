Introduction & About

Sacred Caverns of the Ancient Gods

Named after Bathala — the supreme deity of pre-colonial Filipino mythology — the Bathala Caves in Torrijos, Marinduque are among the most atmospheric cave systems in the Philippines. The name alone hints at the reverence locals have long held for these ancient caverns.

The cave system consists of seven interconnected chambers, each with its own character. Some are vast cathedral-like halls with towering stalactites and stalagmites; others are narrow passages that require crawling through. An underground stream flows through parts of the cave, and in some chambers you can swim through subterranean pools lit by filtered light from above.

The caves are also home to thousands of bats — at dusk, watching them pour out of the cave entrance in a swirling black cloud is one of Marinduque's most dramatic natural spectacles. Local guides are essential for navigating the cave system safely and for sharing the legends and folklore surrounding these sacred caverns.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Bathala Caves

Bathala Caves is located in Torrijos, on the eastern side of Marinduque island. Getting there requires a ferry to Marinduque and then a land trip across the island.

01

Bus to Lucena, then Ferry to Marinduque

From Manila, take a bus to Lucena City (~3₱4 hours), then a ferry from Dalahican Port to Balanacan Port in Marinduque (~3 hours). Ferries run several times daily.

Bus: ~3₱4 hrs  |  Ferry: ~3 hrs  |  ~₱400–₱650 total
02

Van or Jeepney to Torrijos

From Balanacan Port, take a van or jeepney heading to Torrijos on the eastern coast of Marinduque. The ride takes about 1₱1.5 hours along the scenic island road.

Van to Torrijos: ~1₱1.5 hrs  |  ~₱80–₱120
03

Tricycle to Bathala Caves

From Torrijos town proper, hire a tricycle to the Bathala Caves entrance. The caves are a few kilometers from town. Ask locals for directions — everyone knows Bathala Caves.

Tricycle: ~15₱20 min  |  ~₱50–₱80
04

Hire a Local Guide at the Entrance

A local guide is mandatory for exploring Bathala Caves. Guides are available at the cave entrance and are essential for safety — the cave system is complex and some passages are unmarked. Entrance fees and guide fees are minimal.

Guide fee: ~₱150–₱200  |  Entrance: ~₱50

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

📌

Best Time to Visit

  • November — May (Dry Season): Best time — cave passages are more accessible and underground water levels are lower, making exploration easier and safer.
  • Dusk (5:00₱6:00 PM): Arrive in the late afternoon to witness the spectacular bat exodus — thousands of bats streaming out of the cave entrance at sunset.
  • Avoid rainy season: Heavy rains can flood cave passages and make some chambers inaccessible or dangerous.
  • Weekdays: Far fewer visitors — you may have the caves almost entirely to yourself.
🎒

What to Pack

  • Headlamp or flashlight — essential. The caves are dark and your guide's light alone is not enough. Bring extra batteries.
  • Old clothes you don't mind getting dirty — some passages require crawling through mud and water.
  • Rubber sandals or old shoes — the cave floor is wet and slippery in places.
  • Waterproof bag — to protect your phone and valuables from water and mud.
  • Cash — for entrance fees, guide fees, and tricycle hire. No ATMs in Torrijos.
🔦

Cave Safety & Etiquette

  • Never explore without a guide. The cave system is complex — getting lost is a real risk without local knowledge.
  • Do not touch the stalactites or stalagmites. These formations take thousands of years to grow and are easily damaged by human contact.
  • Move slowly and carefully. Cave floors are uneven, wet, and slippery. Take your time.
  • Respect the bats. Do not disturb bat colonies — they are an important part of the cave ecosystem.
  • Pack out all trash. Leave the cave exactly as you found it.
Mini Itinerary

How to Spend a Day at Bathala Caves

Combine your cave visit with Torrijos's coastal scenery and a sunset bat-watching session for a full and memorable day.

Morning
8:00 AM

Arrive in Torrijos

Take an early van from Balanacan Port or Boac to Torrijos. The drive along the eastern coast of Marinduque is scenic — rolling hills, coconut groves, and glimpses of the sea. Grab breakfast at a local eatery in town.

9:30 AM

Enter Bathala Caves

Hire a guide at the cave entrance and begin your exploration. The first chamber is the largest — a vast hall with impressive stalactite formations. Your guide will lead you through the interconnected chambers, sharing the legends of Bathala along the way.

11:30 AM

Underground Swimming

In the deeper chambers, an underground stream forms pools where you can swim in cool, crystal-clear water. The experience of swimming in a cave lit only by your headlamp is surreal and unforgettable.

Afternoon
1:00 PM

Lunch in Torrijos

Return to Torrijos town for lunch. Try fresh grilled fish and sinigang at a local carinderia. The seafood in Marinduque is exceptionally fresh — caught the same morning from the surrounding waters.

3:00 PM

Explore Torrijos Coastline

Spend the afternoon exploring the Torrijos coastline — quiet beaches, fishing villages, and dramatic rock formations along the eastern shore of Marinduque. Hire a tricycle for a scenic coastal loop.

5:00 PM

Bat Exodus at Dusk

Return to the cave entrance just before sunset to witness the bat exodus — thousands of bats streaming out of the cave in a swirling, living cloud. It's one of Marinduque's most dramatic natural spectacles and a photographer's dream.
