Introduction

The Philippines' Premier Theme Park

Enchanted Kingdom is the Philippines' first and most beloved world-class theme park, located in Santa Rosa, Laguna — about 43 km south of Manila. Opened in 1995, it has been the premier family entertainment destination in the country for nearly three decades, drawing millions of visitors annually with its combination of thrilling rides, live entertainment, themed zones, and the spectacular EKstravaganza fireworks and light show.

The park is divided into 7 themed zones: Enchanted Village, Portabello, Brooklyn Place, Midway Boardwalk, Jungle Outpost, Boulderville, and Storyland — each with its own distinct atmosphere, rides, and attractions. From the heart-stopping Space Shuttle (a 360 looping roller coaster) and the Anchor's Away (a giant swinging ship) to the family-friendly Carousel and the water rides of Jungle Outpost, there's something for every age and thrill level.

Beyond the rides, Enchanted Kingdom is known for its live entertainment — stage shows, character meet-and-greets, and the legendary EKstravaganza evening show combining fireworks, lasers, and music that has become a Filipino cultural institution. The park's food scene has also evolved significantly, with themed restaurants, food stalls, and the popular Eldar's Feast buffet restaurant.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Enchanted Kingdom

Enchanted Kingdom is in Santa Rosa, Laguna — about 43 km south of Manila via SLEX. It's one of the most accessible theme parks from the capital.

01

Bus from Manila to Santa Rosa

Take a JAC Liner or Jam Liner bus from Buendia (Pasay) or Cubao bound for Santa Rosa, Laguna. From the Santa Rosa terminal, take a tricycle or jeepney to Enchanted Kingdom (about 10₱15 minutes).

Manila to Santa Rosa: ~1₱1.5 hours  |  ~₱80–₱100
02

Drive from Manila via SLEX

Drive via SLEX — Santa Rosa exit — EK Road. The park has large parking areas. Driving gives you the flexibility to arrive early and leave after the EKstravaganza evening show.

Drive: ~45 min₱1 hour  |  Large parking area at the park
03

Buy Tickets in Advance

Purchase tickets online through the Enchanted Kingdom website or app to avoid queues at the gate. All-day ride passes are available. Check the park's website for current ticket prices, operating hours, and special event schedules.

Day pass: ~₱800–₱1,200  |  Buy online to skip the queue
04

Plan Your Day Around EKstravaganza

The EKstravaganza fireworks and light show is the highlight of the evening — check the schedule on arrival and plan your day to be at the main stage area for the show. It typically runs on weekends and holidays.

EKstravaganza: evenings on weekends  |  Full day: 10AM₱9PM

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

🌞

Best Time to Visit

  • Weekdays: Far less crowded than weekends — shorter queues for rides and a more relaxed experience. If you can visit on a weekday, do it.
  • December: The park is beautifully decorated for Christmas and the EKstravaganza shows are at their most spectacular. The most magical time to visit but also the most crowded.
  • Avoid peak holidays: Christmas, New Year, Holy Week, and school holidays see massive crowds. Expect long queues for popular rides.
  • Arrive early: Gates open at 10 AM — arrive early to get on the most popular rides (Space Shuttle, Anchor's Away, Rio Grande Rapids) before the queues build up.
📌

Must-Do Rides

  • Space Shuttle: The park's signature ride — a 360 looping roller coaster. The most thrilling ride in the park and a rite of passage for every EK visitor.
  • Anchor's Away: A giant swinging ship that reaches near-vertical angles. Screams guaranteed.
  • Rio Grande Rapids: A white-water raft ride — you will get wet. Perfect for hot days.
  • Wheel of Fate: A giant Ferris wheel offering panoramic views of the park and the surrounding Laguna landscape.
  • Agila: A suspended roller coaster — smooth, fast, and exhilarating.
🍽

Food & Practical Tips

  • Eldar's Feast: The park's buffet restaurant — a good option for a full meal without leaving the park. Book in advance on busy days.
  • Food stalls: Each themed zone has food stalls with Filipino favorites, snacks, and drinks. Prices are higher than outside but reasonable for a theme park.
  • Wear comfortable shoes: You'll be walking all day. Closed-toe shoes are required for most rides.
  • Bring a change of clothes: The water rides will soak you. Pack a dry set of clothes or a poncho.
  • Lockers: Available near the entrance for storing bags and valuables during rides.
Mini Itinerary

How to Spend a Full Day at Enchanted Kingdom

A full day at EK — from the first rides in the morning to the EKstravaganza fireworks in the evening — is one of the most complete entertainment experiences near Manila.

Morning
10:00 AM

Gates Open — Head to Space Shuttle First

Arrive at opening time and head straight to the Space Shuttle before the queues build up. This is the park's most popular ride — getting it done early means you can enjoy the rest of the day without a long wait.

11:00 AM

Anchor's Away & Boulderville

Hit Anchor's Away (the giant swinging ship) and explore Boulderville — the zone with the most thrill rides. The queues are still manageable in the late morning.

12:30 PM

Lunch at Eldar's Feast

Take a break for lunch at Eldar's Feast buffet or grab food from the themed stalls. The park is busiest between 12₱2 PM — use this time to eat and rest rather than queue for rides.

Afternoon & Evening
2:00 PM

Rio Grande Rapids & Jungle Outpost

Head to the water rides in the afternoon heat — Rio Grande Rapids and the Jungle Outpost zone. Get soaked and cool down. Change into dry clothes afterwards.

4:00 PM

Live Shows & Storyland

Check the entertainment schedule for afternoon live shows. Explore Storyland and Enchanted Village — the family-friendly zones with character meet-and-greets and gentler rides for younger visitors.

7:30 PM

EKstravaganza Fireworks Show

Position yourself at the main stage area for the EKstravaganza — the park's signature fireworks, lasers, and music spectacular. The perfect finale to a full day at the Philippines' most beloved theme park.
