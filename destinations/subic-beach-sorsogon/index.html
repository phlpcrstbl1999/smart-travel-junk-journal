Introduction & Overview

The Tip of Luzon's Hidden Beach

Subic Beach (not to be confused with Subic Bay in Zambales) is located in Matnog, Sorsogon — the southernmost municipality of Luzon. The beach is known for its fine white sand, remarkably clear turquoise water, and the laid-back atmosphere of a destination that has not yet been overrun by mass tourism.

The beach is part of the Matnog island group, which includes several small islands accessible by bangka from the Matnog port. Island hopping around Matnog reveals hidden coves, pristine snorkeling spots, and the dramatic scenery of the San Bernardino Strait where the Pacific Ocean meets the Sibuyan Sea.

Matnog is also the ferry port for Samar — the gateway between Luzon and the Visayas. Many travelers pass through without stopping, unaware that one of Sorsogon's most beautiful beaches is just minutes from the port.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Subic Beach Sorsogon

Subic Beach is in Matnog, Sorsogon — the southernmost tip of Luzon, about 70 km from Sorsogon City.

01

Fly to Legazpi or Bus to Sorsogon

Fly from Manila to Legazpi Airport (~1 hour) then travel south to Sorsogon City (~2 hours), or take an overnight bus directly to Sorsogon City (~10₱12 hours).

Flight + bus: ~3 hrs  |  Bus: ~10₱12 hrs
02

Van to Matnog

From Sorsogon City, take a van to Matnog (~1.5₱2 hours). Vans depart regularly from the Sorsogon City terminal. Matnog is the southernmost town of Luzon and the ferry port for Samar.

Van: ~₱80–₱120  |  — ~1.5₱2 hrs
03

Bangka to Subic Beach

From the Matnog port, hire a bangka to Subic Beach (~15₱20 minutes). The boat ride offers views of the surrounding islands and the San Bernardino Strait. Negotiate a round-trip rate with waiting time.

🚌 Bangka: ~₱300–₱500 round trip  |  — ~15₱20 min
04

Island Hopping Option

Hire a bangka for a full island hopping tour around the Matnog islands — visiting Subic Beach, Juag Lagoon, and other hidden coves and snorkeling spots in the San Bernardino Strait. A full tour takes 4₱6 hours.

Island hopping: ~₱1,500₱2,500 per boat  |  — 4₱6 hrs

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

🌞

Best Time to Visit

  • March — June (Dry Season): Calmest seas and best visibility for snorkeling. The white sand and turquoise water are at their most beautiful.
  • Combine with Donsol: Visit Donsol for whale sharks (NovemberJune) and add Subic Beach as a relaxing finale to your Sorsogon trip.
  • Avoid rough weather: The San Bernardino Strait can be choppy during typhoon season. Check conditions before booking a bangka.
  • Weekdays are quieter: The beach gets busier on weekends with local visitors from Sorsogon City.
📌

Water Activities

  • Snorkeling: The coral reefs around the Matnog islands are healthy and colorful. Bring your own gear or rent from local operators at the port.
  • Island hopping: The Matnog island group has several beautiful spots — Juag Lagoon, Tikling Island, and Subic Beach are the highlights.
  • Swimming: The water at Subic Beach is calm and clear — excellent for swimming. The beach shelves gently, making it safe for all ages.
  • Sunset views: The San Bernardino Strait offers spectacular sunsets — stay until evening for the best light.
🎒

What to Pack & Know

  • Cash only — bring enough from Sorsogon City. There are no ATMs in Matnog.
  • Food and water — bring supplies from Matnog town. There are no vendors on Subic Beach itself.
  • Reef-safe sunscreen — protect the coral reefs by using mineral-based sunscreen only.
  • Pili nuts: Buy local pili nut products in Matnog or Sorsogon City — the province's most famous food product.
Mini Itinerary

A Day at the Tip of Luzon

Combine Subic Beach with Matnog island hopping and a visit to Donsol for the perfect southern Sorsogon adventure.

Morning
7:00 AM

Depart Sorsogon City

Take an early van from Sorsogon City to Matnog (~1.5 hours). Arrive at the port and arrange a bangka for island hopping or a direct trip to Subic Beach.

9:00 AM

Subic Beach

Arrive at Subic Beach and spend the morning swimming, snorkeling, and relaxing on the white sand. The water is clearest in the morning before the afternoon winds pick up.

11:00 AM

Island Hopping

Continue the island hopping tour to Juag Lagoon and Tikling Island. Snorkel the coral reefs, explore hidden coves, and enjoy the dramatic scenery of the San Bernardino Strait.

Afternoon
1:00 PM

Lunch in Matnog

Return to Matnog town for lunch at a local eatery. Fresh seafood is the specialty — grilled fish, shrimp, and squid caught that morning from the strait.

3:00 PM

Return to Sorsogon City

Take the afternoon van back to Sorsogon City. If time permits, stop at a local market to buy pili nut products and other Sorsogon specialties.

Evening

Dinner in Sorsogon City

Have dinner in Sorsogon City and prepare for your onward journey. The city has good restaurants serving Bicolano cuisine — try kinunot (shark in coconut milk) and laing.
