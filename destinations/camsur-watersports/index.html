Introduction & Overview

Where the Philippines Rides the Wake

The CamSur Watersports Complex (CWC) in Pili, Camarines Sur is one of the most celebrated sports tourism destinations in the Philippines. Opened in 2006 under the initiative of then-Governor Luis Villafuerte, CWC was built to put Camarines Sur on the international sports map — and it succeeded spectacularly.

The complex features a full-size cable wakeboard system — a network of overhead cables that pull riders across a purpose-built lake at speed, allowing them to perform jumps, tricks, and aerial maneuvers over ramps and obstacles. It is the largest cable wakeboard park in Asia and has hosted the IWWF Cable Wakeboard World Championships and numerous international competitions.

Beyond wakeboarding, CWC offers wakeskating, kneeboarding, kayaking, swimming, and a range of accommodation and dining options within the complex. It is a complete resort destination that caters to both serious athletes and first-time visitors looking for a fun, active day out.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to CamSur Watersports Complex

CWC is located in Pili, Camarines Sur — about 10 km from Naga City, the regional center of Bicol. It is easily reached by air or bus from Manila.

01

Fly to Naga City

Fly from Manila to Naga Airport in Camarines Sur (~1 hour). Cebu Pacific and Philippine Airlines operate this route. From the airport, take a tricycle or taxi to CWC in Pili (~20₱30 minutes).

Flight: ~1 hr  |  ~₱1,500₱3,500 one-way
02

Alternatively: Bus from Manila

Several bus lines operate overnight trips from Manila to Naga City (~8₱10 hours). Philtranco, DLTB Co., and Isarog Line are the main operators. From Naga City bus terminal, take a jeepney or tricycle to CWC in Pili.

Bus: ~8₱10 hrs  |  ~₱600–₱900 one-way
03

Jeepney or Tricycle to CWC

From Naga City, take a jeepney bound for Pili and ask to be dropped at CWC (~20 minutes). Alternatively, hire a tricycle for a direct trip. The complex is well-known and any driver will know the way.

Jeepney: ~₱15–₱25  |  Tricycle: ~₱80–₱120
04

Entrance & Activities

CWC has an entrance fee that covers access to the grounds and swimming areas. Wakeboarding sessions are booked separately and include equipment rental. Lessons are available for beginners. The complex also has cottages, a hotel, and restaurants on-site.

Entrance: ~₱100–₱200  |  Wakeboard session: ~₱400–₱600/hr

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

🌞

Best Time to Visit

  • March — June (Dry Season): Best weather for outdoor water sports. Sunny skies and calm conditions make for the most enjoyable wakeboarding sessions.
  • Weekdays are less crowded: CWC gets busy on weekends and holidays. Visit on a weekday for shorter queues and more cable time.
  • International competitions: Check CWC's schedule for hosted events — watching world-class wakeboarders compete is a spectacular experience even for non-riders.
  • Avoid typhoon season peaks (SeptemberNovember) — Bicol is typhoon-prone and outdoor activities may be suspended during bad weather.
📌

Wakeboarding Tips

  • Beginners are welcome: CWC has certified instructors who teach complete beginners. Most people can stand up on the board within their first session.
  • Wear the provided life jacket at all times on the water — it is mandatory and non-negotiable.
  • Start on the beginner cable — CWC has separate cables for beginners and advanced riders. Don't jump to the main cable until you're comfortable.
  • Bring a change of clothes — you will get wet, and the Bicol sun can be intense. A rash guard is recommended for sun protection.
🎒

What to Pack & Know

  • Swimwear and towel — essential. Board shorts or a one-piece swimsuit are ideal for wakeboarding.
  • Waterproof sunscreen — the lake is open and exposed. Reapply frequently.
  • Combine with Caramoan Islands: CWC and the Caramoan Islands are both in Camarines Sur — combine them for a 2₱3 day Bicol adventure.
  • Try Bicol food in Naga: After your session, head to Naga City for Bicol Express, laing, and pinangat — the definitive Bicolano dishes.
Mini Itinerary

How to Spend a Day at CWC

Combine a full day at CamSur Watersports Complex with an evening in Naga City for the perfect Camarines Sur experience.

Morning
8:00 AM

Arrive at CWC

Arrive early to beat the crowds and get the most cable time. Check in, pay your entrance fee, and head to the equipment rental area. If you're a beginner, sign up for a lesson with one of the certified instructors.

9:00 AM

Wakeboard Session

Hit the cable wakeboard system for your first session. Beginners will spend time on the starter cable learning to stand and balance. More experienced riders can move to the main cable and attempt the ramps and obstacles.

11:00 AM

Swimming & Kayaking

Take a break from wakeboarding and enjoy the swimming areas and kayaking on the lake. The complex has designated swimming zones and kayak rentals for a more relaxed water experience.

Afternoon
1:00 PM

Lunch at CWC

Eat at one of the on-site restaurants within the complex. The food court offers Filipino dishes, snacks, and refreshments. Rest and recharge before your afternoon session.

2:30 PM

Afternoon Wakeboard Session

Return to the cable for an afternoon session. By now you'll have found your balance and can start attempting more ambitious moves. Watch the advanced riders on the main cable for inspiration.

5:00 PM

Naga City for Dinner

Head to Naga City for dinner. Try Bicol Express at a local restaurant — the spicy pork and coconut milk dish was invented in Bicol and is best eaten here. Explore the Naga City center and the Peafrancia Shrine before heading back.
