Introduction

Puerto Galera's Mountain Stage

Mount Malasimbo is a scenic mountain above Puerto Galera, Oriental Mindoro — rising above the bay to offer panoramic views of the Verde Island Passage, the surrounding mountains of Mindoro, and the distant peaks of Batangas across the water. The mountain is accessible by motorbike or tricycle from White Beach, and the viewpoint at the summit is one of the most beautiful vantage points in Oriental Mindoro.

Mount Malasimbo is best known internationally as the home of the Malasimbo Music and Arts Festival — an annual outdoor music event held every March on a natural amphitheater carved into the mountain's forested slopes. The festival features world-class jazz, electronic, and world music artists performing on a stage with the Puerto Galera Bay and the Verde Island Passage as a backdrop — one of the most spectacular festival settings in Southeast Asia.

The Verde Island Passage visible from Mount Malasimbo is one of the most biodiverse marine corridors in the world — a narrow strait between Luzon and Mindoro that is considered the "center of the center of marine biodiversity" on Earth. The view from the mountain captures this extraordinary geography — the narrow passage, the islands, and the mountains on both sides — in a single panoramic sweep.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From White Beach to Mount Malasimbo

Mount Malasimbo is above Puerto Galera town — easily reached by motorbike or tricycle from White Beach in about 20₱30 minutes.

01

Motorbike from White Beach

Rent a motorbike from White Beach and ride to the Mount Malasimbo viewpoint — about 20₱30 minutes. The road climbs through the forest above Puerto Galera town. The viewpoint is accessible by motorbike on a paved road.

Motorbike rental: ~₱400–₱600/day  |  Ride: ~20₱30 minutes
02

Tricycle from Puerto Galera

Take a tricycle from Puerto Galera town or White Beach to the Mount Malasimbo area. Negotiate a round-trip fare with waiting time. The tricycle driver can take you to the viewpoint and wait while you enjoy the panorama.

Round-trip tricycle: ~₱200–₱400  |  Best views: late afternoon
03

The Viewpoint

The main viewpoint offers panoramic views of Puerto Galera Bay, the Verde Island Passage, and the mountains of Batangas across the water. The late afternoon light is the most beautiful — the bay turns golden as the sun sets over the passage.

Best time: late afternoon  |  Panoramic bay and passage views
04

Malasimbo Music & Arts Festival

The annual Malasimbo Music and Arts Festival is held every March on the mountain's natural amphitheater. Tickets sell out quickly — book months in advance through the official festival website. The festival runs over 2₱3 days with camping available on the mountain.

Festival: March annually  |  Book at malasimbofestival.com  |  — Camping available

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know

📌

The Malasimbo Festival

  • Book early: The Malasimbo Music and Arts Festival is one of the most popular outdoor music events in the Philippines · tickets sell out months in advance. Check the official website (malasimbofestival.com) for dates and ticket sales.
  • The setting: The festival stage is carved into the mountain's forested slopes — a natural amphitheater with Puerto Galera Bay and the Verde Island Passage as a backdrop. One of the most spectacular festival settings in Southeast Asia.
  • Music lineup: The festival features world-class jazz, electronic, and world music artists — both international headliners and Filipino artists. The programming is consistently excellent.
  • Camping: Camping is available on the mountain during the festival — a magical experience of sleeping under the stars above Puerto Galera Bay. Bring a tent and sleeping bag.
📌

The Viewpoint

  • Late afternoon is best: The viewpoint is most beautiful in the late afternoon — the golden light on Puerto Galera Bay and the Verde Island Passage is extraordinary. Time your visit for 4₱5 PM.
  • Verde Island Passage: The narrow strait visible from the mountain is one of the most biodiverse marine corridors in the world — the "center of the center of marine biodiversity." The view captures this extraordinary geography.
  • Combine with Tamaraw Falls: The viewpoint is on the road between White Beach and Tamaraw Falls — stop here on the way to or from the falls for a perfect half-day combination.
  • Sunrise option: For the most dramatic light, visit at sunrise — the early morning mist over the bay and the first light on the mountains of Batangas is extraordinary.
📌

Combining with Other Destinations

  • White Beach: The main beach hub of Puerto Galera — 20₱30 minutes from Mount Malasimbo. Combine for a beach and mountain day.
  • Tamaraw Falls: 15 km from Puerto Galera — combine with the viewpoint for a perfect half-day inland trip from White Beach.
  • Sabang Beach: Puerto Galera's dive hub — combine with a morning dive and an afternoon viewpoint visit for a complete Puerto Galera day.
  • Aninuan & Talipanan: Quieter beaches west of White Beach — combine with the mountain viewpoint for a peaceful Puerto Galera day away from the crowds.
Mini Itinerary

How to Spend a Day Around Mount Malasimbo

The perfect Puerto Galera inland day — Tamaraw Falls in the morning, Mount Malasimbo viewpoint in the afternoon, White Beach dinner at sunset.

Morning
8:00 AM

Rent a Motorbike & Head to Tamaraw Falls

Rent a motorbike from White Beach and ride the scenic coastal road to Tamaraw Falls — 30 minutes through beautiful Mindoro countryside. Swim in the natural pool at the base of the 30-meter cascade.

10:30 AM

Return via Mount Malasimbo

Ride back toward Puerto Galera and stop at the Mount Malasimbo viewpoint. The mid-morning light on the bay is beautiful — take your time at the panoramic viewpoint before heading back to White Beach.

Afternoon
12:00 PM

Lunch at White Beach

Return to White Beach for lunch at one of the beachfront restaurants. Fresh seafood, cold drinks, and the White Beach atmosphere after a morning in the Mindoro mountains.

2:00 PM

Afternoon Dive or Beach Time

Book an afternoon dive with one of the White Beach dive shops, or simply relax on the beach and snorkel in the clear water. The perfect complement to a morning of mountain and waterfall exploration.

5:00 PM

Sunset at the Viewpoint (Optional)

Return to the Mount Malasimbo viewpoint for sunset — the golden light on Puerto Galera Bay and the Verde Island Passage at dusk is one of the most beautiful views in Oriental Mindoro.
