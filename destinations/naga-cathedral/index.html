Introduction & History

The Cathedral of Bicol's Capital

The Naga Metropolitan Cathedral (Cathedral Parish of Saint John the Baptist) is the mother church of the Archdiocese of Caceres — the oldest diocese in the Bicol Region, established by the Spanish in 1595. The cathedral stands at the heart of Naga City's historic center, facing the city plaza, and has been the spiritual anchor of the Bicolano people for over four centuries.

The cathedral is most famous as the center of the Peafrancia Festival — held every third Saturday of September, it is one of the largest Marian devotions in Asia. The festival culminates in the Fluvial Procession, where the image of Our Lady of Peafrancia is transported by boat along the Naga River, accompanied by thousands of devotees wading in the water and hundreds of decorated bancas. It is one of the most extraordinary religious spectacles in the Philippines.

The cathedral's architecture reflects centuries of renovation and restoration, blending colonial heritage with modern additions while retaining its role as the living spiritual center of Naga City and the Bicol Region.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Naga Cathedral

Naga Cathedral is in the heart of Naga City — the regional center of Bicol, accessible by air or bus from Manila and well-connected to other Bicol destinations.

01

Fly to Naga City

Fly from Manila to Naga Airport (~1 hour). Cebu Pacific and Philippine Airlines operate this route. From the airport, take a tricycle or taxi to the cathedral in the city center (~20 minutes).

Flight: ~1 hr  |  ~₱1,500₱3,500 one-way
02

Alternatively: Bus from Manila

Several bus lines operate overnight trips from Manila to Naga City (~8₱10 hours). Philtranco, DLTB Co., and Isarog Line are the main operators. The bus terminal is a short tricycle ride from the cathedral.

Bus: ~8₱10 hrs  |  ~₱600–₱900 one-way
03

Walk or Tricycle to the Cathedral

The Naga Cathedral is located in the city center facing the Naga City Plaza — within walking distance of most hotels and the main commercial areas. Any tricycle driver will know it immediately.

Walkable from city center  |  Tricycle: ~₱15–₱30
04

Combine with CWC & Caramoan

Naga City is the perfect base for exploring Camarines Sur. Combine the cathedral with a day at CamSur Watersports Complex in Pili and a multi-day trip to the Caramoan Islands for the complete Camarines Sur experience.

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

📌

Best Time to Visit

  • Peafrancia Festival (3rd Saturday of September): The most extraordinary time to visit — the Fluvial Procession along the Naga River is one of the most spectacular religious events in Asia. Book accommodation months in advance.
  • Holy Week (March/April): Solemn and deeply moving — the cathedral hosts elaborate Lenten observances and processions.
  • December: Christmas season brings beautiful decorations and a festive atmosphere to the cathedral and city plaza.
  • Any time of year is suitable for a heritage visit — the cathedral is open daily and the city center is always lively.
—

Visiting the Cathedral

  • Dress modestly — cover shoulders and knees when entering. The cathedral is an active place of worship visited by thousands of devotees daily.
  • Peafrancia Shrine: A short walk from the cathedral, the Basilica Minore of Our Lady of Peafrancia houses the venerated image of the Virgin. Visit both on the same trip.
  • Daily masses are held throughout the day. Check the schedule at the parish office to plan your visit around services.
  • The city plaza surrounding the cathedral is a pleasant spot to sit, observe local life, and enjoy the colonial-era architecture of Naga's historic center.
📌

Naga City Food & Culture

  • Bicol Express: The iconic spicy pork and coconut milk dish — invented in Bicol and best eaten here. Try it at Laida's or any local restaurant near the plaza.
  • Laing: Taro leaves cooked in coconut cream — a Bicolano staple that is rich, creamy, and deeply satisfying.
  • Pinangat: Taro leaves stuffed with pork and shrimp, wrapped and cooked in coconut milk — a Naga City specialty.
  • Naga City Night Market: The evening market near the city center is a great place to try street food, local snacks, and fresh produce after your cathedral visit.
Mini Itinerary

A Day in Naga City

Combine Naga Cathedral with the Peafrancia Shrine, CamSur Watersports Complex, and the best of Bicolano food for a perfect day in the heart of Bicol.

Morning
8:00 AM

Naga Metropolitan Cathedral

Start at the Naga Cathedral in the morning. Attend the early mass or simply explore the cathedral's interior and exterior. The city plaza in the morning is peaceful and beautiful — a great time for photography.

9:30 AM

Peafrancia Shrine

Walk to the Basilica Minore of Our Lady of Peafrancia — a short distance from the cathedral. The venerated image of the Virgin is housed here and the basilica is a significant pilgrimage site visited by devotees year-round.

11:00 AM

Naga City Market & Breakfast

Explore the Naga City public market and have a late breakfast of laing and rice at a local eatery. The market is lively and colorful — a great window into everyday Bicolano life.

Afternoon
1:00 PM

CamSur Watersports Complex

Head to CWC in Pili (~20 minutes from Naga) for an afternoon wakeboarding session. Even if you've never tried it before, the beginner cable and patient instructors make it accessible and fun for everyone.

4:00 PM

Return to Naga City

Return to Naga City and freshen up at your hotel. The late afternoon is a good time to stroll the city center and browse the local shops near the plaza.

6:30 PM

Dinner: Bicol Express & Pinangat

Have dinner at a local Bicolano restaurant. Order Bicol Express, pinangat, and kinunot — the holy trinity of Bicolano cuisine. Wash it down with fresh buko juice and end the day with a walk around the illuminated cathedral plaza.
