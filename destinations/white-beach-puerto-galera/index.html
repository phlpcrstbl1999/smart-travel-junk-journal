Introduction

The Classic Manila Weekend Beach

White Beach in Puerto Galera, Oriental Mindoro is the most iconic beach destination accessible from Manila — a beautiful crescent of fine white sand and clear turquoise water in a sheltered bay, backed by the green hills of Mindoro. For over four decades, it has been the default weekend beach escape for Manila residents, offering a combination of beautiful beaches, world-class diving, vibrant nightlife, and easy accessibility that no other Philippine beach destination can match.

Puerto Galera is a UNESCO Man and Biosphere Reserve — the bay and its surrounding waters are protected for their extraordinary marine biodiversity. The diving around Puerto Galera is world-renowned — over 40 dive sites within easy boat distance, ranging from shallow coral gardens to dramatic wall dives and wreck dives. The area is particularly famous for its nudibranchs (sea slugs) — Puerto Galera is considered one of the best nudibranch diving destinations in the world.

White Beach itself is a lively, social beach — lined with restaurants, bars, beach resorts, and dive shops. It is not a secluded paradise — it is a vibrant, energetic beach destination with something for everyone. For those seeking more peace, the nearby Aninuan Beach and Talipanan Beach offer quieter alternatives just a short tricycle ride away.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to White Beach Puerto Galera

Puerto Galera is accessed by ferry from Batangas City Port — about 2 hours from Manila by bus, then a 1-hour ferry crossing to Mindoro.

01

Bus from Manila to Batangas City

Take a DLTB, JAC Liner, or Jam Liner bus from Buendia (Pasay) or Cubao to Batangas City Grand Terminal. The ride takes about 2 hours. From the terminal, take a tricycle to the Batangas City Port (about 10 minutes).

Manila to Batangas: ~2 hours  |  ~₱150–₱200  |  Tricycle to port: ~₱30
02

Ferry from Batangas to Puerto Galera

Take a fast craft or RORO ferry from Batangas City Port to Puerto Galera, Oriental Mindoro. The crossing takes about 1 hour. Several operators run this route — Si-Kat Ferry and Montenegro Lines are the main ones. Ferries run throughout the day.

Batangas to Puerto Galera: ~1 hour  |  ~₱250–₱350  |  Ferries run all day
03

Tricycle to White Beach

From the Puerto Galera pier (Muelle), take a tricycle to White Beach — about 10₱15 minutes. White Beach is the main beach area with the most resorts, restaurants, and dive shops. Sabang Beach (the dive hub) is in the opposite direction.

Muelle to White Beach: ~10₱15 min  |  ~₱50–₱80 per person
04

Book Accommodation in Advance

White Beach has dozens of resorts ranging from budget guesthouses to mid-range beachfront hotels. Book in advance for weekends and holidays — the beach fills up quickly. Many resorts have their own dive shops offering packages that combine accommodation and diving.

Budget: ~₱800–₱1,500/night  |  Beachfront: ~₱2,000₱5,000/night

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

📌

Best Time to Visit

  • November — May (Dry Season): The best time for White Beach — calm seas, clear water, and sunny skies. DecemberApril is peak season — book accommodation well in advance.
  • Avoid peak weekends: White Beach is extremely popular on long weekends and holidays. The beach can get very crowded. Weekday visits are far more relaxed.
  • Typhoon season (JuneOctober): The ferry crossing can be rough and may be cancelled during bad weather. Check conditions before traveling.
  • Holy Week: Puerto Galera is one of the most popular Holy Week destinations from Manila — expect massive crowds and book months in advance.
🤼

Diving Puerto Galera

  • World-class diving: Puerto Galera has over 40 dive sites — from the famous Sabang dive sites (The Canyons, Hole in the Wall, Shark Cave) to the coral gardens around White Beach.
  • Nudibranch capital: Puerto Galera is one of the world's best destinations for nudibranch (sea slug) photography — hundreds of species have been recorded in the area.
  • Dive shops everywhere: Both White Beach and Sabang Beach have numerous dive shops offering equipment rental, courses, and guided dives. Compare prices and reviews before booking.
  • Beginners welcome: The calm, shallow waters around White Beach are ideal for beginner divers and snorkelers. Many dive shops offer introductory dives and PADI Open Water courses.
📌

Beyond White Beach

  • Tamaraw Falls: A beautiful 30-meter waterfall about 15 km from Puerto Galera — a popular half-day trip by tricycle or motorbike. The falls have a natural swimming pool at the base.
  • Mount Malasimbo: A scenic mountain above Puerto Galera with panoramic views of the bay — home to the famous Malasimbo Music and Arts Festival held annually in March.
  • Aninuan & Talipanan Beaches: Quieter, less developed beaches west of White Beach — ideal for those seeking a more peaceful alternative to the busy White Beach scene.
  • Sabang Beach: The dive hub of Puerto Galera — more lively and party-oriented than White Beach, with the best access to the famous Sabang dive sites.
Mini Itinerary

How to Spend a Weekend at White Beach

Two days at White Beach — diving, beach time, Tamaraw Falls, and the vibrant Puerto Galera nightlife — is the classic Manila weekend escape.

Day 1
6:00 AM

Depart Manila — Bus to Batangas

Take the early bus from Buendia to Batangas City. Catch the morning ferry to Puerto Galera. Arrive at White Beach by 10₱11 AM — early enough for a full first day.

11:00 AM

Check In & Beach Time

Check in to your resort and head straight to the beach. The water at White Beach is calm and clear — perfect for swimming and snorkeling. Rent snorkel gear from the beach vendors.

2:00 PM

Afternoon Dive

Book an afternoon dive with one of the White Beach dive shops. The coral gardens around White Beach are excellent for beginners — turtles, reef fish, and pristine coral formations.

7:00 PM

Beachfront Dinner & Nightlife

Dinner at one of the beachfront restaurants — fresh seafood, cold beer, and the sound of the waves. White Beach has a lively bar scene in the evenings — the classic Puerto Galera night out.

Day 2
8:00 AM

Morning Dive or Snorkel

The morning is the best time for diving — clearest water and most active marine life. Book a morning dive or take the snorkel boat to the offshore coral gardens.

11:00 AM

Tamaraw Falls Day Trip

Rent a motorbike or take a tricycle to Tamaraw Falls — about 30 minutes from White Beach. Swim in the natural pool at the base of the 30-meter cascade before heading back for the afternoon ferry.

3:00 PM

Ferry Back to Batangas

Take the afternoon ferry back to Batangas City and the bus back to Manila. Arrive in Manila by early evening — the perfect end to a classic Puerto Galera weekend.
