Introduction & Overview

The Philippines' Premier Arts Institution

The Cultural Center of the Philippines (CCP) was established in 1969 on reclaimed land along Manila Bay. Designed by National Artist Leandro Locsin in the Brutalist style, the CCP complex is the most important arts and culture institution in the Philippines · the home of the country's national performing arts companies.

The CCP houses the Main Theater (the premier venue for ballet, opera, and theater), the Tanghalang Nicanor Abelardo (the main concert hall), the Tanghalang Aurelio Tolentino (a smaller theater), and the CCP Museum of Philippine Art. The resident companies include the Philippine Philharmonic Orchestra, Ballet Philippines, Philippine Opera Company, and Tanghalang Pilipino.

The CCP is also known for its annual Pasinaya Open House Festival — a free one-day arts festival where all CCP venues are open to the public with free performances, workshops, and exhibitions.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

Getting to the Cultural Center of the Philippines

The CCP is on Roxas Boulevard, Pasay — accessible by taxi, Grab, or jeepney along the Manila Bay waterfront.

01

Grab or Taxi

Grab and taxis are the most convenient options. Tell the driver "Cultural Center of the Philippines" or "CCP" — it is well-known and easily located on Roxas Boulevard.

Grab/Taxi: ~₱100–₱200 from Makati  |  Roxas Blvd, Pasay
02

Jeepney along Roxas Boulevard

Jeepneys running along Roxas Boulevard pass the CCP. Ask the driver to drop you at the Cultural Center stop.

Jeepney along Roxas Blvd: ~₱12–₱15  |  Bay views throughout
03

Check the Performance Schedule

Visit the CCP website before your trip to check the performance schedule. The Philippine Philharmonic Orchestra, Ballet Philippines, and visiting international companies perform regularly.

Tickets: ~₱500–₱3,000  |  Book online at culturalcenter.gov.ph
04

Pasinaya Open House Festival

The annual Pasinaya Festival (usually February) is a free one-day arts festival — all CCP venues open to the public with free performances, workshops, and exhibitions. One of the best free events in Manila.

Pasinaya: usually February  |  Free admission

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

📌

Performances & Events

  • Philippine Philharmonic Orchestra: The national orchestra performs regularly at the Tanghalang Nicanor Abelardo — world-class classical music at affordable prices.
  • Ballet Philippines: The national ballet company performs at the Main Theater — classical and contemporary ballet of the highest standard.
  • Philippine Opera Company: Full opera productions in the Main Theater — a rare and extraordinary experience.
  • Tanghalang Pilipino: The national theater company — Filipino plays and musicals in Filipino and English.
📌

CCP Museum & Architecture

  • CCP Museum of Philippine Art: A collection of Philippine visual art — paintings, sculpture, and installation art by National Artists and contemporary Filipino artists.
  • Leandro Locsin's architecture: The CCP Main Building is a masterpiece of Brutalist architecture — the massive concrete structure floating above the Manila Bay waterfront is one of the most distinctive buildings in the Philippines.
  • The grounds: The CCP complex includes open plazas and gardens facing Manila Bay — beautiful for an evening walk before or after a performance.
  • Pasinaya Festival: The annual open house festival is the best way to experience the full CCP complex for free.
📌

Practical Tips

  • Book tickets in advance for popular performances — Ballet Philippines and the PPO sell out quickly.
  • Dress code: Smart casual is appropriate for most performances. Formal attire is expected for gala events.
  • Combine with Manila Baywalk: The CCP is at the southern end of the Manila Baywalk — arrive early for the sunset before your performance.
  • Parking: The CCP has parking facilities — arrive early on performance nights as parking fills up quickly.
