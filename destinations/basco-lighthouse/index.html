Introduction & History

Batanes' Most Iconic Landmark

Perched on a grassy hill above Basco — the capital of Batanes province — the Basco Lighthouse is one of the most photographed landmarks in the Philippines. Built during the Spanish colonial era, the white cylindrical tower has guided ships through the treacherous waters of the Luzon Strait for well over a century.

Batanes is the northernmost province of the Philippines, sitting between the Philippine Sea to the east and the South China Sea to the west, just 190 km south of Taiwan. The province is known for its dramatic landscapes — rolling green hills, stone houses built to withstand typhoons, rugged coastlines, and a way of life that has remained largely unchanged for centuries.

The lighthouse sits on Naidi Hills, a short walk from Basco town center. The climb to the top rewards visitors with a sweeping 360-degree panorama — the Pacific Ocean stretching to the horizon, the green hills of Batan Island rolling inland, and on clear days, the distant outline of Taiwan to the north. It is one of the most dramatic viewpoints in the entire Philippines.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Basco Lighthouse

Batanes is one of the most remote provinces in the Philippines. The only practical way to get there is by plane — but the journey is absolutely worth it.

01

Fly Manila to Basco

Philippine Airlines and SkyJet Airlines operate flights from NAIA (Manila) to Basco Airport in Batanes. Flight time is about 1.5₱2 hours. Book well in advance — seats fill up fast, especially during peak season (MarchMay).

Flight time: ~1.5₱2 hours  |  ~₱3,000₱8,000 round trip
02

Arrive at Basco Airport

Basco Airport is small and charming — one of the most scenic airports in the Philippines. From the airport, your accommodation host or a local tricycle can take you to your guesthouse in Basco town. Most accommodations are within 10 minutes of the airport.

Tricycle from airport: ~₱50–₱100  |  — ~5₱10 minutes
03

Rent a Motorbike or Join a Tour

The best way to explore Batanes is by renting a motorbike (₱500–₱700/day) or joining a guided tour with a local operator. Roads are well-maintained and the island is small enough to cover in a day. A local guide adds invaluable cultural context.

Motorbike rental: ~₱500–₱700/day  |  Guided tour: ~₱800–₱1,500
04

Walk to Naidi Hills

The Basco Lighthouse is on Naidi Hills, a short walk or tricycle ride from Basco town center. The path up the hill is well-marked and takes about 10₱15 minutes on foot. The lighthouse is open to visitors and the grounds are free to explore.

Walk from town: ~10₱15 min  |  Free entry to grounds

Essential Visitor Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Visit

📌

Best Time to Visit

  • March — May (Dry Season): The best time to visit Batanes. Clear skies, calm seas, and lush green hills. Book flights months in advance as this is peak season.
  • Sunrise & Sunset: The lighthouse at golden hour is spectacular. Sunrise offers soft pink light over the Pacific; sunset paints the hills amber and gold.
  • June — February: Batanes experiences frequent typhoons and strong winds. Some flights may be cancelled. Check weather forecasts carefully before booking.
  • Avoid peak holidays: Holy Week and Christmas see the highest demand for flights. Book as early as possible.
📷

Photography Tips

  • Shoot from the base of the hill looking up at the lighthouse against the sky — the classic Batanes composition.
  • Include the rolling green hills in your frame — the combination of white lighthouse, green hills, and blue sky is iconic.
  • Golden hour is essential — the warm light transforms the landscape. Plan to be at the lighthouse at least 30 minutes before sunset.
  • Wide-angle lens recommended — the panoramic view from the top requires a wide field of view to capture fully.
  • Drone photography is popular in Batanes — check local regulations and be mindful of other visitors.
📌

Practical Tips

  • Bring cash. Batanes has very limited ATM access. Withdraw enough pesos in Manila before your flight.
  • Pack a windbreaker. Batanes is known for strong winds — even on sunny days, the hilltop can be breezy and cool.
  • Respect the Ivatan culture. The indigenous Ivatan people have a rich and unique culture. Be respectful, ask before photographing locals, and support local businesses.
  • Book accommodation early. Batanes has limited guesthouses and homestays. Book months in advance for peak season.
  • Hire a local guide. Local guides provide invaluable context about Ivatan history, culture, and the significance of each landmark.
Visitor Guide

How to Experience Basco Lighthouse

A complete guide to the lighthouse and a suggested Batan Island day tour combining the best of Batanes in one unforgettable day.

At the Lighthouse
Step 1

Climb Naidi Hills

Walk or ride to the base of Naidi Hills and begin the short climb to the lighthouse. The path winds through green grass with the Pacific Ocean coming into view as you ascend. The anticipation builds with every step.

Step 2

The Lighthouse Grounds

Explore the lighthouse grounds — the white tower, the keeper's quarters, and the surrounding grassy hilltop. The structure is well-maintained and the grounds are open to visitors. Walk around the base for different perspectives.

Step 3

360 Panoramic View

Take in the full panorama from the hilltop — the Pacific Ocean to the east, the South China Sea to the west, the rolling green hills of Batan Island, and Basco town below. On exceptionally clear days, the outline of Taiwan is visible to the north.

Step 4

Sunset Watch

Return for sunset — the lighthouse glows golden against the darkening sky, and the hills turn amber and green. This is the most photographed moment in all of Batanes. Arrive at least 30 minutes before sunset to find your spot.

Batan Island Tour
Morning

Marlboro Hills (Rakuh-a-Idi)

Start your day at the rolling green hills of Marlboro — one of the most dramatic landscapes in the Philippines. Ride a horse across the hills for the full experience. The view of the Pacific from the hilltop is breathtaking.

Mid-Morning

Honesty Coffee Shop

Stop at the famous Honesty Coffee Shop in Ivana — an unmanned store where visitors pay on the honor system. A unique and heartwarming testament to the trust and integrity of the Ivatan people.

Afternoon

Basco Town & Heritage Sites

Explore Basco town — the Ivatan Heritage Village, the old Spanish-era stone houses, and the local market. Try traditional Ivatan food: uvud balls (banana pith), luis (pork preserved in fat), and coconut crab.

Evening

Sunset at Basco Lighthouse

End your day at the lighthouse for the golden hour. The combination of the white tower, the green hills, and the blazing sky is the defining image of Batanes — and one of the most beautiful sunsets in the Philippines.
