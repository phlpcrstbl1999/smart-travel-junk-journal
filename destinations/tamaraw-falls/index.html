Introduction

Puerto Galera's Most Famous Waterfall

Tamaraw Falls is the most iconic waterfall in Oriental Mindoro — a powerful 30-meter cascade on the Tamaraw River, set in lush tropical forest about 15 km from Puerto Galera town along the road to Calapan. The falls are named after the Tamaraw (Bubalus mindorensis) — the critically endangered dwarf buffalo endemic to Mindoro — a reminder of the island's extraordinary natural heritage.

The falls plunge into a wide, deep natural pool of cool, clear mountain water — one of the most refreshing swimming spots in Oriental Mindoro. The surrounding forest is lush and green, and the sound of the cascade echoing through the trees creates a peaceful, immersive natural atmosphere. The site is managed as a tourist park with basic facilities — changing rooms, picnic areas, and a small entrance fee.

Tamaraw Falls is most commonly visited as a half-day trip from White Beach — a 30-minute ride by tricycle or motorbike along the scenic coastal road. It pairs perfectly with a Puerto Galera beach weekend, offering a refreshing inland contrast to the beach and diving scene. The road to the falls also passes through beautiful Mindoro countryside — rice fields, coconut plantations, and mountain views.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From White Beach to Tamaraw Falls

Tamaraw Falls is about 15 km from Puerto Galera town along the road to Calapan — easily reached by tricycle or motorbike from White Beach.

01

Rent a Motorbike from White Beach

The most popular way to reach Tamaraw Falls is by renting a motorbike from White Beach — available from numerous rental shops for about ₱400–₱600 per day. The 30-minute ride along the coastal road is scenic and enjoyable.

Motorbike rental: ~₱400–₱600/day  |  Ride: ~30 minutes
02

Tricycle from Puerto Galera

Take a tricycle from Puerto Galera town or White Beach to Tamaraw Falls. Negotiate a round-trip fare including waiting time — about ₱300–₱500 for the round trip. The driver will wait while you visit the falls.

Round-trip tricycle: ~₱300–₱500  |  Allow 2₱3 hours total
03

Enter the Tourist Park

Pay the entrance fee at the gate. The falls are a short walk from the entrance — about 5 minutes down a paved path. Changing rooms and picnic areas are available inside. The natural pool at the base of the falls is the main swimming area.

Entrance: ~₱30–₱50  |  Natural pool for swimming  |  Changing rooms available
04

Combine with Mount Malasimbo

On the return journey to White Beach, stop at Mount Malasimbo for the panoramic view of Puerto Galera Bay — one of the most beautiful views in Oriental Mindoro. The viewpoint is accessible by motorbike or tricycle.

Mount Malasimbo viewpoint: ~15 min from the falls  |  Panoramic bay views

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

📌

Best Time to Visit

  • Year-round: Tamaraw Falls is accessible year-round. The falls are most powerful during and after the rainy season (JuneOctober) but the road can be slippery on a motorbike in wet conditions.
  • Dry season (NovMay): The best time for the motorbike ride and swimming — dry roads, clear skies, and manageable water levels in the pool.
  • Morning visits: Visit in the morning before the afternoon heat — the forest is cooler and the falls are less crowded. Most Puerto Galera visitors go in the afternoon.
  • Combine with White Beach: Tamaraw Falls works best as a half-day trip from White Beach — morning at the falls, afternoon at the beach and diving.
📌

Getting Around

  • Motorbike is best: Renting a motorbike gives you the freedom to stop at viewpoints along the road, visit Mount Malasimbo, and explore the Mindoro countryside at your own pace.
  • Wear a helmet: Helmets are required by law and provided with motorbike rentals. The road to the falls has some steep and winding sections — ride carefully.
  • The road is scenic: The 15 km road from Puerto Galera to the falls passes through beautiful Mindoro countryside — rice fields, coconut plantations, and mountain views. Take your time.
  • Fuel up first: Fill up the motorbike before leaving White Beach — there are no petrol stations along the road to the falls.
📌

At the Falls

  • Swimming is the main activity: The natural pool at the base of the falls is the highlight — cool, clear mountain water perfect for swimming. Bring swimwear and a towel.
  • Don't swim directly under the falls: The force of the water at the base of the 30-meter cascade is powerful. Swim in the wider pool area away from the direct impact zone.
  • Bring snacks: There are small food stalls near the entrance but bringing your own snacks and drinks is recommended. The park has picnic tables.
  • Waterproof your valuables: The mist from the falls reaches the surrounding area. Keep your phone and camera protected.
Mini Itinerary

Half-Day Trip from White Beach

The perfect Puerto Galera half-day — Tamaraw Falls in the morning, Mount Malasimbo viewpoint on the way back, beach and diving in the afternoon.

Morning
8:00 AM

Rent a Motorbike at White Beach

Rent a motorbike from one of the White Beach rental shops. Check the bike's condition and fuel level before departing. The ride to the falls takes about 30 minutes along the scenic coastal road.

8:30 AM

Tamaraw Falls

Arrive at the falls in the cool morning. Pay the entrance fee and walk down to the cascade. Swim in the natural pool at the base — the cool mountain water is a perfect contrast to the warm sea at White Beach.

10:00 AM

Mount Malasimbo Viewpoint

Ride back toward Puerto Galera and stop at the Mount Malasimbo viewpoint — panoramic views of Puerto Galera Bay, the surrounding mountains, and the Verde Island Passage. One of the best views in Oriental Mindoro.

Afternoon
11:30 AM

Return to White Beach

Return the motorbike and head to the beach for lunch at one of the beachfront restaurants. Fresh seafood, cold drinks, and the White Beach atmosphere after a morning in the Mindoro countryside.

2:00 PM

Afternoon Dive or Snorkel

Book an afternoon dive with one of the White Beach dive shops — the coral gardens around White Beach are excellent for a relaxed afternoon dive after the morning waterfall trip.
