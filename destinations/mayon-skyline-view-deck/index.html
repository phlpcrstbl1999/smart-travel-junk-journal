Introduction & About

The Closest You Can Get

The Mayon Skyline View Deck sits on a ridge in Legazpi City, offering the closest road-accessible view of Mayon Volcano — the Philippines' most active volcano and the world's most geometrically perfect volcanic cone. From the deck, Mayon's 2,463-meter summit rises directly above you, filling the sky in a way that no photograph can fully capture.

The view deck is part of the Legazpi City Tourism Complex and sits at an elevation that places you well above the surrounding lowlands, giving a panoramic view that takes in not just Mayon but also the Albay Gulf, the surrounding towns, and on clear days, the distant islands of San Miguel Bay. It is the single most dramatic viewpoint in the Bicol Region.

Mayon is an active stratovolcano with over 50 recorded eruptions since 1616 — the most recent major eruption occurring in 2018. PHIVOLCS monitors the volcano continuously and issues alert levels that determine how close visitors can safely approach. The Skyline View Deck is within the safe zone at normal alert levels and provides an unmatched close-up perspective of the volcano's slopes, lava channels, and summit crater.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to the Skyline View Deck

The Mayon Skyline View Deck is in Legazpi City, Albay — the main gateway to the Mayon Volcano area, reachable by flight or overnight bus from Manila.

01

Fly to Legazpi City

Fly from Manila to Legazpi Airport. Philippine Airlines and Cebu Pacific operate this route daily (~1 hour). From the airport, take a tricycle or taxi to Legazpi City proper (~15 minutes).

Flight: ~1 hr  |  ~₱1,500₱3,500 one-way
02

Alternatively: Overnight Bus

Philtranco and DLTB Co. operate overnight buses from Manila to Legazpi City (~10₱12 hours). An affordable option — book a deluxe seat for comfort on the long ride through the Bicol corridor.

Bus: ~10₱12 hrs  |  ~₱700–₱1,100 one-way
03

Tricycle to the View Deck

From Legazpi City center, hire a tricycle to the Mayon Skyline View Deck. The ride takes about 15₱20 minutes up the ridge road. Ask your driver to wait — the view deck is a short visit and you'll need a ride back down.

Tricycle: ~15₱20 min  |  ~₱100–₱150 (waiting included)
04

Check Alert Levels Before Visiting

Always check PHIVOLCS (Philippine Institute of Volcanology and Seismology) for Mayon's current alert level before visiting. At Alert Level 3 or above, access to the view deck and surrounding areas may be restricted or closed for safety.

PHIVOLCS: phivolcs.dost.gov.ph  |  Check alert level daily

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

🌞

Best Time to Visit

  • Early morning (6:00₱9:00 AM): Mayon is most likely to be cloud-free in the early morning before convective clouds build around the summit. Arrive by 7 AM for the best chance of a clear view.
  • November — April (Dry Season): Best overall conditions. Clear skies and low humidity give the sharpest views of the cone.
  • After rain: The air is washed clean after a rain shower — Mayon often appears exceptionally clear and vivid in the hour after rainfall.
  • Avoid midday: Clouds typically build around the summit by 10₱11 AM. Plan your visit for early morning.
📷

Photography Tips

  • Telephoto lens compresses the distance and makes Mayon's cone appear even more dramatic and imposing.
  • Wide-angle lens captures the full panorama — Mayon, the Albay Gulf, and the surrounding landscape in a single frame.
  • Golden hour (sunrise/sunset) bathes the cone in warm light and creates dramatic shadows on the volcano's flanks.
  • Night photography: On clear nights, Mayon's glowing summit crater is visible — a spectacular long-exposure subject.
⚠

Safety & Practical Tips

  • Always check PHIVOLCS alert levels before visiting. The view deck is safe at Alert Level 0₱2 but may be closed at higher levels.
  • Bring a jacket — the ridge is exposed and can be windy and cool, especially in the early morning.
  • Combine with Cagsawa and Daraga — all three Mayon viewpoints are within 15 km of each other and easily covered in a single day.
  • Binoculars enhance the experience significantly — you can see the lava channels and summit crater in detail on a clear day.
Mini Itinerary

The Ultimate Mayon Volcano Day

Hit all three of Albay's best Mayon viewpoints in a single day — Cagsawa at sunrise, Daraga Church mid-morning, and the Skyline View Deck before lunch.

Morning
5:45 AM

Cagsawa Ruins at Sunrise

Start at the Cagsawa Ruins for the iconic sunrise shot — the buried bell tower with Mayon glowing in the background. The ground-level perspective here is unique and deeply atmospheric.

8:00 AM

Daraga Church

Take a tricycle to Daraga Church for the elevated hilltop view of Mayon framed by the Baroque facade. Walk the church grounds and enjoy the mid-morning light on the volcanic stonework.

10:00 AM

Mayon Skyline View Deck

Head to the Skyline View Deck for the closest road-accessible view of Mayon. The cone fills the sky above you — a scale that is genuinely overwhelming in person. Bring binoculars to see the summit crater and lava channels in detail.

Afternoon
12:00 PM

Lunch in Legazpi City

Eat at a local restaurant in Legazpi City. Order the full Bicolano spread — Bicol Express, laing, pinangat, and kinunot (shark in coconut milk). Albay's cuisine is one of the most distinctive in the Philippines.

2:00 PM

Legazpi City Exploration

Explore Legazpi City — visit the Legazpi City Museum, browse the Embarcadero de Legazpi waterfront mall, or take a tricycle to the Lignon Hill Nature Park for another Mayon viewpoint and a zipline over the forest.

5:00 PM

Legazpi Boulevard Sunset

End the day at Legazpi Boulevard along the waterfront. Watch Mayon turn shades of orange, pink, and purple as the sun sets behind you. The volcano's silhouette against the evening sky is one of the most beautiful sights in the Philippines.
