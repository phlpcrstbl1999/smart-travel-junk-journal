Introduction

Bataan's Most Dramatic Coastline

Located in the municipality of Morong, Bataan, Five Fingers Cove (also known as Limang Daliri) is a unique coastal formation where five rocky limestone peninsulas extend into the South China Sea, creating a series of sheltered coves, hidden beaches, and dramatic viewpoints. The formation is best appreciated from above — from the hilltop viewpoint, the five "fingers" are clearly visible, each one a narrow rocky ridge covered in vegetation jutting into the blue sea.

The coves between the fingers are sheltered from the open sea, creating calm, clear waters perfect for swimming and snorkeling. The rocky peninsulas themselves offer dramatic walking trails with views of the South China Sea, the Bataan coastline, and on clear days, the distant outline of Corregidor Island. The combination of dramatic geology, clear water, and relative seclusion makes Five Fingers Cove one of the most rewarding coastal destinations in Bataan.

Five Fingers Cove is still relatively undiscovered compared to more famous Philippine beach destinations, which means the coves are often uncrowded and the natural environment is well-preserved. The area is managed by the local community and a small entrance fee supports maintenance and conservation efforts.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Five Fingers Cove

Five Fingers Cove is located in Morong, Bataan — about 130 km west of Manila. It's about 30₱40 minutes from Bagac (Las Casas) and 1 hour from Balanga City.

01

Drive from Manila

Drive from Manila via NLEXSCTEX to Bataan. From the SCTEX exit, follow the road toward Morong municipality. The drive takes about 2.5₱3 hours. Having your own vehicle is the most convenient way to reach Five Fingers Cove and combine it with other Bataan attractions.

Drive: ~2.5₱3 hours  |  Morong, Bataan
02

Bus to Balanga then Van to Morong

Take a bus from Cubao or Pasay to Balanga City (2.5₱3 hours), then hire a van or jeepney to Morong municipality — about 45₱60 minutes from Balanga. From Morong town, take a tricycle to Five Fingers Cove.

Bus to Balanga: ~2.5₱3 hrs  |  Van to Morong: ~45₱60 min
03

Register at the Entrance

Register at the community-managed entrance and pay the small environmental fee. Local guides are available and recommended for the hilltop viewpoint trail and the cove exploration. The guides know the best swimming spots and viewpoints.

Entrance fee: ~₱50  |  Guide: ~₱150–₱200
04

Combine with Bataan Day Tour

Five Fingers Cove is the perfect afternoon destination after visiting Mount Samat in the morning and Las Casas at midday. The scenic coastal drive from Bagac to Morong along the Bataan coast is beautiful in itself.

Combine with Mount Samat & Las Casas  |  Full day recommended

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Visit

🌞

Best Time to Visit

  • November — May (Dry Season): Best time — calm seas, clear water, and sunny skies. Ideal for swimming, snorkeling, and the hilltop viewpoint.
  • Sunrise & Sunset: The five fingers formation is most dramatic at golden hour — the rocky peninsulas glow warm orange and the sea turns gold.
  • Low Tide: Visit at low tide to explore the rocky formations between the fingers and access some of the smaller hidden coves.
  • Avoid typhoon season (June — October): Rough seas and strong winds make the coves unsafe for swimming and the viewpoint trails slippery.
📌

Activities

  • Hilltop viewpoint: The best way to appreciate the five fingers formation is from the hilltop — a short but steep trail with a dramatic payoff.
  • Swimming in the coves: The sheltered coves between the fingers have calm, clear water perfect for swimming. Check with your guide for the safest spots.
  • Snorkeling: The rocky formations create diverse marine habitats — bring your own gear for the best experience.
  • Photography: The aerial-like view from the hilltop, the dramatic rock formations, and the clear blue water make Five Fingers one of the most photogenic spots in Bataan.
🎒

What to Pack

  • Swimwear — the coves are perfect for swimming.
  • Trekking shoes for the hilltop viewpoint trail — the path is rocky and steep.
  • Sunscreen and hat — the viewpoint and coves are exposed to the sun.
  • Snorkeling gear — rental may not be available at the site.
  • Packed lunch and water — food options near the cove are limited.
  • Cash — no ATMs near the cove. Withdraw in Balanga or Bagac before heading out.
Visitor Guide

How to Experience Five Fingers Cove

A complete guide to the cove and the perfect Bataan day tour using Five Fingers as your scenic finale.

At the Cove
Step 1

Hilltop Viewpoint

Begin with the hilltop viewpoint trail — a short but steep climb that rewards you with the defining view of Five Fingers Cove. From above, the five rocky peninsulas extending into the blue South China Sea are clearly visible. Take your time at the top.

Step 2

Explore the Peninsulas

Descend and walk along the rocky peninsulas with your guide. The views from the tips of the fingers — looking back at the coastline and out to the open sea — are dramatic and different from every angle.

Step 3

Swimming in the Coves

Find the best swimming cove with your guide and spend time in the clear, calm water. The sheltered coves are perfect for a relaxed swim after the viewpoint hike. Snorkel around the rocky formations for marine life.

Step 4

Sunset from the Viewpoint

Return to the hilltop viewpoint for sunset. The five fingers formation at golden hour — with the South China Sea turning gold and the sky blazing — is one of the most beautiful natural scenes in Bataan.

Full Bataan Day
Morning

Mount Samat National Shrine

Start with the war memorial — diorama museum, memorial wall, and the 92-meter cross observation deck. A solemn and powerful start to the day.

Midday

Las Casas Filipinas de Acuzar

Heritage tour of the restored colonial houses, calesa ride, and lunch at a heritage restaurant. Allow 4₱5 hours for the full experience.

Afternoon

Five Fingers Cove

End your Bataan day at Five Fingers Cove — viewpoint, swimming, and sunset. The perfect scenic close to a day of history and heritage.
