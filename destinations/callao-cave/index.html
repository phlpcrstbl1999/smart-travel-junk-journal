Introduction & History

The Cave with a Chapel Inside

Located in Peablanca, Cagayan — about 24 km from Tuguegarao City — Callao Cave is the most famous cave in the Cagayan Valley region and one of the most visited natural attractions in northern Luzon. The cave system consists of seven interconnected chambers, each with its own character — towering stalactites, massive stalagmites, and cathedral-like spaces that inspire awe at every turn.

The most iconic feature of Callao Cave is its first chamber, which has a large natural opening in the ceiling that allows sunlight to stream in — creating a dramatic, ethereal light show on the cave floor. This natural skylight has made the first chamber the site of an active Roman Catholic chapel, where mass is held regularly. The combination of natural limestone formations and religious devotion creates one of the most unique spiritual spaces in the Philippines.

Callao Cave is also of significant archaeological importance — fossil remains of the Homo luzonensis, a previously unknown species of ancient human, were discovered here in 2007 and formally described in 2019. The cave sits within the Peablanca Protected Landscape and Seascape, a protected area that also encompasses the Pinacanauan River and surrounding karst formations.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Callao Cave

Callao Cave is located in Peablanca, Cagayan, about 24 km from Tuguegarao City — the capital of Cagayan province and the main gateway to the region.

01

Fly Manila to Tuguegarao

The fastest option is to fly from NAIA Manila to Tuguegarao Airport. Philippine Airlines and Cebu Pacific operate daily flights. Flight time is about 1 hour. From the airport, Callao Cave is about 30₱40 minutes by van or tricycle.

Flight: ~1 hour  |  ~₱1,500₱4,000 one-way
02

Bus from Manila to Tuguegarao

Dominion Bus Lines and Florida Bus operate overnight buses from Cubao, Quezon City to Tuguegarao City. Travel time is about 9₱10 hours. Buses depart in the evening and arrive in the morning.

Travel time: ~9₱10 hours  |  ~₱700–₱1,000 one-way
03

Tuguegarao to Peablanca

From Tuguegarao City, take a jeepney or van to Peablanca town (about 20₱30 minutes). From Peablanca, hire a tricycle to Callao Cave — about 10 minutes. Alternatively, hire a van from Tuguegarao directly to the cave.

Jeepney to Peablanca: ~₱30  |  Tricycle to cave: ~₱50–₱80
04

Arrive at Callao Cave

The cave entrance is reached by climbing 184 steps up the limestone cliff face. The steps are well-maintained with handrails. At the top, register and pay the entrance fee before entering the cave. Guides are available and recommended.

Entrance fee: ~₱50–₱100  |  Guide: ~₱150–₱200

Essential Visitor Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Visit

🌞

Best Time to Visit

  • October — May (Dry Season): Best time — the Pinacanauan River is calm and clear, making the boat ride to the cave more enjoyable.
  • Morning visits (8:00₱10:00 AM): The natural skylight in the first chamber creates the most dramatic light effects in the morning when the sun is at the right angle.
  • Weekdays: Far fewer crowds than weekends. The cave is more peaceful and atmospheric without tour groups.
  • Avoid June — September: Typhoon season can cause the Pinacanauan River to flood, making access difficult or impossible.
📌

Inside the Cave

  • Hire a local guide. The cave has seven chambers and the deeper sections require a guide for safety and navigation.
  • Bring a flashlight or headlamp for the deeper chambers — lighting is limited beyond the first few chambers.
  • Wear non-slip shoes. The cave floor is wet and slippery in many sections. Sandals and flip-flops are not safe.
  • Don't touch the formations. Stalactites and stalagmites take thousands of years to form — even touching them can damage them permanently.
  • Respect the chapel. The first chamber has an active chapel — be quiet and respectful if a mass is in progress.
📌

The River Experience

  • Take the boat ride on the Pinacanauan River to reach the cave — it's a scenic and memorable approach through the jungle.
  • Swimming in the river is possible near the cave — the water is clear and refreshing, especially after exploring the cave.
  • Combine with Palaui Island — Callao Cave and Palaui Island are both in Cagayan and can be combined in a multi-day Cagayan itinerary.
  • Bring cash — there are no ATMs near the cave. Withdraw in Tuguegarao before heading out.
Visitor Guide

How to Experience Callao Cave

A complete guide to exploring all seven chambers and combining the cave with the best of Cagayan in one unforgettable trip.

At the Cave
Step 1

Climb the 184 Steps

The approach to the cave entrance involves climbing 184 steps carved into the limestone cliff. The climb takes about 10₱15 minutes and offers increasingly dramatic views of the Pinacanauan River and the surrounding jungle below.

Step 2

First Chamber — The Chapel

Enter the first and most famous chamber. The natural skylight in the ceiling floods the space with light, illuminating the limestone formations and the small chapel below. If mass is not in progress, explore the altar and the surrounding formations.

Step 3

Chambers 2₱4 — The Heart of the Cave

Follow your guide deeper into the cave. Chambers 2 through 4 feature increasingly dramatic formations — massive stalactites hanging from the ceiling, towering stalagmites rising from the floor, and narrow passages connecting the chambers.

Step 4

Chambers 5₱7 — The Deep Chambers

The deeper chambers require a flashlight and a guide. Chamber 7 is the most remote and least visited — a vast, silent space where the only sounds are dripping water and the distant calls of cave swiftlets nesting in the ceiling.

Step 5

River Swimming & Boat Ride

After the cave, descend to the Pinacanauan River for a swim in the clear, cool water. Take a boat ride along the river for a different perspective of the limestone cliffs and jungle. A perfect end to the cave experience.

Cagayan Day Trip
Morning

Callao Cave

Start early at Callao Cave to catch the morning light in the first chamber. Explore all seven chambers with a guide, then swim in the Pinacanauan River before heading out.

Afternoon

Tuguegarao City

Return to Tuguegarao for lunch. Visit the Tuguegarao Cathedral and the Cagayan Museum. Try local Cagayan dishes — pancit batil patong (Cagayan's signature noodle dish) is a must.

Next Day

Palaui Island

Extend your trip to Palaui Island — one of the most pristine and beautiful islands in the Philippines. About 2₱3 hours from Tuguegarao via Santa Ana. A perfect complement to the cave experience.
