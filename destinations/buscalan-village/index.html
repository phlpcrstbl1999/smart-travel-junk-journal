Introduction & Overview

A Living Legend of Indigenous Art

Buscalan Village is a remote Kalinga community in Tinglayan, Kalinga, perched on a mountain ridge above the Chico River. It is home to Whang-Od Oggay — the world's oldest known practitioner of traditional Kalinga tattooing (mambabatok), born around 1917 and still actively tattooing visitors.

Whang-Od uses the traditional hand-tapping method — a thorn from a pomelo tree attached to a bamboo stick, dipped in charcoal ink, and tapped into the skin by hand. The designs are traditional Kalinga patterns — geometric motifs that historically marked warriors, headhunters, and community status. Today, Whang-Od and her apprentices (her grandnieces) continue this living art form for visitors from around the world.

Visiting Buscalan is not just about getting a tattoo — it is an immersion in Kalinga culture, a journey through spectacular mountain scenery, and an encounter with one of the most remarkable human beings alive. The village itself, the community, and the landscape are all extraordinary.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Buscalan Village

Buscalan is in Tinglayan, Kalinga — a remote mountain village requiring a bus to Tabuk, a jeepney to Tinglayan, and a 2-hour trek to the village.

01

Bus to Tabuk City, Kalinga

Take a bus from Manila to Tabuk City, Kalinga (~10₱12 hours). Tabuk is the capital of Kalinga and the staging point for the Buscalan trip.

Bus: ~10₱12 hrs  |  ~₱600–₱900
02

Jeepney to Tinglayan

From Tabuk, take a jeepney to Tinglayan municipality (~3₱4 hours). The road follows the Chico River through dramatic mountain gorges. Tinglayan is the jump-off point for Buscalan.

Jeepney: ~₱150–₱250  |  — ~3₱4 hrs
03

Trek to Buscalan Village

From Tinglayan, a 2-hour trek climbs to Buscalan Village on the mountain ridge. The trail is steep but well-marked. Hire a local guide from Tinglayan — they are essential for the first visit.

Trek: ~2 hrs  |  Guide: ~₱300–₱500
04

Stay Overnight in Buscalan

Several homestays in Buscalan Village offer basic accommodation. Staying overnight allows you to experience the village at dawn and dusk, and gives you more time with Whang-Od and the community. Book in advance during peak season.

Homestay: ~₱300–₱500/night  |  Meals included

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

📌

Getting a Tattoo

  • Queue early: Whang-Od and her apprentices see many visitors. Arrive early and queue patiently — the process cannot be rushed.
  • Whang-Od vs. apprentices: Whang-Od herself may only do small designs due to her age. Her grandnieces Grace and Elyang are also highly skilled mambabatok.
  • The process: Traditional hand-tapping takes longer than machine tattooing and is more painful. Small designs take 30₱60 minutes.
  • Aftercare: Follow the aftercare instructions given by the artist. The tattoo will need proper care for the first few weeks.
📌

Trekking Tips

  • Physical fitness required: The 2-hour trek to Buscalan is steep and demanding. Wear sturdy footwear and bring plenty of water.
  • Hire a guide from Tinglayan: Local guides know the trail and can introduce you to the community. They also provide important income for the area.
  • Pack light: You'll be carrying your pack up a steep mountain. Leave non-essentials at your Tabuk accommodation.
  • Respect the community: Buscalan is a living village, not a tourist attraction. Be respectful, ask permission before photographing, and follow community guidelines.
🎒

What to Pack & Know

  • Cash only — bring enough from Manila or Tabuk. There are no ATMs in Tinglayan or Buscalan.
  • Donation to the community: Beyond the tattoo fee, consider buying local products (woven textiles, handicrafts) to support the community economy.
  • Combine with Chico River: The Chico River rafting and Buscalan Village make a natural 3-day Kalinga adventure.
  • Sleeping Beauty Mountain: The iconic mountain is visible from Buscalan — arrange a trek with a local guide.
Mini Itinerary

2 Days in Buscalan

A proper Buscalan trip requires 2 days — one for the journey and tattoo, one for the village experience and return.

Day 1
Morning

Jeepney to Tinglayan

Take the morning jeepney from Tabuk to Tinglayan (~3₱4 hours). The journey along the Chico River gorge is spectacular.

Afternoon

Trek to Buscalan

Trek from Tinglayan to Buscalan Village (~2 hours). Arrive, check into your homestay, and meet the community.

Late Afternoon

Tattoo Session

Queue for your tattoo session with Whang-Od or her apprentices. The experience of receiving a traditional Kalinga tattoo in the village where the art was born is profound.

Day 2
Morning

Village Life & Sunrise

Wake early for sunrise over the Kalinga mountains from the Buscalan ridge. Spend the morning exploring the village, talking to community members, and buying local handicrafts.

Afternoon

Trek Back & Return

Trek back to Tinglayan and take the jeepney to Tabuk. Return to Manila or continue to the next Cordillera destination.
