Introduction & Overview

The Quiet Wonder of Hungduan

The Hapao Rice Terraces are located in Hungduan, Ifugao — one of the five clusters of the Ifugao Rice Terraces UNESCO World Heritage Site. Unlike the more famous Banaue and Batad clusters, Hapao receives far fewer visitors, offering a more intimate and authentic experience of the living terrace landscape.

The Hapao terraces are notable for their natural hot spring — a warm mineral spring that flows through the terrace area, where visitors can soak in the warm water surrounded by the ancient rice terraces. The combination of the terraces, the hot spring, and the surrounding Ifugao mountain landscape makes Hapao a uniquely rewarding destination.

The terraces are actively farmed by the local Ifugao community, and visiting during planting or harvest season offers the opportunity to see the terraces in their most vibrant and culturally active state. Local guides from the community provide context about the terrace culture and the Ifugao way of life.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Hapao Rice Terraces

Hapao is in Hungduan, Ifugao — about 30 km from Banaue. It is best visited as a day trip from Banaue or combined with the Batad terraces.

01

Overnight Bus to Banaue

Take an overnight bus from Manila to Banaue, Ifugao (~9₱10 hours). Banaue is the hub for all Ifugao terrace visits.

Bus: ~9₱10 hrs  |  ~₱500–₱700
02

Jeepney to Hungduan

From Banaue, hire a jeepney to Hungduan municipality (~1₱1.5 hours). The road passes through scenic Ifugao mountain terrain. Ask your driver to take you to the Hapao terrace area.

Jeepney: ~₱300–₱500  |  — ~1₱1.5 hrs
03

Walk Through the Terraces

Explore the Hapao terraces on foot with a local guide. The terrace paths wind through the active rice fields, offering close-up views of the stone walls, irrigation channels, and the farming community at work.

Walk: ~1₱2 hrs  |  Local guide recommended
04

Soak in the Hot Spring

After the terrace walk, soak in the Hapao Hot Spring — a natural warm mineral spring flowing through the terrace area. The combination of warm water and the surrounding terrace landscape is uniquely relaxing.

Hot spring: ~₱50–₱100 entrance  |  Bring swimwear

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

🌞

Best Time to Visit

  • March — May (Planting Season): Flooded terraces create mirror-like reflections — the most photogenic time.
  • June — August: Vivid green rice growing in the terraces — lush and beautiful.
  • October — November (Harvest): Golden rice ready for harvest — warm colors and active community life.
  • Combine with Batad: Visit Hapao and Batad on the same Ifugao trip for the two most contrasting terrace experiences.
📌

Hot Spring Tips

  • Bring swimwear — the hot spring is a proper soaking pool. Swimwear is required.
  • Best after the terrace walk: Soak in the hot spring after walking the terraces — the warm water is a perfect reward for tired legs.
  • Morning visits: The hot spring is less crowded in the morning. Afternoon visits can get busy with local visitors.
  • The water temperature is warm but not scalding — comfortable for extended soaking.
🎒

What to Pack & Know

  • Cash only — no ATMs in Hungduan. Bring enough from Banaue.
  • Swimwear and towel for the hot spring.
  • Sturdy footwear for the terrace paths — the stone walls can be slippery.
  • Ifugao handicrafts: Buy woven textiles and wood carvings from local artisans — supporting the community directly.
Mini Itinerary

A Day at Hapao

Combine Hapao Rice Terraces with the hot spring and a return visit to Banaue for a complete Ifugao day.

Morning
8:00 AM

Depart Banaue for Hungduan

Take a jeepney from Banaue to Hungduan (~1₱1.5 hours). The mountain road offers beautiful views of the Ifugao highlands.

9:30 AM

Hapao Terrace Walk

Walk through the Hapao terraces with a local guide. The terraces are actively farmed — you may see Ifugao farmers at work, maintaining the stone walls and irrigation channels.

11:30 AM

Hapao Hot Spring

Soak in the Hapao Hot Spring — warm mineral water surrounded by rice terraces. A uniquely relaxing experience in an extraordinary setting.

Afternoon
1:00 PM

Lunch in Hungduan

Have lunch at a local eatery in Hungduan. Try traditional Ifugao dishes — pinikpikan (Cordillera chicken) and etag (smoked pork) are the local specialties.

3:00 PM

Return to Banaue

Take a jeepney back to Banaue. Visit the Banaue Rice Terraces Viewpoint for the classic panoramic view before the evening bus back to Manila.
