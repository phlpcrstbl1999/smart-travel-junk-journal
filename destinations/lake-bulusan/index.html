Introduction & Overview

Sorsogon's Hidden Natural Gem

Lake Bulusan is a volcanic crater lake located within the Bulusan Volcano Natural Park in the municipality of Bulusan, Sorsogon. The lake sits at an elevation of about 600 meters above sea level, surrounded by dense old-growth rainforest that is home to endemic birds, rare orchids, and diverse wildlife.

The lake's surface is remarkably still and reflective, mirroring the surrounding forest and the sky above. A well-maintained trail circles the lake, passing through the rainforest and offering views of the water from multiple angles. The park also has picnic areas, a swimming area, and trails leading deeper into the forest toward the slopes of Bulusan Volcano.

The Bulusan Volcano Natural Park is a protected area and one of the most biodiverse ecosystems in the Bicol Region. It is a haven for birdwatchers, nature photographers, and anyone seeking a peaceful escape into pristine Philippine rainforest.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Lake Bulusan

Lake Bulusan is in Bulusan, Sorsogon — about 50 km from Sorsogon City. It is best reached via Sorsogon City as a base.

01

Fly to Legazpi or Bus to Sorsogon

Fly from Manila to Legazpi Airport (~1 hour) then travel south to Sorsogon City (~2 hours), or take an overnight bus directly to Sorsogon City (~10₱12 hours).

Flight + bus: ~3 hrs total  |  Bus: ~10₱12 hrs
02

Van to Bulusan Town

From Sorsogon City, take a van or bus to Bulusan town (~1.5₱2 hours). Vans depart regularly from the Sorsogon City terminal. The road passes through scenic Sorsogon countryside.

Van: ~₱80–₱120  |  — ~1.5₱2 hrs
03

Tricycle to the Park Entrance

From Bulusan town proper, hire a tricycle to the Bulusan Volcano Natural Park entrance (~15 minutes). Pay the entrance fee and register with the park rangers before proceeding to the lake.

Tricycle: ~₱50–₱80  |  Entrance: ~₱50–₱100
04

Walk to Lake Bulusan

From the park entrance, a short 10₱15 minute walk through the rainforest leads to the lake shore. The circular trail around the lake takes about 45 minutes to complete. Swimming is permitted in designated areas.

Walk: ~10₱15 min  |  Lake circuit: ~45 min

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

📌

Best Time to Visit

  • March — May (Dry Season): Best weather for the lake visit. Clear skies allow the lake to reflect the surrounding forest beautifully.
  • Early morning: The lake is most serene and mirror-still in the early morning before the wind picks up. Mist often hangs over the water at dawn.
  • Check Bulusan Volcano's alert level before visiting — PHIVOLCS issues alerts when the volcano is active. At alert level 2 or above, access may be restricted.
  • Avoid heavy rain periods — the trails can become slippery and the forest paths muddy during prolonged rainfall.
🦋

Wildlife & Nature

  • Birdwatching: The park is home to numerous endemic Philippine bird species. Bring binoculars and move quietly along the lake trail for the best sightings.
  • Rare orchids and ferns grow along the forest trails — look carefully at the trees and undergrowth as you walk.
  • Swimming area: A designated swimming area at the lake shore allows visitors to swim in the cool, clear volcanic water.
  • No littering: The park is a protected area. Carry out all waste and respect the natural environment.
🎒

What to Pack & Know

  • Sturdy footwear — the forest trails can be uneven and slippery. Hiking sandals or trail shoes are recommended.
  • Insect repellent — the rainforest has mosquitoes and other insects. Apply before entering the park.
  • Combine with Donsol: Lake Bulusan and Donsol whale sharks are both in Sorsogon — combine them for a 2-day Sorsogon nature adventure.
  • Pili nuts: Buy local pili nut products in Bulusan town — the province is famous for its pili nuts and the local sweets are excellent.
Mini Itinerary

A Day at Lake Bulusan

Combine Lake Bulusan with the Bulusan Volcano trails and a visit to Subic Beach for a perfect Sorsogon nature day.

Morning
6:00 AM

Depart Sorsogon City

Take an early van from Sorsogon City to Bulusan town. The 1.5-hour journey passes through scenic Sorsogon countryside with views of the coast and mountains.

8:00 AM

Lake Bulusan at Dawn

Arrive at the park and walk to Lake Bulusan. The early morning mist over the water and the stillness of the rainforest create an atmosphere of profound peace. Complete the circular lake trail and take in the reflections.

10:00 AM

Forest Trails & Swimming

Explore the forest trails deeper into the park, or swim in the designated lake swimming area. The water is cool and refreshing — a perfect reward after the morning walk.

Afternoon
12:00 PM

Lunch in Bulusan Town

Return to Bulusan town for lunch at a local eatery. Try fresh seafood and local Sorsogon dishes. Buy pili nut sweets and pastries to take home.

2:00 PM

Subic Beach Sorsogon

Drive to Subic Beach in Matnog (~1 hour) for the afternoon. The pristine white sand and clear water are the perfect complement to the morning's rainforest experience.

5:00 PM

Return to Sorsogon City

Head back to Sorsogon City for dinner and your onward journey. A deeply satisfying day combining volcanic lake, rainforest, and beach.
