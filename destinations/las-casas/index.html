Introduction

A Living Museum of Philippine Heritage

Located on a beachfront property in Bagac, Bataan, Las Casas Filipinas de Acuzar is the life's work of entrepreneur and heritage advocate Jose "Jerry" Acuzar. Over decades, Acuzar acquired more than 25 authentic Spanish colonial houses from across the Philippines · many of which were facing demolition — and had them painstakingly dismantled, transported to Bagac, and meticulously restored and reassembled on the beachfront property.

The result is one of the most extraordinary heritage experiences in Southeast Asia. Walking through Las Casas is like walking through 300 years of Philippine architectural history — ancestral houses from Ilocos Norte, Pampanga, Bulacan, and other provinces stand side by side, each one a masterpiece of Spanish colonial craftsmanship. The houses feature original materials — antique capiz shell windows, hand-carved wooden details, coral stone walls, and period furniture — creating an atmosphere of extraordinary authenticity.

Las Casas is both a heritage resort and a living museum. Guests can stay overnight in the restored houses, dine in heritage restaurants, and explore the property by calesa (horse-drawn carriage). Day visitors can join guided tours that explain the history and significance of each house. The property also hosts cultural events, art exhibitions, and heritage workshops throughout the year.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Las Casas

Las Casas Filipinas de Acuzar is located in Bagac, Bataan — about 140 km west of Manila. It's about 1 hour from Balanga City, the capital of Bataan.

01

Drive from Manila

The most convenient way is to drive from Manila via NLEXSCTEX to Bataan. From the SCTEX exit, follow the road to Bagac. The drive takes about 2.5₱3 hours. Having your own vehicle allows you to combine Las Casas with Mount Samat and Five Fingers Cove in one day.

Drive: ~2.5₱3 hours  |  Bagac, Bataan
02

Bus to Balanga then Van to Bagac

Take a bus from Cubao or Pasay to Balanga City (2.5₱3 hours), then hire a van or jeepney to Bagac municipality — about 1 hour from Balanga. From Bagac town, take a tricycle to Las Casas.

Bus to Balanga: ~2.5₱3 hrs  |  Van to Bagac: ~1 hr  |  ~₱300–₱450
03

Book a Day Tour Package

Las Casas offers day tour packages that include a guided tour of the heritage houses, a calesa ride, and lunch. Day tours must be booked in advance through the Las Casas website or by phone. Walk-in day visits may not always be available.

Day tour: ~₱1,500₱2,500 per person  |  Book in advance
04

Stay Overnight

For the full Las Casas experience, stay overnight in one of the restored heritage houses. Waking up in a 200-year-old ancestral home on a Bataan beach is an extraordinary experience. Book well in advance — rooms fill up quickly on weekends.

Overnight rates: ~₱5,000₱15,000/night  |  Book months ahead

Essential Visitor Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Visit

📌

Making the Most of Your Visit

  • Book in advance. Day tours and overnight stays must be reserved ahead of time. Walk-in visits are not always available.
  • Take the guided tour. The guides at Las Casas are knowledgeable and passionate — their stories about each house and its origins bring the heritage to life.
  • Ride the calesa. A horse-drawn carriage ride through the property is one of the most atmospheric ways to experience Las Casas.
  • Allow at least 4₱5 hours for a day visit — there is a lot to see and the property is large. Rushing through it does the heritage a disservice.
📷

Photography Tips

  • Golden hour is magical — the warm light on the colonial facades at sunrise and sunset creates extraordinary photography conditions.
  • The beachfront setting adds a unique dimension — colonial houses with the South China Sea as a backdrop is a composition found nowhere else.
  • Interior photography is allowed in most houses — the capiz shell windows, antique furniture, and period details are extraordinary subjects.
  • Drone photography requires prior permission from the management — inquire when booking your visit.
📌

Dining & Practical Tips

  • Dine at the heritage restaurants on the property — the food is excellent and the setting inside a restored colonial house is extraordinary.
  • Try the Kapampangan and Ilocano dishes — the cuisine reflects the origins of the houses and the regions they came from.
  • Wear comfortable walking shoes — the property is large and involves walking on uneven heritage surfaces.
  • Bring cash — while cards are accepted, some vendors and activities on the property are cash-only.
Visitor Guide

How to Experience Las Casas

A complete guide to the heritage resort and a suggested Bataan day tour.

At Las Casas
Step 1

Arrival & Orientation

Arrive at the property and meet your guide. The orientation gives you an overview of the project — how Acuzar acquired the houses, how they were dismantled and transported, and the restoration process. The scale of the undertaking is immediately impressive.

Step 2

Guided Heritage House Tour

Walk through the restored houses with your guide. Each house has a story — its origin province, its original owners, the family history, and the architectural details that make it unique. The Ilocano houses, the Kapampangan mansions, and the Tagalog ancestral homes each have a distinct character.

Step 3

Calesa Ride

Board a calesa for a horse-drawn carriage ride through the property. The clip-clop of hooves on the cobblestone paths, the colonial facades on either side, and the sea breeze from the beach create an atmosphere of extraordinary historical immersion.

Step 4

Lunch at a Heritage Restaurant

Dine at one of the heritage restaurants on the property. The food is excellent — traditional Filipino dishes served in a restored colonial dining room. The combination of authentic cuisine and authentic setting is one of the highlights of Las Casas.

Bataan Day Tour
Morning

Mount Samat National Shrine

Start your Bataan day at the shrine — the diorama museum, memorial wall, and cross observation deck. Allow 2₱3 hours for a thorough visit.

Midday

Las Casas Filipinas de Acuzar

Head to Las Casas for the guided heritage tour, calesa ride, and lunch. Allow 4₱5 hours for a full day visit experience.

Afternoon

Five Fingers Cove

End your day at Five Fingers Cove — a dramatic coastal formation with stunning views of the South China Sea. A perfect scenic close to a Bataan heritage day.
