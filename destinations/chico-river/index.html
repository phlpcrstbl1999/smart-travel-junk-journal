Introduction & Overview

Kalinga's Sacred River

The Chico River is the primary river of Kalinga province in the Cordillera Administrative Region, flowing from the mountains of the Cordillera through dramatic gorges and valleys before joining the Cagayan River in the lowlands. It is one of the most significant rivers in the Cordillera — both ecologically and culturally.

The river is sacred to the Kalinga people, who have lived along its banks for centuries. In the 1970s, the Kalinga and Bontoc peoples famously resisted the construction of the Chico River Dam — a resistance led by the legendary Kalinga chieftain Macli-ing Dulag that became one of the most celebrated indigenous rights victories in Philippine history.

Today, the Chico River offers whitewater rafting, kayaking, swimming, and river trekking through some of the most spectacular gorge scenery in the Cordillera. The river is also the gateway to the Buscalan Village of the legendary tattoo artist Whang-Od.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to the Chico River

The Chico River is accessible from Tabuk City, the capital of Kalinga province, reached by bus from Manila or Baguio.

01

Bus to Tabuk City, Kalinga

Take a bus from Manila to Tabuk City, Kalinga (~10₱12 hours). Several bus lines serve this route. Tabuk is the capital of Kalinga and the base for Chico River activities.

Bus: ~10₱12 hrs  |  ~₱600–₱900
02

Alternatively: Via Baguio

Travel to Baguio City first, then take a bus or van to Tabuk (~4₱5 hours). This allows you to combine a Baguio visit with the Kalinga trip.

Baguio to Tabuk: ~4₱5 hrs  |  ~₱200–₱350
03

Arrange River Activities

Book whitewater rafting or kayaking through local tour operators in Tabuk. The Chico River has sections suitable for different skill levels — from gentle floats to challenging rapids.

Rafting: ~₱500–₱1,500 per person  |  Guide required
04

Combine with Buscalan Village

The Chico River is the route to Buscalan Village — home of the legendary tattoo artist Whang-Od. Arrange a multi-day trip that combines river activities with the Buscalan village visit.

Buscalan: ~3₱4 hrs from Tabuk by jeepney + trek

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

🌞

Best Time to Visit

  • March — May (Dry Season): Best water levels for rafting and swimming. Clear skies and calm conditions.
  • November — February: Cooler temperatures make river activities more comfortable. Water levels are moderate.
  • Avoid peak rainy season (JulySeptember) — the river can flood and become dangerous for water activities.
  • Combine with Buscalan: Plan a 3-day Kalinga trip combining the Chico River with Buscalan Village and Sleeping Beauty Mountain.
📌

River Activities

  • Whitewater rafting: The Chico River has sections with Class IIIV rapids suitable for different experience levels. Book through accredited operators in Tabuk.
  • Swimming: The river has calm sections with crystal-clear water perfect for swimming. Ask your guide for the safest spots.
  • River trekking: Wade through shallow sections of the river with a guide — a unique way to experience the gorge scenery.
  • Kayaking: Available through some operators in Tabuk for experienced paddlers.
📌

Kalinga Culture & Tips

  • Kalinga hospitality: The Kalinga people are known for their warmth and hospitality. Accept invitations to share food and drink — it is a sign of respect.
  • Traditional tattoos: Kalinga tattooing is a living art form. Visit Buscalan Village to see Whang-Od and her apprentices at work.
  • Cash only — bring enough from Manila or Baguio. ATM access in Kalinga is limited.
  • Respect indigenous customs: Ask permission before photographing community members and follow local guidelines.
Mini Itinerary

3 Days in Kalinga

Combine the Chico River with Buscalan Village and Sleeping Beauty Mountain for a complete Kalinga adventure.

Day 1 — River
Morning

Arrive in Tabuk

Arrive in Tabuk City and check into your accommodation. Arrange your river activities and Buscalan Village trip with a local tour operator.

Afternoon

Chico River Rafting

Spend the afternoon whitewater rafting on the Chico River. The rapids, the gorge scenery, and the crystal-clear water make for an exhilarating experience.

Days 2₱3
Day 2

Buscalan Village

Travel to Buscalan Village to visit Whang-Od and her apprentices. Watch the traditional Kalinga tattooing process and, if you wish, receive a tattoo from the world's oldest mambabatok.

Day 3

Sleeping Beauty Mountain

Trek to Sleeping Beauty Mountain for panoramic views of the Kalinga highlands. The mountain's distinctive silhouette — resembling a reclining figure — is one of the most iconic landscapes in the Cordillera.
