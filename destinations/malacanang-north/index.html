Introduction & History

The Presidential Residence by Paoay Lake

Built in 1977 along the shores of Paoay Lake, the Malacaang of the North served as the official residence of President Ferdinand E. Marcos whenever he visited his home province of Ilocos Norte. The mansion was designed to mirror the grandeur of Malacaang Palace in Manila — hence the name.

The two-story structure spans over 2,500 square meters and features a blend of Spanish colonial and modern Filipino architecture. Its most striking feature is the lakeside location — the mansion sits on stilts over the water, offering panoramic views of Paoay Lake and the surrounding mountains. The interiors are filled with period furniture, vintage photographs, and personal belongings of the Marcos family.

After the 1986 People Power Revolution, the mansion was abandoned and fell into disrepair. In 2011, it was restored and reopened as a public museum by the provincial government of Ilocos Norte. Today, visitors can explore the state rooms, view the vintage car collection, and learn about a controversial chapter of Philippine history through exhibits and guided tours.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Malacaang of the North

Malacaang of the North is located along Paoay Lake in Paoay, Ilocos Norte, about 490 km north of Manila. It's just 3 km from Paoay Church.

01

Fly Manila to Laoag

The fastest route is to fly from NAIA or Clark Airport to Laoag International Airport. Philippine Airlines and Cebu Pacific operate daily flights. From Laoag, Paoay is just a 20-minute tricycle ride away.

Flight: ~1 hour  |  ~₱1,500₱3,500 one-way
02

Take an Overnight Bus from Manila

Partas, Farinas, and Florida Bus Lines run overnight buses from Cubao or Pasay to Laoag City. Buses depart around 9₱10 PM and arrive in Laoag by 7₱8 AM. Book a deluxe seat for comfort.

Travel time: ~10₱12 hours  |  ~₱800–₱1,200 one-way
03

Laoag to Paoay Lake

From Laoag City, take a tricycle or jeepney to Paoay town. Ask the driver to take you to Malacaang of the North along Paoay Lake. You can also rent a tricycle for the day to visit the mansion, Paoay Church, and the sand dunes in one trip.

Tricycle: ~₱150–₱250  |  — ~25₱30 minutes
04

Arrive at the Museum

The mansion is located at Suba, Paoay, right along the lakeshore. There's a parking area and a small visitor center at the entrance. Entry fee is minimal and includes access to all exhibits and the vintage car collection.

Entry: ~₱30–₱50  |  Open TueSun 9:00 AM — 4:00 PM

Essential Visitor Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Visit

🌞

Best Time to Visit

  • Weekday Mornings (9:00₱11:00 AM): Fewer tourists, cooler weather, and better lighting for photography. The museum is quieter and you can explore at your own pace.
  • Late Afternoon (3:00₱4:00 PM): Catch the golden hour light reflecting off Paoay Lake. The lakeside view from the mansion is stunning at sunset.
  • Avoid Mondays: The museum is closed on Mondays for maintenance and cleaning.
  • Peak Season (DecFeb): Expect larger crowds, especially during holidays. Arrive early to beat the tour groups.
📌

What to See Inside

  • State Rooms: The formal living room, dining hall, and presidential office — preserved with original furniture and decor from the 1970s₱80s.
  • Personal Quarters: Bedrooms and private spaces with personal belongings, clothing, and photographs of the Marcos family.
  • Vintage Car Collection: Classic cars including a 1937 Cadillac, Mercedes-Benz, and presidential limousines displayed in the garage.
  • Historical Exhibits: Documents, awards, and memorabilia chronicling President Marcos' political career and the Martial Law era.
  • Lakeside Balcony: Panoramic views of Paoay Lake — perfect for photos and quiet reflection.
📌

Etiquette & Guidelines

  • Photography is allowed in most areas, but no flash inside the state rooms. Some exhibits may have photography restrictions — check with staff.
  • Dress modestly. This is a historical site and museum — avoid beachwear or overly casual attire.
  • Guided tours are available for free or a small tip. Guides provide valuable historical context and stories about the mansion.
  • Don't touch the exhibits. Many items are fragile and irreplaceable. Stay behind ropes and barriers.
  • Be respectful. The site represents a controversial period in Philippine history. Visitors have diverse opinions — keep discussions civil.
Visitor Guide

How to Experience Malacaang of the North

A complete guide to exploring the museum, understanding its history, and visiting nearby attractions in one day.

Museum Tour
Step 1

Entrance & Visitor Center

Pay the entrance fee at the visitor center. Pick up a brochure or ask for a guided tour. Guides are knowledgeable and provide historical context that enriches the experience.

Step 2

Ground Floor: State Rooms

Start with the formal living room, dining hall, and presidential office. Notice the period furniture, chandeliers, and vintage decor — all preserved from the 1970s₱80s. The dining table is set as if waiting for guests.

Step 3

Second Floor: Personal Quarters

Explore the bedrooms and private spaces. See personal belongings, clothing, shoes, and family photographs. The contrast between public grandeur and private intimacy is striking.

Step 4

Vintage Car Collection

Walk to the garage to see the vintage car collection — classic 1930s₱1970s vehicles including presidential limousines, a Cadillac, and a Mercedes-Benz. Car enthusiasts will love this section.

Step 5

Lakeside Balcony & Grounds

End your visit on the lakeside balcony. Take in the panoramic view of Paoay Lake and the surrounding mountains. Walk the grounds and enjoy the peaceful lakeside atmosphere.

Nearby Sites
5 min away

Paoay Lake

Walk along the lakeshore for peaceful views and fresh air. Local fishermen bring in their catch here every evening. Perfect for a quiet stroll or picnic.

10 min away

Paoay Church

A UNESCO World Heritage Site built in 1694. Massive coral-stone buttresses and Earthquake Baroque architecture. One of the most iconic churches in the Philippines.

15 min away

Paoay Sand Dunes

85+ hectares of golden sand dunes. Book a 4x4 ride or try sandboarding. A surreal desert landscape just minutes from the lakeside mansion.

30 min away

Laoag City Heritage Tour

Visit the Sinking Bell Tower, Saint William Cathedral, and the Tobacco Monopoly Monument. Try the famous Laoag empanada at the Empanada Park.
