Introduction & History

The Gibraltar of the East

Located at the entrance of Manila Bay, Corregidor Island — nicknamed "The Rock" — served as the last bastion of Filipino and American resistance against the Japanese invasion during World War II. From January to May 1942, a combined force of Filipino and American soldiers under General Douglas MacArthur and later General Jonathan Wainwright held the island against overwhelming Japanese forces.

The island's most famous feature is the Malinta Tunnel — a massive underground complex carved into the rock that served as headquarters, hospital, and refuge during the siege. The tunnel system stretches for hundreds of meters and contains lateral tunnels that housed thousands of soldiers and civilians during the bombardment. Today, a dramatic sound and light show inside the tunnel recreates the events of the siege.

Corregidor is now a living museum — the ruins of barracks, gun batteries, the Pacific War Memorial, and the Japanese Garden of Peace are all accessible to visitors. The island offers a profound and moving experience of WWII history combined with beautiful views of Manila Bay and the surrounding islands. It is accessible only by ferry from Manila, adding to the sense of journey and discovery.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Corregidor Island

Corregidor Island is located at the mouth of Manila Bay — about 48 km west of Manila. It is accessible only by ferry from the CCP Complex in Manila.

01

Book Your Ferry Tour

Corregidor tours are operated by Sun Cruises from the CCP Complex Ferry Terminal in Manila. Tours must be booked in advance — day tours and overnight packages are available. The ferry departs early morning and returns in the late afternoon.

Ferry from CCP Complex  |  Departs ~7:30 AM  |  Book in advance
02

Ferry Crossing to Corregidor

The ferry crossing takes about 1.5₱2 hours across Manila Bay. The journey itself is scenic — views of the Manila skyline, the bay, and the approaching island. Breakfast is sometimes served on board.

Crossing time: ~1.5₱2 hours  |  Day tour: ~₱1,500–₱2,500 per person
03

Guided Island Tour

Upon arrival, join the guided tram tour of the island — visiting the Malinta Tunnel, the Pacific War Memorial, the ruins of the barracks and gun batteries, the Japanese Garden of Peace, and the lighthouse. The tour takes about 4₱5 hours.

Tram tour included  |  Malinta Tunnel light show included
04

Overnight Option

For a deeper experience, book an overnight package — stay in the island's hotel, watch the sunset over Manila Bay, and explore the island in the early morning before the day-trippers arrive. The island at night is atmospheric and moving.

Overnight package: ~₱3,000–₱5,000 per person  |  Book well in advance

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Visit

🌞

Best Time to Visit

  • November — May (Dry Season): Best time — calm seas for the ferry crossing and comfortable temperatures for the outdoor island tour.
  • May 6 (Corregidor Day): Anniversary of the fall of Corregidor — commemorative ceremonies and special events on the island.
  • Weekdays: Fewer visitors and a more contemplative atmosphere. The island is most moving when it's quiet.
  • Avoid typhoon season (June — October): Rough seas can cancel ferry crossings.
—

What to See

  • Malinta Tunnel: The underground headquarters — the sound and light show recreating the 1942 siege is one of the most powerful historical experiences in the Philippines.
  • Pacific War Memorial: The circular memorial with its eternal flame honoring the Filipino and American soldiers who fought and died on Corregidor.
  • Battery Way & Ruins: The massive gun batteries and the ruins of barracks, officers' quarters, and military installations.
  • Japanese Garden of Peace: A serene garden honoring the Japanese soldiers who also died on the island — a powerful symbol of reconciliation.
📌

Practical Tips

  • Book well in advance — ferry tours fill up quickly, especially on weekends and holidays.
  • Wear comfortable walking shoes — the island tour involves significant walking on uneven terrain.
  • Bring sunscreen and a hat — much of the island tour is outdoors and exposed to the sun.
  • Bring cash — for food, souvenirs, and any additional activities on the island.
  • Approach with solemnity. Corregidor is a war memorial — maintain a respectful atmosphere throughout your visit.
Visitor Guide

How to Experience Corregidor Island

A complete guide to the island and a suggested Cavite heritage day tour.

On the Island
Morning

Ferry Crossing & Arrival

Board the ferry at CCP Complex at 7:30 AM. The 1.5₱2 hour crossing across Manila Bay is scenic and atmospheric. Arrive at Corregidor and begin the guided tram tour of the island.

Mid-Morning

Battery Way & Ruins

Visit the massive gun batteries and the ruins of the military installations — barracks, officers' quarters, and the topside area. The scale of the fortifications gives a sense of the island's strategic importance.

Midday

Malinta Tunnel Light Show

Enter the Malinta Tunnel for the sound and light show — a dramatic recreation of the 1942 siege. The combination of the underground setting, the narration, and the sound effects creates one of the most powerful historical experiences in the Philippines.

Afternoon

Pacific War Memorial & Japanese Garden

Visit the Pacific War Memorial with its eternal flame, then the Japanese Garden of Peace. The contrast between the two memorials — one honoring the defenders, one honoring the attackers — is deeply moving and thought-provoking.

Cavite Day Tour
Morning

Aguinaldo Shrine (Kawit)

Start your Cavite day at the birthplace of Philippine independence — the shrine, the balcony, and the museum. Allow 1.5₱2 hours.

Full Day

Corregidor Island

The Corregidor day tour is a full-day experience — ferry crossing, island tour, Malinta Tunnel, and return ferry. Best done as a dedicated day trip.

Afternoon

Tagaytay Ridge

Drive up to Tagaytay for the Taal Volcano view, fresh highland air, and excellent Cavite cuisine. A scenic and relaxing close to a heritage-rich day.
