Introduction

The Philippines' Most Unique Dining Experience

Villa Escudero Plantations and Resort is one of the most distinctive and beloved heritage destinations in the Philippines · an 800-hectare working coconut plantation in San Pablo, Quezon that has been in the Escudero family since the 1880s. What began as a hacienda has evolved into a world-famous resort that offers guests an immersive experience of 19th-century Filipino plantation life, culminating in the most unique dining experience in the country.

The resort's signature attraction is the Labasin Waterfall Restaurant — a bamboo dining pavilion built directly at the base of a natural waterfall, where guests eat their lunch with their bare feet in the cool, flowing water of the Labasin River. The buffet of traditional Filipino dishes is served while the waterfall cascades just meters away — an experience so distinctive that it has made Villa Escudero one of the most photographed and talked-about dining experiences in Southeast Asia.

Beyond the waterfall lunch, Villa Escudero offers a full day of plantation activities — carabao cart rides through the coconut groves, bamboo rafting on the river, a folk arts museum with one of the finest collections of Philippine antiques and religious art in the country, cultural shows of traditional Filipino dances, and a tour of the working coconut plantation. The resort is available as a day tour or overnight stay.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Villa Escudero

Villa Escudero is in San Pablo, Quezon — about 100 km south of Manila. The drive takes about 2 hours via SLEX.

01

Drive from Manila via SLEX

Drive via SLEX — Sto. Tomas — San Pablo, Quezon. Villa Escudero is well-signposted from the San Pablo area. Having your own vehicle is the most convenient option — it gives you flexibility on timing and allows you to combine with other Quezon destinations.

Manila to Villa Escudero: ~2 hours  |  Large parking area at the resort
02

Book the Day Tour Package

Villa Escudero offers day tour packages that include the waterfall buffet lunch, all plantation activities, the folk arts museum, and cultural shows. Book in advance through the Villa Escudero website — packages sell out on weekends and holidays.

Day tour: ~₱1,500–₱2,000 per person  |  Book at villaescudero.com
03

The Waterfall Lunch

The Labasin Waterfall Restaurant serves a buffet of traditional Filipino dishes — kare-kare, sinigang, lechon, pancit, and more — while you sit with your feet in the cool river water at the base of the waterfall. Remove your shoes at the entrance and wade in.

Buffet lunch: included in day tour  |  Remove shoes — wade in the water
04

Plantation Activities

After lunch, explore the plantation — carabao cart rides through the coconut groves, bamboo rafting on the Labasin River, the folk arts museum (one of the finest collections of Philippine antiques in the country), and the afternoon cultural show of traditional Filipino dances.

Carabao cart  |  Cultural show  |  Folk arts museum

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

📌

Booking & Timing

  • Book in advance: Day tour packages sell out on weekends and holidays. Book through the official Villa Escudero website at least a week in advance for weekends, earlier for peak holidays.
  • Arrive by 10 AM: The day tour typically runs from 10 AM to 5 PM. Arriving early gives you time to explore the grounds before the waterfall lunch service begins at noon.
  • Weekdays are quieter: The resort is significantly less crowded on weekdays — a more relaxed and atmospheric experience, especially at the waterfall restaurant.
  • Overnight stays: Villa Escudero also offers overnight accommodation in traditional nipa huts — a magical experience of waking up in the plantation. Book well in advance.
📌

The Waterfall Experience

  • Remove your shoes: You wade into the waterfall restaurant barefoot — the water is cool, clear, and about ankle-deep at the dining tables. Wear clothes you don't mind getting slightly wet.
  • The buffet: The Filipino buffet is generous and delicious — kare-kare, sinigang, lechon, pancit, fresh coconut dishes, and traditional desserts. Go hungry.
  • Waterproof your valuables: The mist from the waterfall reaches the dining area. Keep your phone and camera in a waterproof bag or pocket when not in use.
  • The waterfall itself: After lunch, you can walk closer to the Labasin Falls for photos — the cascade is beautiful and the surrounding bamboo forest is lush and cool.
📌

Don't Miss

  • Folk Arts Museum: One of the finest collections of Philippine antiques, religious art, and cultural artifacts in the country — santos, antique furniture, colonial silverware, and rare Philippine artworks. Allow at least an hour.
  • Carabao cart ride: A slow, peaceful ride through the coconut plantation on a traditional carabao cart — one of the most quintessentially Filipino experiences available near Manila.
  • Cultural show: The afternoon show features traditional Filipino folk dances — tinikling, singkil, and other regional dances performed by the resort's cultural troupe.
  • Coconut products: Buy fresh coconut products from the plantation store — virgin coconut oil, coconut jam, and other artisanal products made from the estate's own coconuts.
Mini Itinerary

How to Spend a Day at Villa Escudero

A full Villa Escudero day tour — from the plantation grounds in the morning to the cultural show in the afternoon — is one of the most complete and memorable day trips from Manila.

Morning
6:00 AM

Depart Manila

Leave Manila by 6 AM to beat SLEX traffic. The 2-hour drive to San Pablo is pleasant in the early morning. Aim to arrive at Villa Escudero by 9₱10 AM for the start of the day tour.

10:00 AM

Plantation Grounds & Museum

Begin with the Folk Arts Museum — the finest collection of Philippine antiques in the resort. Then explore the plantation grounds, the bamboo groves, and the Labasin River before the lunch service begins.

12:00 PM

Waterfall Buffet Lunch

Remove your shoes and wade into the Labasin Waterfall Restaurant. The Filipino buffet is served while the waterfall cascades beside you and the cool river water flows around your feet. The most unique lunch in the Philippines.

Afternoon
2:00 PM

Carabao Cart & Bamboo Rafting

Take the carabao cart ride through the coconut plantation — a slow, peaceful journey through 800 hectares of coconut palms. Then try bamboo rafting on the Labasin River for a different perspective of the plantation.

3:30 PM

Cultural Show

The afternoon cultural show features traditional Filipino folk dances — tinikling (bamboo pole dance), singkil, and other regional dances. One of the best free cultural performances near Manila.

5:00 PM

Plantation Store & Departure

Browse the plantation store for coconut products and pasalubong before the drive back to Manila. Stop at Kamay ni Hesus in Lucban on the way back for a quick visit to the hilltop shrine.
