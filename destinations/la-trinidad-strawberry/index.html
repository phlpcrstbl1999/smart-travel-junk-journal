Introduction & Overview

The Strawberry Capital of the Philippines

La Trinidad is the capital municipality of Benguet province and the strawberry capital of the Philippines. The cool highland climate of the Cordillera — with temperatures averaging 15₱20°C — creates ideal conditions for growing strawberries, and the rolling hillside farms of La Trinidad have been producing the country's finest strawberries for decades.

The La Trinidad Strawberry Farm allows visitors to walk through the fields and pick fresh strawberries directly from the plants — a hands-on agricultural experience that is especially popular with families and children. The strawberries are sweet, plump, and far superior to anything available in Manila's supermarkets.

Beyond strawberry picking, La Trinidad offers fresh strawberry products — jam, wine, taho, ice cream, and pastries — all made from locally grown fruit. The town is just 15 minutes from Baguio City and is a natural addition to any Baguio itinerary.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Baguio to La Trinidad Strawberry Farm

La Trinidad is just 15 minutes from Baguio City — easily reached by jeepney, taxi, or private vehicle.

01

Get to Baguio City First

Take a bus from Manila to Baguio City (~5₱6 hours). La Trinidad is best visited as a day trip from Baguio — stay in Baguio and make the short trip to the strawberry farm.

Manila to Baguio: ~5₱6 hrs  |  ~₱400–₱600
02

Jeepney from Baguio to La Trinidad

From Baguio City, take a jeepney bound for La Trinidad (~15 minutes). Jeepneys depart regularly from the Baguio City Market area. Ask the driver to drop you at the strawberry farm.

Jeepney: ~₱12–₱15  |  — ~15 min
03

Strawberry Picking

Pay the entrance fee at the farm and receive a container for picking. Walk through the strawberry fields and pick directly from the plants. You pay by weight for what you pick. The experience is best from November to May.

Picking fee: ~₱50–₱100  |  Pay by weight for picked berries
04

Strawberry Products & Pasalubong

After picking, browse the stalls selling strawberry jam, wine, taho, ice cream, and pastries. Buy strawberry jam as pasalubong — La Trinidad jam is the most popular souvenir from the Baguio area.

Strawberry jam: ~₱80–₱150/jar  |  Strawberry ice cream on-site

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

🌞

Best Time for Strawberries

  • November — May (Peak Season): The best time for strawberry picking — the plants are most productive during the cool dry months.
  • January — March: Peak of the strawberry season — the largest, sweetest berries and the most abundant fields.
  • June — October (Off-season): Fewer strawberries available and some fields may be resting. The farm is still open but picking may be limited.
  • Morning visits: Arrive early for the freshest berries and before the afternoon crowds arrive from Baguio.
📌

Farm Visit Tips

  • Wear comfortable shoes — the farm paths can be muddy, especially after rain. Avoid open-toed shoes.
  • Don't eat while picking — you pay for what you pick by weight. Eating in the field is not permitted.
  • Handle the plants gently — the strawberry plants are delicate. Avoid stepping on the rows and handle the fruit carefully.
  • Bring a small bag for your purchases — the farm provides picking containers but you'll want a bag for the products you buy afterward.
📌

What to Buy & Know

  • Strawberry jam: The most popular pasalubong from La Trinidad — buy directly from the farm stalls for the freshest product.
  • Strawberry wine: A unique local product — sweet, fruity, and a great conversation piece back home.
  • Combine with Baguio: La Trinidad is just 15 minutes from Baguio — combine with a full Baguio City itinerary for the complete Benguet experience.
  • Mt. Pulag: The highest peak in Luzon is in Benguet — arrange a trek from Baguio or La Trinidad for the ultimate Cordillera adventure.
Mini Itinerary

A Morning at La Trinidad

Combine La Trinidad Strawberry Farm with Baguio City highlights for a perfect Benguet day.

Morning
7:00 AM

Depart Baguio for La Trinidad

Take an early jeepney from Baguio City to La Trinidad. Arriving early means fresher berries and fewer crowds at the farm.

7:30 AM

Strawberry Picking

Spend the morning picking strawberries in the fields. Take your time walking through the rows, selecting the ripest berries. The cool morning air and the smell of fresh strawberries make this a genuinely delightful experience.

9:30 AM

Strawberry Products & Breakfast

Browse the farm stalls for strawberry jam, wine, and pastries. Have breakfast at a local eatery in La Trinidad — try strawberry taho or fresh strawberries with cream.

Afternoon
11:00 AM

Return to Baguio City

Take a jeepney back to Baguio City and spend the afternoon exploring the city — Burnham Park, Session Road, and the Baguio Public Market.

2:00 PM

Baguio Public Market

Buy more strawberry jam and ube products at the Baguio Public Market — prices are competitive and the selection is excellent. This is the best place for pasalubong shopping.

Evening

Dinner & Session Road

Have dinner on Session Road and enjoy the cool Baguio evening. The city comes alive at night with restaurants, cafes, and street food vendors.
