Introduction & History

Older Than Harvard, the Heart of Philippine Academia

The University of Santo Tomas (UST) was founded on April 28, 1611 by Bishop Miguel de Benavides — making it the oldest existing university in Asia and older than Harvard University (founded 1636). It is operated by the Order of Preachers (Dominicans) and is the largest Catholic university in the world in terms of enrollment.

The university's Main Building (1927) is a magnificent Neo-Baroque structure that dominates the UST campus in Sampaloc, Manila. The campus also contains the Arch of the Centuries — a 17th-century stone arch built in 1611, the oldest existing arch in the Philippines · and the UST Museum of Arts and Sciences, one of the oldest museums in the country.

UST has produced many of the Philippines' most important historical figures — including Jos Rizal, who studied medicine here before being expelled for his nationalist activities. The university was also used as an internment camp for Allied civilians during the Japanese occupation of WWII.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

Getting to the University of Santo Tomas

UST is in Sampaloc, Manila — easily reached by LRT or jeepney from most parts of the city.

01

LRT Line 1 — Espaa Station

Take LRT Line 1 to Espaa Station. The UST campus is a short walk east along Espaa Boulevard — the Main Building is visible from the station.

LRT Line 1: Espaa Station  |  ~5 min walk
02

Jeepney along Espaa Boulevard

Jeepneys running along Espaa Boulevard pass the UST main gate. Ask the driver to drop you at "UST" or "Santo Tomas."

Jeepney along Espaa Blvd: ~₱12–₱15  |  Main gate on Espaa
03

Campus Visitor Access

Visitors are welcome on the UST campus. Present a valid ID at the main gate. The Main Building, Arch of the Centuries, and the UST Museum are open to visitors.

Valid ID required  |  Museum: TueSat, 8 AM₱5 PM
04

UST Museum of Arts and Sciences

The UST Museum is one of the oldest museums in the Philippines · housing natural history specimens, religious art, and historical artifacts. A small entrance fee applies.

Museum entrance: ~₱50–₱100  |  Natural history + religious art

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

📌

Campus Highlights

  • Main Building (1927): The magnificent Neo-Baroque main building — the most iconic structure on campus. The facade, the central tower, and the grand entrance hall are all worth seeing.
  • Arch of the Centuries: A 17th-century stone arch built in 1611 — the oldest existing arch in the Philippines. Located near the main entrance of the campus.
  • UST Museum of Arts and Sciences: One of the oldest museums in the Philippines · natural history specimens, religious art, and historical artifacts from the colonial era.
  • The Chapel of the Santsimo Rosario: The university chapel — a beautiful Neo-Baroque church at the heart of the campus.
📌

Historical Significance

  • Jos Rizal: The national hero studied medicine at UST before being expelled for his nationalist activities. His presence is felt throughout the campus.
  • WWII internment: The UST campus was used as an internment camp for Allied civilians during the Japanese occupation (1942₱1945). A memorial marks this history.
  • Dominican heritage: UST has been operated by the Dominican Order for over 400 years — the longest continuous educational institution in the Philippines.
  • Alumni: UST has produced presidents, national artists, scientists, and cultural figures who have shaped Philippine history.
📌

Practical Tips

  • Dress modestly when visiting the campus — it is an active university and a place of worship.
  • Best visited on weekdays when the campus is active and the museum is open.
  • Photography: Photography is permitted on the campus grounds. The Main Building facade and the Arch of the Centuries are the most photogenic spots.
  • Combine with Quiapo: UST and Quiapo Church are in the same area — combine both in a single morning visit.

Historic Manila Academia

9:00 AM

University of Santo Tomas

11:00 AM

Quiapo Church

Afternoon
1:00 PM

Binondo Lunch

Walk to Binondo for lunch — the world's oldest Chinatown is a short walk from Quiapo. Try pancit, hopia, and dim sum.

Cross Jones Bridge to Intramuros — Fort Santiago, San Agustin Church, and Manila Cathedral for the complete historic Manila afternoon.
