Introduction

Batanes' Most Dramatic Landscape

Located in the southern part of Batan Island near the municipality of Ivana, Rakuh-a-Idi — popularly known as Marlboro Hills — is a vast expanse of rolling green hills that tumble dramatically toward the Pacific Ocean. The landscape is unlike anything else in the Philippines · wide open, windswept, and hauntingly beautiful.

The hills are home to wild horses that roam freely across the grasslands, adding to the cinematic quality of the landscape. The name "Marlboro Hills" was coined by early visitors who felt the scenery resembled the rugged American West landscapes used in vintage cigarette advertisements — though the Ivatan name Rakuh-a-Idi carries far deeper cultural significance as a sacred ancestral land.

From the highest point of the hills, the view is staggering — the Pacific Ocean to the east, the South China Sea to the west, the volcanic peak of Mount Iraya rising to the north, and the green hills rolling in every direction. It is one of the most photographed and most emotionally powerful landscapes in the entire Philippines.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Basco to Marlboro Hills

Marlboro Hills is located near Ivana municipality in southern Batan Island — about 12 km south of Basco town. It's a standard stop on any Batan Island tour.

01

Fly to Basco, Batanes

Philippine Airlines and SkyJet Airlines fly from NAIA Manila to Basco Airport. Flight time is about 1.5₱2 hours. Book well in advance — Batanes flights sell out quickly, especially MarchMay.

Flight: ~1.5₱2 hours  |  ~₱3,000₱8,000 round trip
02

Rent a Motorbike or Join a Tour

From Basco, rent a motorbike (₱500–₱700/day) or join a guided Batan Island tour. Marlboro Hills is a standard stop on the South Batan tour route, along with the Honesty Coffee Shop, Valugan Boulder Beach, and Diura Fishing Village.

Motorbike: ~₱500–₱700/day  |  Guided tour: ~₱800–₱1,500
03

Ride South to Ivana

From Basco, ride south along the coastal road through the municipalities of Mahatao and Ivana. The road itself is scenic — stone houses, rolling hills, and ocean views at every turn. Marlboro Hills is signposted from the main road.

Basco to Marlboro Hills: ~30₱40 min  |  Near Ivana municipality
04

Arrive & Explore

Park at the base and walk up the hills. The terrain is grassy and open — no formal trail, just follow the ridgeline upward. Horse riding is available at the base for those who want to explore the hills on horseback — a quintessential Batanes experience.

Horse riding: ~₱200–₱300  |  No entrance fee

Essential Visitor Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Visit

📌

Best Time to Visit

  • March — May: Peak season — lush green hills, clear skies, and calm winds. The landscape is at its most vibrant and photogenic.
  • Early Morning: The hills are bathed in soft golden light and the wild horses are most active. Mist sometimes lingers in the valleys, adding a mystical quality.
  • Late Afternoon: The setting sun casts long shadows across the rolling hills, creating dramatic textures and warm colors.
  • Avoid typhoon season (JuneNovember): Strong winds and rain can make the hills slippery and dangerous. Flights may also be cancelled.
📌

Horse Riding Tips

  • Horse riding is available at the base of the hills. Local handlers will guide you across the grasslands — a truly memorable experience.
  • Wear long pants if you plan to ride — the saddles can be rough on bare skin.
  • Respect the horses. They are working animals — don't startle them or approach them without the handler's guidance.
  • The horses are not tame pets — they roam freely and are rounded up for riding. Treat them with care and respect.
  • Tip your handler generously — horse riding is an important source of income for local families.
📷

Photography & Etiquette

  • The classic shot: A lone horse on the hilltop with the Pacific Ocean behind it. Be patient — the horses move around and the perfect composition will come.
  • Drone photography is spectacular here — the rolling hills and ocean backdrop make for extraordinary aerial footage. Check local regulations.
  • Respect the land. Rakuh-a-Idi is sacred Ivatan ancestral land. Do not litter, damage the grass, or disturb the horses.
  • Stay on the hills. Do not venture to the cliff edges — the drop to the ocean below is sheer and dangerous.
  • Ask before photographing local handlers and horse riders — they appreciate the courtesy.
Visitor Guide

How to Experience Marlboro Hills

A complete guide to Marlboro Hills and the full South Batan Island tour — the best way to experience the landscape and culture of Batanes in one day.

At Marlboro Hills
Step 1

Arrive & Take in the View

Park at the base and walk up the first ridge. The view opens up immediately — rolling green hills, the Pacific Ocean, and Mount Iraya in the distance. Take a moment to absorb the scale and silence of the landscape before exploring further.

Step 2

Ride a Horse Across the Hills

Hire a horse and handler at the base for a ride across the grasslands. The experience of riding across these open hills with the ocean below is one of the most cinematic and memorable things you can do in the Philippines.

Step 3

Reach the Highest Point

Walk or ride to the highest accessible point of the hills. The 360-degree view from the top is extraordinary — ocean on both sides, volcanic peak to the north, and the green hills rolling in every direction. This is the defining view of Batanes.

Step 4

Watch for Wild Horses

Spend time watching the wild horses that roam freely across the hills. They are unpredictable and beautiful — sometimes grazing quietly, sometimes galloping across the ridgeline against the sky. Pure magic.

South Batan Tour
Before

Honesty Coffee Shop (Ivana)

Stop at the famous unmanned Honesty Coffee Shop in Ivana before or after Marlboro Hills. Pay on the honor system, enjoy local snacks and coffee, and experience the extraordinary trust of the Ivatan community.

Nearby

Valugan Boulder Beach

A beach covered entirely in massive volcanic boulders — no sand, just enormous smooth rocks shaped by centuries of Pacific waves. One of the most unusual and dramatic beaches in the Philippines.

Nearby

Diura Fishing Village

A traditional Ivatan fishing village where fishermen still use tataya (traditional outrigger boats). Watch the boats come in with the day's catch and experience authentic Ivatan coastal life.

Evening

Basco Lighthouse at Sunset

End your day at the Basco Lighthouse on Naidi Hills for the golden hour. The white tower against the blazing sky, with the green hills below — the perfect close to a perfect Batanes day.
