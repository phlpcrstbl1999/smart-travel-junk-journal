Introduction

Quirino's Riverside Sanctuary

Located along the Siitan River in Quirino province, Siitan Nature's Park is a natural riverside park that offers a peaceful and beautiful escape into the Sierra Madre wilderness. The Siitan River — a tributary of the Cagayan River system — flows through the park with clear, cool mountain water, creating a series of natural swimming holes, gentle rapids, and scenic river pools surrounded by lush forest.

The park is named after the Siitan River and has been developed as a community-managed ecotourism destination by the local government of Quirino province. The facilities are simple but well-maintained — picnic areas, basic changing rooms, and access to the river. The emphasis is on the natural environment rather than artificial attractions, making Siitan a genuine nature retreat rather than a commercial tourist park.

Siitan Nature's Park is particularly popular for swimming, river picnics, and nature walks along the forested riverbanks. The surrounding forest is part of the Sierra Madre mountain range — one of the most biodiverse ecosystems in the Philippines · and the park offers a rare opportunity to experience this extraordinary natural environment in a relaxed and accessible setting.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

From Manila to Siitan Nature's Park

Siitan Nature's Park is located along the Siitan River in Quirino province — about 300 km north of Manila. The nearest major city is Cabarroguis, the provincial capital.

01

Bus from Manila to Cabarroguis

Autobus Transport and other bus lines operate services from Cubao, Quezon City to Cabarroguis, Quirino. Travel time is about 7₱8 hours. Buses typically depart in the evening for an overnight journey.

Travel time: ~7₱8 hours  |  ~₱500–₱700 one-way
02

Cabarroguis to Siitan

From Cabarroguis, hire a tricycle, van, or habal-habal to Siitan Nature's Park. The park is accessible by road and the journey takes about 20₱40 minutes. Ask locals or your accommodation host for the most current directions.

Tricycle/van: ~20₱40 min  |  ~₱100–₱200
03

Register at the Park Entrance

Register at the park entrance and pay the small entrance fee. The park has basic facilities — picnic areas, changing rooms, and river access. Local guides are available for nature walks along the forested riverbanks.

Entrance fee: ~₱30–₱50  |  Nature walk guide: ~₱100–₱150
04

Combine with Quirino Day Tour

Siitan Nature's Park is the perfect afternoon destination after visiting Aglipay Caves in the morning and Governor Rapids at midday. The peaceful river setting provides a relaxing contrast to the more adventurous earlier stops.

Combine with Aglipay Caves & Governor Rapids  |  Full day recommended

Essential Visitor Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Visit

🌞

Best Time to Visit

  • November — May (Dry Season): Best time — clear river water, accessible trails, and comfortable temperatures for swimming and nature walks.
  • Afternoon visits: Siitan is perfect for a relaxed afternoon after more active morning activities. The river is most beautiful in the late afternoon light.
  • Weekdays: The park is quieter and more peaceful on weekdays. The river feels more like a private sanctuary without weekend crowds.
  • Avoid heavy rain periods: The river can rise quickly after heavy rain. Check conditions before visiting during the rainy season.
📌

Swimming & Activities

  • Swim in the designated areas — the park staff will indicate the safest swimming holes. Avoid areas with strong currents.
  • Nature walks along the forested riverbanks are a highlight — the Sierra Madre forest is rich in birdlife and the trail is beautiful.
  • Picnicking on the riverbank is one of the best ways to enjoy the park — bring your own food and set up beside the river.
  • Photography — the combination of clear water, forest reflections, and dappled light makes Siitan one of the most photogenic river parks in Quirino.
📌

Eco-Tourism Etiquette

  • Leave no trace. Pack out all your trash — the park is community-managed and depends on visitors respecting the environment.
  • Don't use soap or shampoo in the river. The Siitan River is a natural ecosystem — chemical products harm the water quality and aquatic life.
  • Respect the forest. Don't pick plants, disturb wildlife, or leave the marked trails without a guide.
  • Support the community. Buy local food and crafts from vendors near the park entrance — your spending directly supports the families who manage the park.
Visitor Guide

How to Experience Siitan Nature's Park

A complete guide to the park and the perfect Quirino province day tour using Siitan as your relaxing finale.

At the Park
Step 1

Arrive & Settle In

Register at the entrance, find a picnic spot on the riverbank, and take a moment to absorb the atmosphere — the sound of the river, the forest canopy overhead, the cool air. Siitan is a place to slow down and breathe.

Step 2

Swimming in the River Pools

Enter the river at the designated swimming areas. The water is clear and cool — a perfect contrast to the heat of the day. Float in the pools, explore the gentle rapids, and enjoy the sensation of clean mountain water.

Step 3

Nature Walk Along the Riverbank

Take a guided or self-guided walk along the forested riverbank. The Sierra Madre forest is extraordinary — tall trees, ferns, mosses, and the constant sound of birds and running water. Look for kingfishers along the river's edge.

Step 4

Riverside Picnic & Sunset

Set up your picnic on the riverbank and enjoy a meal with the river as your backdrop. If you stay until late afternoon, the light filtering through the forest canopy onto the water creates a magical golden atmosphere.

Full Quirino Day
Morning

Aglipay Caves National Park

Start with cave exploration and forest trekking at Aglipay Caves. Explore the chambers, observe the bat colony, and experience the national park forest.

Midday

Governor Rapids

Head to the Cagayan River for swimming, kayaking, and a riverside picnic at Governor Rapids. The powerful rapids and clear pools are energizing and refreshing.

Afternoon

Siitan Nature's Park

End your Quirino day at Siitan — the most peaceful and relaxing of the three sites. Swim, walk, picnic, and let the river wash away the day's adventures before heading back to Cabarroguis.
