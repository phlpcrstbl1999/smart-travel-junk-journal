Introduction & Overview

Where Manila Watches the Sun Go Down

The Manila Baywalk is the promenade along Roxas Boulevard — the grand coastal boulevard that runs along the western edge of Manila, facing Manila Bay. For over a century, this waterfront has been the gathering place of Manileos at the end of the day, drawn by what is widely considered one of the most beautiful sunsets in Asia.

The baywalk stretches from Rizal Park (Luneta) in the north to the Cultural Center of the Philippines and SM Mall of Asia in the south — a continuous waterfront promenade of several kilometers, lined with restaurants, cafes, and vendors selling street food and fresh coconuts.

The baywalk was dramatically renovated in 2021 with the addition of a white sand beach — a controversial but popular addition that transformed the waterfront into a beach destination in the heart of the city. The combination of the white sand, the promenade, and the legendary sunset makes the Manila Baywalk one of the most visited public spaces in the Philippines.

How to Get There
Location & Getting There

Getting to the Manila Baywalk

The Manila Baywalk runs along Roxas Boulevard — easily reached by LRT, jeepney, or taxi from most parts of Manila.

01

LRT Line 1 — United Nations Station

Take LRT Line 1 to United Nations Station. Walk west along UN Avenue to Roxas Boulevard — the baywalk is a 10-minute walk from the station.

LRT Line 1: UN Station  |  ~10 min walk to Roxas Blvd
02

From Rizal Park

The baywalk begins at the western edge of Rizal Park — walk west from the Rizal Monument to reach the promenade in about 5 minutes.

Walk from Rizal Park: ~5 min  |  Combine both sites
03

Jeepney along Roxas Boulevard

Jeepneys run along Roxas Boulevard from Baclaran in the south to Intramuros in the north — a scenic coastal route with Manila Bay views throughout.

Jeepney along Roxas Blvd: ~₱12–₱15  |  Bay views throughout
04

Best Time: Sunset

The baywalk is most beautiful at sunset — arrive by 5:30 PM to secure a good spot. The sky typically turns its most dramatic colors between 5:45 and 6:15 PM, depending on the season.

Sunset: ~5:45₱6:15 PM  |  Free to access

Essential Travel Tips

Everything You Need to Know Before You Go

📌

The Manila Bay Sunset

  • Best months: The sunset is most dramatic during the dry season (NovemberMay) when the skies are clearer. The colors are most vivid when there is some cloud cover to catch the light.
  • Arrive early: The promenade gets crowded at sunset — arrive by 5:30 PM to find a good spot along the waterfront.
  • Photography: The sunset is one of the most photographed scenes in the Philippines. A wide-angle lens captures the full sweep of the bay and sky.
  • White sand beach: The renovated baywalk includes a white sand beach area — a popular spot for photos and relaxation.
🍽

Food & Atmosphere

  • Street food: Vendors along the promenade sell fresh coconuts, fish balls, kwek-kwek, and other Manila street food classics — perfect for a sunset snack.
  • Baywalk restaurants: Several restaurants along Roxas Boulevard offer bay views — book a window table for the sunset.
  • Fresh buko (coconut): A cold fresh coconut from a baywalk vendor is the quintessential Manila Bay experience.
  • Evening atmosphere: After sunset, the promenade comes alive with families, couples, and groups enjoying the cool bay breeze.
📌

Practical Tips

  • Free to access — the baywalk promenade is a public space with no entrance fee.
  • Combine with Rizal Park: The baywalk begins at the western edge of Rizal Park — visit both in a single afternoon/evening.
  • Cultural Center of the Philippines: The CCP is at the southern end of the baywalk — check for performances during your visit.
  • Safety: The baywalk is generally safe but be aware of your belongings in crowded areas, especially at sunset.
Mini Itinerary

A Manila Bay Afternoon & Evening

Combine the Manila Baywalk with Rizal Park, the National Museum, and the Cultural Center for the perfect Manila Bay day.

Afternoon
2:00 PM

National Museum

Start the afternoon at the National Museum of the Philippines — free admission, the Spoliarium, and the Tree of Life. Allow 2 hours.

4:30 PM

Rizal Park

Walk to Rizal Park and stroll through the gardens. Visit the Rizal Monument and the Chinese Garden before heading to the baywalk.

5:30 PM

Manila Baywalk Sunset

Walk to the Manila Baywalk for the legendary sunset. Find a spot along the promenade, buy a fresh coconut from a vendor, and watch the sky turn gold over Manila Bay.

Evening
7:00 PM

Dinner along Roxas Boulevard

Have dinner at one of the restaurants along Roxas Boulevard with Manila Bay views. The evening atmosphere on the baywalk is one of the most pleasant in Manila.
